# The Scarlet Keys — Campaign Guide

*Arkham Horror: The Card Game*

> *"…by May or June I realised that there was, all over the world, a surprising and unwonted burst of activity on the part of bizarre, furtive, and esoteric mystical organisations ordinarily quiescent and seldom heard from."*
> — H. P. Lovecraft for Hazel Heald, "Out of the Aeons"

The Scarlet Keys is a campaign for *Arkham Horror: The Card Game* for 1–4 players. It contains ten scenarios: "Riddles and Rain," "Dancing Mad," "Dead Heat," "Dealings in the Dark," "Dogs of War," "On Thin Ice," "Sanguine Shadows," "Shades of Suffering," "Without a Trace," and "Congress of the Keys." Each may also be played on its own in Standalone Mode.

**Around the World.** This is an open-ended, non-linear campaign. Following the "Riddles and Rain" prologue, investigators receive a map of the world and choose where to embark. The order of locations determines scenario order and may alter the story and gameplay of each scenario before an epic finale.

**Expansion Icon.** Cards in this expansion are identified by a swirl symbol before each card's collector number.

## Additional Rules and Clarifications

### New Cardtype: Keys

Keys are powerful artifacts that may be "shifted" to either aid or hinder investigators, depending on whom they are attached to and whether their **Stable** or **Unstable** side is faceup.

- During a scenario's resolution, a key may become bound to a single bearer, marked in the Campaign Log. A key may be bound to an investigator, a story asset, or an enemy.
- A key always enters play attached to its bearer (as marked in the Campaign Log). At the start of each scenario, each investigator who bears 1+ keys attaches them to their investigator card. Whenever a story asset or enemy enters play, if it bears 1+ keys, those keys also enter play attached to it.
  - If a key's bearer is an investigator or story asset, it enters play **Stable** side faceup.
  - If a key's bearer is an enemy, it enters play **Unstable** side faceup.
- A key cannot leave play unless its bearer leaves play. If the bearer of 1+ keys leaves play, each key they control is set aside, out of play. (Those keys remain bound to their bearer for future scenarios unless noted otherwise.)
- While a key is attached to an investigator, that investigator may trigger its shift ability during any player window as a fast ability. This is called "shifting" the key. As part of resolution, it flips to its other side, so its other shift ability is active. The investigator must then perform the shift ability on its Unstable side to flip it back over.
  - While a key is attached to a story asset, it may be shifted by any investigator who controls that asset.
  - Each key (by title) attached to an investigator or story asset can only be shifted once per round.
- While a key is under an enemy's control, its shift ability only resolves when a card or game effect instructs an investigator to shift that key, after which it remains Unstable side faceup.
  - A key attached to an enemy cannot be flipped to its Stable side.
  - There is no limit to the number of times a key attached to an enemy can be shifted.
- Some card effects may directly flip a key attached to an investigator or story asset from Stable to Unstable or vice versa. This is **not** the same as shifting a key, and does not resolve its shift ability.

### The Red-Gloved Man

During the campaign, investigators confront members of the secret society known as the Red Coterie. One is the iconic Red-Gloved Man. While playing The Scarlet Keys, investigators **cannot** purchase or include The Red-Gloved Man asset (*The Dunwich Legacy* card #310) in their decks.

## New Keywords

### Concealed X

Concealed enemies represent hidden enemies whose true location is a mystery until investigators work to expose where—and sometimes who—they truly are.

When a scenario includes one or more concealed enemies, investigators set aside several double-sided concealed mini-cards. The facedown side represents the possible location of a concealed enemy; the revealed side shows the mini-card's true nature (an enemy's true location, or a decoy).

When an investigator draws an enemy with the concealed X keyword (or is instructed to resolve an enemy's concealed keyword), they spawn that enemy into a game area above the agenda deck, **not** at any location. This area is "the shadows," representing enemies investigators know exist somewhere on the map but are unsure where. Then, that investigator takes the set-aside concealed mini-card matching that enemy, along with X decoys (X = the enemy's concealed value), shuffles them facedown, and distributes them as evenly as possible among each location in play, starting with the locations nearest to them. Then, at each of those locations that already had 1+ concealed mini-cards, they shuffle each of them facedown as well.

> **Example:** Amina draws Coterie Agent (A), which has "concealed 2." She spawns the Coterie Agent into the shadows, takes the set-aside Coterie Agent (A) mini-card plus 2 decoys, shuffles them facedown, and places one at each of the three locations nearest her. If any of those locations already had 1+ concealed mini-cards, she shuffles each concealed mini-card at those locations as well.

**Enemies in the shadows — additional rules:**

- Enemies in the shadows are considered in play, but not at any particular location. They cannot move until they leave the shadows.
- Enemies in the shadows can qualify as the "nearest" enemy, but only if there are no other enemies in play at any location.
- Enemies in the shadows cannot be damaged or leave play via player card effects.

### Concealed Mini-Cards

While in play, concealed mini-cards represent the possible location of an enemy in the shadows. To deal with such an enemy, its true location must be discovered by exposing its mini-card via **fighting, evading, or investigating**.

- Concealed mini-cards are not enemies and cannot be engaged. However, any investigator at the same location may attempt to expose it by successfully attacking or evading it (as if it were an engaged enemy), or by successfully investigating its location. The difficulty to attack/evade a concealed mini-card equals the shroud value of its location.
  - An investigator may also use a card effect that automatically evades an enemy, deals damage to an enemy, or discovers a clue at a location in order to instead expose a concealed mini-card.
- If an investigator chooses to expose a concealed mini-card, that effect replaces the standard effects of the action/ability that exposed it. (i.e., if an enemy's mini-card is exposed by evasion/damage, the matching enemy is not evaded nor damaged. If exposed by discovering clues/investigating, no clues are discovered.)
- If a concealed mini-card is exposed, flip it to its revealed side.
  - If it is a decoy, set it aside, out of play, with no effect.
  - If it is an enemy's mini-card, that enemy is now exposed. Place the matching enemy in the shadows at that mini-card's location, then set that mini-card aside, out of play. (That enemy is no longer in the shadows, and is now at the location where its mini-card was.) Then, if there are no other enemies in the shadows, set all remaining concealed mini-cards in play aside, out of play.
- Only one concealed mini-card may be exposed per effect unless explicitly stated.

> **Example:** A Coterie Agent and a Coterie Assassin are in the shadows. (1) Kymani plays an event that discovers a clue at their location, but instead exposes the concealed mini-card there—a decoy, set aside. (2) They move to a connecting location and use an evade action to expose another mini-card—the Coterie Assassin's! (3) The mini-card is set aside, and the Coterie Assassin moves to Kymani's location, engaging them. Because another enemy remains in the shadows, no other concealed mini-cards are removed.

### Alert

Each time an investigator fails a skill test while attempting to evade an enemy with the **alert** keyword, after applying all results, that enemy performs an attack against the evading investigator. The enemy does not exhaust after an alert attack. This attack occurs whether or not the enemy is engaged with the evading investigator.

### Patrol

During the enemy phase (framework step 3.2), each ready, unengaged enemy with the **patrol** keyword moves to a connecting location along the shortest path toward the designated location (named in parentheses next to "patrol").

- If multiple locations qualify, the lead investigator chooses.
- If a patrol enemy would be compelled to move via a path blocked by a card ability, the enemy does not move.

## Campaign Setup

1. Choose investigator(s).
2. Each player assembles their investigator deck.
3. Choose difficulty level.
4. Assemble the campaign chaos bag:
   - **Easy** (*I want to wield the Keys*): +1, +1, 0, 0, 0, –1, –1, –1, –2, –2, plus the standard symbol tokens.
   - **Standard** (*I want to be bound in Red*): +1, 0, 0, –1, –1, –1, –2, –2, –3, –4, plus symbol tokens.
   - **Hard** (*I want to face the Outside*): 0, 0, 0, –1, –1, –2, –2, –3, –3, –4, –5, plus symbol tokens.
   - **Expert** (*I want to be Hollowed*): 0, –1, –1, –2, –2, –3, –3, –4, –4, –5, –6, –8, plus symbol tokens.

You are now ready to begin at the Prologue.

## File #5–A (Prologue): Riddles and Rain

**Intro 1 (setup):** Arkham, winter 1924. Vehicles, a streetlamp, pets, then *people* begin to vanish—and not just from the city, but from everyone's memory. Everyone but you. A letter arrives from "Li Flint" enclosing a ticket to the Riverview Theater, asking you to come alone.

- If only one investigator: proceed to **Intro 2**.
- If more than one investigator, the lead investigator chooses:
  - Go alone, as requested → **Intro 3**.
  - Go with backup → **Intro 4**.

**Intro 2 / 3 / 4:** You meet Inspector Li Flint of the International Criminal Police Commission (ICPC) in disguise at the theater. (Intro 2 = solo greeting; Intro 3 = came alone; Intro 4 = brought backup, Flint is displeased but relents.) All proceed to **Intro 5**.

**Intro 5:** Flint shares a file of similar cases worldwide (London, Shanghai, Bruges, Rio, Cairo…) and two photographs: a distorted London street corner and a man in a grey suit and wide-brimmed hat with distinctive **red gloves**. Flint's partner took the photos before vanishing. Flint wants to work together and travel to London. Decide:
  - Take his offer → **Intro 6**.
  - Go to London on your own terms → **Intro 7**.

**Intro 6:** Accept the file, take the next train to Boston.
- *Trust is born of naïveté.* Remove 1 (bless) token, add 1 (curse) token. *(Can be removed later through an act of secrecy.)*
- Read "Tracking Time." Mark **1 time**. Proceed to Setup.

**Intro 7:** Travel to London independently, untailed.
- *Deception breeds uncertainty.* Remove 1 (curse) token, add 1 (bless) token. *(Can be removed later through an act of trust.)*
- Read "Tracking Time." Mark **2 time**. Proceed to Setup.

### Tracking Time

When instructed to mark time, fill that many boxes under "Time Passed." If you fill a box bearing a Greek-letter symbol, go to the matching **Status Report** (page 69) and read it. Time makes events more dangerous; some opportunities are only open during certain windows.

### Setup (Riddles and Rain)

- Gather encounter sets: **Riddles and Rain, Crimson Conspiracy, Dark Veiling, Outsiders, Shadow of a Doubt, Strange Happenings, Chilling Cold, Locked Doors,** and **The Midnight Masks**.
  - From The Midnight Masks, gather only the five treachery cards (2× False Lead, 3× Hunting Shadow). Do not gather its locations/acts/agendas/reference.
- Set **Crimson Conspiracy** and **Outsiders** aside, out of play.
- Put **Rainy London Streets** into play; each investigator begins there. Set each other location aside.
- Set **The Red-Gloved Man** enemy and **The Eye of Ravens** key aside.
- Gather each decoy mini-card and: The Red-Gloved Man and Coterie Agents (A, B, C). Set aside.
- Shuffle the rest to build the encounter deck. Begin.

### Resolutions

**No resolution reached (all resigned/defeated):** Skip to **Resolution 4**.

**Resolution 1:** The red-gloved man vanishes. You find a small ruby marble fitting the eyesocket of a long-dead corpse, with coffin poetry:
> With red we are bound. / Through red we are one. / Thus in red do we bury our kin and our ken. / May you rest, until you are needed once more.
Inspector Flint arrives at gunpoint, held by a woman in a black suit with a silver badge.
- Each investigator earns XP = Victory X of cards in the victory display.
- Choose an investigator to bear **The Eye of Ravens**; update Campaign Log. (From now on, keys are kept handy and begin play attached to their bearer.)
- Record *you haven't seen the last of the Red-Gloved Man.*
- Mark **1 time**. Proceed to Interlude: The Foundation.

**Resolution 2:** The red-gloved man speaks cryptically of "the first shift—the first curse," gestures to the coffin (same poem), gifts you the ruby marble, then vanishes. Same rewards as Resolution 1 (bearer of The Eye of Ravens chosen by you; record *haven't seen the last of the Red-Gloved Man*; mark 1 time → Interlude).

**Resolution 3:** The man is gone; you find the corpse with one rotted, one smooth eyesocket (the marble was taken). Same coffin poem.
- XP as above.
- **The Red-Gloved Man** is the bearer of **The Eye of Ravens**. (If he enters play in future scenarios, the key begins attached to him.)
- Record *haven't seen the last of the Red-Gloved Man.*
- Mark **1 time**. Proceed to Interlude.

**Resolution 4:** You wait for Flint in a tea room; he arrives shaken (another ICPC detective is missing). A woman in black (Commissioner Taylor) interrupts at gunpoint.
- XP as above.
- **The Red-Gloved Man** is the bearer of **The Eye of Ravens**.
- Record *haven't seen the last of the Red-Gloved Man.*
- Mark **2 time**. Proceed to Interlude.

## Interlude: The Foundation

**The Foundation 1:** Commissioner Taylor (with agents Hudson and Antonova) reveals the **Foundation**, an international agency devoted to discovery, research, and containment of objects with **paradimensional capability**. The Red-Gloved Man belongs to the **Red Coterie**, who seek artifacts called **Keys**. Taylor offers to make your group a semi-independent "cell" of the Foundation reporting to her, with travel papers and intel, in exchange for reporting findings. You accept. Decide:
- Tell Taylor about the disappearances → **The Foundation 2**.
- Do not tell Taylor → **The Foundation 3**.

**The Foundation 2:** You tell Taylor. She tasks you with acquiring Keys at all costs.
- Remove 1 (curse) token; add 1 (bless) token (if already 4, each investigator earns 1 XP instead).
- Record *the cell told the truth to Taylor.* → **The Foundation 4**.

**The Foundation 3:** You stay silent.
- Remove 1 (bless) token; add 1 (curse) token (if already 4, each investigator earns 1 XP instead).
- Record *the cell hid the truth from Taylor.* → **The Foundation 4**.

**The Foundation 4:** Flint will head to Shanghai. The Foundation has leads on the Red Coterie:
- **Shanghai** — a secret cabal led by a woman with a red parasol.
- **Alexandria** — beastly killings; a Coterie member involved.
- **Buenos Aires** — high-profile burglaries; likely a Key.
- **Nairobi** — a Coterie sanctum.
- **Anchorage** — a prospector's journal; possible Key site.
- **Istanbul** — a Coterie agent reached out (defector or trap).
- **Kathmandu** — a "spirit" matching a Coterie agent.
- **Marrakesh** — a deadly Coterie agent; tomb robberies and strange overgrowth.
- **Havana** — a Coterie agent smuggling rum/illicit goods.

Read "Embarking and Travel" and "The Foundation Dossiers," then embark from London.

### Embarking and Travel

When you embark, travel anywhere using the campaign map. Each space connects to others by paths. For each path used to reach your destination, mark **1 time** (minimum 1). Each space shows a number-letter combo pointing to the matching page in this guide. You don't have to stop at every space you pass through; stop to read the story there (interlude or scenario).

> **Example:** Shelley travels from Alexandria to Marrakesh: marks 1 time to pass through Rome (doesn't stop), marks 1 more to reach Marrakesh (stops). Marrakesh is "11–B" → File #11–B: Dead Heat.

- **Red-bordered locations** (no letter): only reachable once a Campaign Log entry permits.
- **Green-bordered locations**: possible side stories (from other standalone products). To play one, travel there and spend additional time equal to its normal XP cost (instead of paying the XP cost). If not stopping, count it as one contiguous path.
- Once you stop at a location, you cannot stop there again for the rest of the campaign unless instructed otherwise.

### The Foundation Dossiers

Throughout the guide, **Foundation Dossier** sidebars may be peeked at for any space on the map, at any time, to plan your route. While reading a dossier, you may not peek at any other text on that page until you travel there.

**Map regions / notable spaces:** Arkham, Monte Carlo, Venice, Cairo, Bermuda (20-E), Rio de Janeiro (44-O), Buenos Aires (16-D), San Juan, Reykjavik, London, Quito, Stockholm (36-L), Constantinople/Istanbul (21-F), Marrakesh (11-B), Lagos (37-M), Alexandria (38-N), Nairobi (54-W), Moscow (26-G), Rome (51-T), plus later-unlocked spaces (Shanghai 32-J, Anchorage 33-K, Havana 28-I, Kuala Lumpur/Hong Kong 50-S/46-Q, Manokwari 45-P, Perth 55-X, Tunguska 59-Z, etc.).

## File #11–B: Dead Heat (Marrakesh)

> **Foundation Dossier #11–B** — *Subject Class:* Red · *Real Identity:* Unknown · *Last Known Location:* Marrakesh, Morocco. Subject ("**Amaranth**") is a woman of European descent, early 20s, often wearing a large red flower partly obscuring her face. **Paradimensional Capabilities:** power to transfer life and death—heal wounded entities and sap life from the living via tactile contact; possible near-apocalyptic ramifications. Sightings: Arkham 1923 (healing a Coterie member while withering nearby trees); Lisbon 1924 (one agent killed, one catatonic on contact); unconfirmed in Marrakesh. **Approach:** extremely dangerous; physical contact prohibited. More info in San Francisco (File #26–G2).

**Intro 1:** Arrive in Marrakesh; check time:
- < 15 time → **Intro 2**
- 15–24 time → **Intro 3**
- ≥ 25 time → **Intro 4**

**Intro 2 / 3:** Meet contact **Latif** at a rooftop café. He suspects Amaranth is behind tomb robberies, dying crops, missing persons, "walking dead," and black thorned vines. He believes she's at the Bahia Palace Gardens. Proceed to Setup. (Intro 3 = more time passed; Latif is grimmer, his contact missing.)

**Intro 4:** Arrive to a ruined Marrakesh overtaken by vines; epicenter at Bahia Palace. Skip to **Resolution 5**.

### Setup (Dead Heat)
- Gather: **Dead Heat, Scarlet Sorcery, Spreading Corruption, Ghouls, Striking Fear.**
- Put all 5 Marrakesh locations into play (non-Abandoned versions); set aside the Abandoned versions. Investigators begin at **Marrakesh Railway Station**.
- For each location, place resources = (investigators + 1) as **civilians**. Then check time:
  - ≤ 10 time: no change.
  - 11–17 time: 1 civilian slain (chosen evenly).
  - 18–24 time: 2 civilians slain (chosen evenly).
- Set aside: double-sided **Amaranth** enemy, **Razin Farhi** enemy, **Khalid** enemy, 2× **Ancient Raider**, **The Last Blossom** key, and the double-sided "Save the Civilians / Aftermath" story card.
- Build act/agenda per time:
  - < 15 time: no change.
  - 15–24 time: remove act 1; begin at act 2a; place doom = # players on agenda 1; spawn **Amaranth (Lurking Corruption)** at Bahia Palace Garden with The Last Blossom attached; put Save the Civilians into play; shuffle Khalid into encounter deck.
- Shuffle remaining to build encounter deck. Begin.

**Civilians:** Resources on locations = innocent civilians. Enemies treat civilians as investigators for movement/attacks (attacked civilians are automatically slain). Track slain civilians on the reference card.
**Choosing a Random Location:** shuffle the 5 not-in-play location versions and draw 1.
**Replacing Locations:** swapped locations take the other's place; all tokens/attachments/cards/investigators/enemies are now considered at the new location (nothing is considered to have moved).

### Resolutions (Dead Heat)
**No resolution (all resigned/defeated):** Each remaining civilian is slain. If still Act 1, draw Save the Civilians and resolve the **Aftermath** side. Then: if **Razin Farhi** in play → **Resolution 1**; otherwise → **Resolution 2**.

- **Resolution 1** (lovers reunited): XP = Victory X. **Amaranth** bears **The Last Blossom**. Record *the lovers are reunited.* Mark **1 time**. Embark.
- **Resolution 2:** XP = Victory X. **Amaranth** bears **The Last Blossom**. Record *haven't seen the last of Amaranth.* Mark **1 time**. Embark.
- **Resolution 3** (call her true name "Eryn Cochwyn"): She regains herself, weeping. XP = Victory X. Choose investigator to bear **The Last Blossom**. Record *Amaranth has left the Coterie.* Mark **2 time**. Embark.
- **Resolution 4:** Amaranth flees. XP = Victory X. Choose investigator to bear **The Last Blossom**. Record *haven't seen the last of Amaranth.* Mark **1 time**. Embark.
- **Resolution 5** (Intro 4 path): City devastated. Record *haven't seen the last of Amaranth.* Each investigator suffers 1 mental trauma; earns 3 bonus XP (helping rebuild). **Amaranth** bears **The Last Blossom**. Mark **1 time**. Embark.

## File #16–D: Sanguine Shadows (Buenos Aires)

> **Foundation Dossier #16–D** — *Subject Class:* Yellow · *Real Identity:* Unknown · *Last Known Location:* Buenos Aires. - **Beta (β):** Taylor reveals the Coterie's Tunguska sanctum. Add 1 (curse) token. In Central Asia log, write "**59–Z**" at Tunguska (now travelable). *(Note: traveling there ends the campaign.)*
- **Gamma (γ):** People/objects return "changed." Add 1 (curse) token. Each investigator adds 1 **Paradimensional Understanding** weakness (from **Globetrotting**; doesn't count toward deck size).
- **Epsilon (ε):** You're being followed. For the rest of the campaign, at each scenario setup, gather the Red Coterie enemies whose names match those you "haven't seen the last of," choose one at random, and shuffle it into that scenario's encounter deck.
- **Zeta (ζ):** A Key is stolen. Choose 1 bearer-by-an-investigator Key at random (2 if you control 5+ Keys). Choose at random among Red Coterie enemies matching "haven't seen the last of" entries—that's the thief. Update Keys so the stolen Key(s)' bearer is that enemy. In Central Asia/Americas/North Atlantic logs, write "**14–C**" at Kabul, Quito, San Juan, and Reykjavik (now travelable).
- **Theta (Θ):** A red envelope from Desi: his Ybor City people know the new passphrase—drop by (unlocks #52–U via passphrase).
- **Psi (ψ):** Telegram from Flint: meet in Hong Kong, "parasol is key." In Southeast Asia log, write "**50–S**" at Hong Kong (now travelable).
- **Omega (ω):** You're knocked out. Add 1 (curse) token. You're out of time—travel directly to **Tunguska** and proceed to the Finale.

## Design Notes (summary)

By MJ Newman. The Red Coterie ("Congress of the Keys") originated in *Call of Cthulhu: The Card Game*; here they're reimagined as an Illuminati-esque secret society with no single leader—each member a distinct personality players can reason with independently. The finale's trial scene was one of the first pieces written, designed as a "reckoning" that tallies every choice across the nonlinear campaign. The Foundation was created as a foil and to justify the globetrotting premise; its dossiers, the campaign-log design, and the guide's structure all lean into the secret-government-conspiracy theme. Mechanics like the concealed keyword and the world map followed naturally from themes of secrecy and the campaign's epic scale.
