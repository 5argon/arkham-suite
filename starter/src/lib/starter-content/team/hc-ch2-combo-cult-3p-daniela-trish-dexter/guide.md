### Class Access

In a 3 player team with 3 Core Set investigators, there are 2 possible outcomes :

- They span 4 classes, because they form a chain where each overlaps with the next in the class access. (Chain)
- They span all 5 classes, because only 2 of them overlap in the class access. (Not a chain)

In this team, only Trish and Dexter are adjacent, while Daniela is **not adjacent** to either, and therefore they cover all 5 classes : Guardian, Survivor, Rogue, Seeker, Mystic.

### Overlap Resolutions

Trish and Dexter both play 2 [Out the Door](/card/60370), so in this team Trish starts with 2 [Establish Motive](/card/60268) in their place. Her upgrade plan turns Out the Door into [Out the Door (1)](/card/60373) with her first 2 XP. Upgrade Establish Motive into Out the Door (1) instead, and the overlap is gone as soon as your second scenario.
