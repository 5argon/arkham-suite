### Class Access

In a 4 player team with 4 Core Set investigators, only one is left out of the ring of classes, so they always form a chain and span all 5 classes.

In this team, Daniela, Joe, Trish and Dexter form a **chain**, with Daniela next to Joe, Joe next to Trish and Trish next to Dexter, and therefore cover all 5 classes : Guardian, Survivor, Seeker, Rogue, Mystic.
