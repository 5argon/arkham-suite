### Class Access

In a 4 player team with 4 Core Set investigators, only one is left out of the ring of classes, so they always form a chain and span all 5 classes.

In this team, Daniela, Trish, Dexter and Isabelle form a **chain**, with Daniela next to Isabelle, Trish next to Dexter and Dexter next to Isabelle, and therefore cover all 5 classes : Guardian, Survivor, Rogue, Seeker, Mystic.
