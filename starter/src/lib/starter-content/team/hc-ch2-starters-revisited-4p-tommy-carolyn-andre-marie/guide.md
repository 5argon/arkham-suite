### Overlap Resolutions

The author's original starter decks use both Perception and Unexpected Courage six times, two more than the four copies available of each. Carolyn plays <span class="icon-seeker"></span> Psychoanalysis in place of Perception — still an <span class="icon-intellect"></span> commit, and one she can also play for the horror healing her deck already revolves around. Tommy gives up Unexpected Courage in turn, but Carolyn and Marie already claim three of the four Guts, so only one of his two copies becomes Guts — the other becomes <span class="icon-guardian"></span> Adapt and Overcome, whose <span class="icon-combat"></span> icon and ignored Alert and Retaliate suit a deck that spends its turns shooting.
