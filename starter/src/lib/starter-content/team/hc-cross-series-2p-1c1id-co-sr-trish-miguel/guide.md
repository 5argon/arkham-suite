This team is assembled from decks across multiple of [HungryColquhoun](/starter/hungry-colquhoun)'s starter deck series, where their cards do not overlap. Follow these links to read the play guide written for each deck.

- <span class="icon-rogue"></span> **Trish Scarborough** — [I Spy a Seeker](/starter/hungry-colquhoun/ch2-core-only/trish-scarborough-i-spy-a-seeker) (CO)
- <span class="icon-survivor"></span> **Miguel de la Cruz** — [Disaster Artist](/starter/hungry-colquhoun/ch2-starters-revisited/disaster-artist) (SR)

In the team's name, **1C+1ID** means 1 Core Set investigator and 1 Investigator Deck investigator, and the letters after each name are the deck's series: **CO** is [Chapter 2 Core Only](/starter/hungry-colquhoun/ch2-core-only/) and **SR** is [Ch2 Starters Revisited](/starter/hungry-colquhoun/ch2-starters-revisited/).

### Class Access

In a 2 player team with 1 Core Set investigator, they span 2 classes. The Investigator Deck investigator can fill another class and reach 3 classes in total. If they share the Core Set investigator's second class instead, the team only reaches 2 classes.

In this team, Trish covers Rogue and Seeker. Miguel accordingly filled in Survivor, and nobody on this team can use Guardian or Mystic.
