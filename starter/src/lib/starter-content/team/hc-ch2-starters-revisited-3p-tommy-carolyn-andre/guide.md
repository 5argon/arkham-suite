### Class Access

In a 3 player team with only Investigator Deck investigators, each one uses their own class only, so the team reaches exactly 3 classes.

In this team, Tommy, Carolyn and André cover Guardian, Seeker and Rogue, and nobody on this team can use Mystic or Survivor.

### Overlap Resolution

The author's original starter decks use Perception six times, two more than the four copies available. Carolyn plays <span class="icon-seeker"></span> Psychoanalysis instead — still an <span class="icon-intellect"></span> commit, and one she can also play for the horror healing her deck already revolves around.
