This team is assembled from decks across multiple of [HungryColquhoun](/starter/hungry-colquhoun)'s starter deck series, where their cards do not overlap. Follow these links to read the play guide written for each deck.

- <span class="icon-guardian"></span> **Tommy Muldoon** — [Got Yourself a Gun](/starter/hungry-colquhoun/ch2-starter-guide/got-yourself-a-gun) (SG)
- <span class="icon-seeker"></span> **Carolyn Fern** — [Living her Bast Life](/starter/hungry-colquhoun/ch2-starters-revisited/living-her-bast-life) (SR)
- <span class="icon-survivor"></span> **Miguel de la Cruz** — [Disaster Artist](/starter/hungry-colquhoun/ch2-starters-revisited/disaster-artist) (SR)

In the team's name, **0C+3ID** means 0 Core Set investigators and 3 Investigator Deck investigators, and the letters after each name are the deck's series: **SG** is [Chapter 2 Starter Guide](/starter/hungry-colquhoun/ch2-starter-guide/) and **SR** is [Ch2 Starters Revisited](/starter/hungry-colquhoun/ch2-starters-revisited/).

### Class Access

In a 3 player team with only Investigator Deck investigators, each one uses their own class only, so the team reaches exactly 3 classes.

In this team, Tommy, Carolyn and Miguel cover Guardian, Seeker and Survivor, and nobody on this team can use Rogue or Mystic.
