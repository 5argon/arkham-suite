### Overlap Resolutions

The author's original starter decks use both Perception and Unexpected Courage six times, two more than the four copies available of each. Carolyn plays <span class="icon-seeker"></span> Psychoanalysis in place of Perception — still an <span class="icon-intellect"></span> commit, and one she can also play for the horror healing her deck already revolves around. Miguel plays <span class="icon-survivor"></span> On the Brink instead — one <span class="icon-wild"></span> icon rather than two, but it returns his other committed cards to hand when the test fails, right at home in a deck built to fail on purpose.
