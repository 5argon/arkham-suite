This team is assembled from decks across multiple of [HungryColquhoun](/starter/hungry-colquhoun)'s starter deck series, where their cards do not overlap. Follow these links to read the play guide written for each deck.

- <span class="icon-seeker"></span> **Joe Diamond** — [Shoot First, Questions Later](/starter/hungry-colquhoun/ch2-starter-guide/shoot-first-questions-later) (SG)
- <span class="icon-mystic"></span> **Marie Lambeau** — [Marie Poppin' Cultists](/starter/hungry-colquhoun/ch2-starters-revisited/marie-poppin-cultists) (SR)

In the team's name, **1C+1ID** means 1 Core Set investigator and 1 Investigator Deck investigator, and the letters after each name are the deck's series: **SG** is [Chapter 2 Starter Guide](/starter/hungry-colquhoun/ch2-starter-guide/) and **SR** is [Ch2 Starters Revisited](/starter/hungry-colquhoun/ch2-starters-revisited/).

### Class Access

In a 2 player team with 1 Core Set investigator, they span 2 classes. The Investigator Deck investigator can fill another class and reach 3 classes in total. If they share the Core Set investigator's second class instead, the team only reaches 2 classes.

In this team, Joe covers Seeker and Guardian. Marie accordingly filled in Mystic, and nobody on this team can use Rogue or Survivor.
