This team is assembled from decks within [HungryColquhoun](/starter/hungry-colquhoun)'s [**Chapter 2 Core Only** starter decks series](/starter/hungry-colquhoun/ch2-core-only/), with any overlaps resolved for your convenience by arkham-starter.com's editor. Follow these links to read the play guide written for each deck.

- <span class="icon-rogue"></span> **Trish Scarborough** — [I Spy a Seeker](/starter/hungry-colquhoun/ch2-core-only/trish-scarborough-i-spy-a-seeker)
- <span class="icon-mystic"></span> **Dexter Drake** — [The Prestige](/starter/hungry-colquhoun/ch2-core-only/dexter-drake-the-prestige)

**Note :** The [Chapter 2 Core Only starter decks series](/starter/hungry-colquhoun/ch2-core-only/) has high overlaps between Joe-Trish (both wants max copies of Dorothy, Magnifying Glass, Deduction, Working a Hunch, etc.), and between Dexter-Isabelle (both wants max copies of Cosmic Flame, Second Sight, Jim, Soul Link, etc.). That means:

- From 10 possible 2P teams : 8 remains that I could pre-built for you.
- From 10 possible 3P teams : 4 remains. (There are 3 teams each with Joe-Trish or Dexter-Isabelle, totalling to 6 teams I could not.)
- From 5 possible 4P teams : None remains, and I do not want to heavily modify HC's deck just to make 4P teams, it'd not be their decks anymore.
