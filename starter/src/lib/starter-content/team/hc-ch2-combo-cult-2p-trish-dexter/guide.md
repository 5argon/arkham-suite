### Class Access

In a 2 player team with 2 Core Set investigators, there are 2 possible outcomes :

- They span 3 classes, because they overlap in the class access. (Adjacent)
- They span 4 classes, because they didn't overlap in the class access. (Not adjacent)

With only 2 players, some classes always stay out of reach : 2 classes in the first case, and 1 class in the 2nd case.

In this team, Trish and Dexter are **adjacent**, both able to use Rogue, and therefore cover 3 classes : Rogue, Seeker, Mystic, and nobody on this team can use Guardian or Survivor.

### Overlap Resolutions

Trish and Dexter both play 2 [Out the Door](/card/60370), so in this team Trish starts with 2 [Establish Motive](/card/60268) in their place. Her upgrade plan turns Out the Door into [Out the Door (1)](/card/60373) with her first 2 XP. Upgrade Establish Motive into Out the Door (1) instead, and the overlap is gone as soon as your second scenario.
