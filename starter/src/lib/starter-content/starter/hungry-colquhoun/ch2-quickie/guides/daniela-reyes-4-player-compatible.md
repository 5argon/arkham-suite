<strong><ins>Role</ins>:</strong> Damage dealer.

<strong><ins>Concepts and Combos</ins>:</strong> You have ample strong weapons to fight enemies with, likely saving any [Winchester Model 12](/card/12029) you find for the big bad guy). There's also 27 <span class="icon-combat"></span> icons here to help with killing, plus [Counterattack](/card/12026). Clues are [Lesson Learned](/card/12022), ["Look what I found!"](/card/12078) and [Scene of the Crime](/card/12024), with these events being recurable with [Hunter's Instinct](/card/12074). There's barely any card draw, [Right Tool for the Job](/card/12023) will help find weapons and you can also draw a card with an enemy around and punish their attack of opportunity with Daniela's <span class="icon-reaction"></span> ability.

<strong><ins>Drawbacks</ins></strong><strong>:</strong> While [Levelheaded](/card/12076) can help with <span class="icon-willpower"></span> treacheries, sticking close to Izzie for <span class="icon-wild"></span> commits from her and healing horror using [Lucky Charm](/card/12061) with Dexter are recommended. [Resilience](/card/12021) will help with soak should you need it. Resource economy is quite poor if you want to be playing events (though remember [Hunter's Instinct](/card/12074) can recur [Emergency Cache](/card/12089)) so you may need to take resource actions.

<h2 style="color:rgb(92, 180, 253);"><strong>Campaign starter and planned progression</strong></h2>

For the starter deck, please remove any XP bearing cards and instead use the cards listed in the side deck. A recommended order of purchases/upgrades is:

- <strong>[Jumpsuit](/card/12075) → [Sledgehammer](/card/08094)</strong>

- <strong>[Broken Bottle](/card/12086) → [Counterattack](/card/12026)</strong>

- <strong>[Machete](/card/12020) → [Winchester Model 12](/card/12029)</strong>
