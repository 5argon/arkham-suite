<strong><ins>Role</ins>:</strong> Jack of all trades, horror cleric, occasional evader.

<strong><ins>Concepts and Combos</ins>:</strong> With [Jim Culver](/card/12060) you can clue-find at a 6 with [Second Sight](/card/12062) or fight at an 8 with upgraded [Cosmic Flame](/card/12071). [Hand-Crank Flashlight](/card/12088) and [Thieves' Kit](/card/12049) provide back-up clue-finding options while your spells come online, as does [Will of the Cosmos](/card/12066). [Lucky Charm](/card/12061) allows you to move horror off other investigators, which [Fearless](/card/12069) can then heal. If you haven't found Cosmic Flame evading is a possibility with this deck, with there being 9 <span class="icon-agility"></span> and <span class="icon-wild"></span> icons respectively - as well as [Premonition](/card/12064) to help make your next test a guarantee.

<strong><ins>Drawbacks</ins></strong><strong>:</strong> [Lucky Cigarette Case](/card/12044) was gobbled up by Trish's deck, so card draw isn't as strong and [Jim Culver](/card/12060) is your main option for this. The deck is resource poor on the whole, [Paint the Town Red](/card/12051) should only typically be used in scenario 2 where it can draw a 4 health Elusive enemy (otherwise basic resource actions are better).
