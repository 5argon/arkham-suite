<strong><ins>Role</ins>:</strong> Clue-finder ("cluever"), evader.

<strong><ins>Concepts and Combos</ins>:</strong> Draw cards with [Lucky Cigarette Case](/card/12044), move around with [Olivier Bishop](/card/12046), find clues with [Magnifying Glasses](/card/12034) plus [Dorothy Simmons](/card/12030), and occasionally shoot things with [M1903 Hammerless](/card/12045) (which you can install with [Prestidigitation](/card/12052) fast if you need to). The only change to my original Trish deck is swapping [Unexpected Courage](/card/12094) for [Guts](/card/12090), <ins><strong><a href="https://arkhamdb.com/decklist/view/60848/trish-scarborough-i-spy-a-seeker-chapter-2-deck-guide-1.0">please see a more detailed version of this guide by clicking what you're reading right now!</a></strong></ins>

<strong><ins>Drawbacks</ins></strong><strong>:</strong> [Black Chamber Operative](/card/12009) is always a pain, while there's a ways to counter play him here having someone else kill him is advisable. This deck doesn't have a load of sanity soak and <span class="icon-willpower"></span> icons for relevant treacheries, Dexter can however heal horror off of you to a good degree.
