<strong><ins>Role</ins>:</strong> Jack of all trades.

<strong><ins>Concepts and Combos</ins>:</strong> [Meat Cleaver](/card/12077) with [Aleksey Saburov](/card/12072) and [Cloak of Resonance](/card/12058) allow you to deal good damage (putting the horror on your soak assets) and heal horror off yourself from Izzie's <span class="icon-fast"></span> ability. [Old Compass](/card/12083) or [Hand-Crank Flashlight](/card/12088) is your clue finding, bolstered by 21 <span class="icon-wild"></span> symbols, [Perception](/card/12093) and [Scrape By](/card/12082).

<strong><ins>Drawbacks</ins></strong><strong>:</strong> This deck is resource economy poor, expect to take some resource actions to get going in most cases.
