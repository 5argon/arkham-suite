<h2 style="color:rgb(72, 177, 79);"><strong>Overview</strong></h2>

- <strong><ins>Taking slapstick literally</strong></ins>: With his [Polished Cane](/card/60363), André slaps people with a stick at an 8 with [Olivier](/card/12046). This will often trigger an oversuccess, giving you both an extra action and automatically evading the enemy. Both [Hammerless](/card/12045) and [A Sudden Fall](/card/60378) then deal extra damage to exhausted foes. [The Grapevine](/card/60374) allows you to get straight to bad guys (even Aloof ones) and engage them while providing card draw. Of course you can also evade enemies with your great <span class="icon-agility"></span> stat too.

- <ins><strong>Cash for clues</a></strong></ins>: When you're not blugeoning enemies with walking implements, you're getting cash and finding clues. [Out the Door](/card/60373) and ["Watch this!"](/card/60371) throw resources at you, while [Thieves' Kit](/card/12049) provides money from investigating. [Prestidigitation](/card/12052) allows you to flip between clue-finding and enemy management with ease. When you're not investigating with a Kit (e.g. you have your weapons out), resources convert to clues with [Right Under Their Noses](/card/60368), [Pay Your Dues](/card/60365) and [Silver Tongue](/card/60380). André's extra action coupled to [Olivier's](/card/12046) free move gives great versatility, often enabling up to 4 clues in a turn.

- <strong><ins>There's no people like show people</ins>:</strong> As André has a slightly padded deck with 3 signature skills, card draw is important. [Lucky Cigarette Case](/card/12044) gives cards for oversuccess, while upgraded [Grapevine](/card/60374) pairs card draw with efficient enemy management. [Manual Dexterity](/card/12091) and a [Perception](/card/12093) round this off, replacing themselves while boosting tests. In terms of the encounter deck, André sports a respectable number of <span class="icon-willpower"></span> and <span class="icon-wild"></span> icons here in addition to [Know the Line](/card/60353). While the Film Star only has [Olivier Bishop](/card/12046) as soak, his basic health and sanity are good.

<h2 style="color:rgb(72, 177, 79);"><strong>Playtesting notes</strong></h2>

I made a couple of small tweaks on play testing. Firstly I swapped the order of [Another Day, Another Dollar](/card/12056) and [upgraded Right Under Their Noses](/card/60382) (so the former is bought inside your first 19 XP). This plays a little better with [upgraded Silver Tongue](/card/60380), which itself works very well with [Thieves' Kit](/card/12049). Surprisingly, André felt like my main clue-finder while playing - in addition to his great enemy management. The other small change was removing one copy of [Perception](/card/12093) rather than [Unexpected Courage](/card/12094) from the final deck. Given you do <span class="icon-combat"></span> tests with [Polished Cane](/card/60363), the <span class="icon-wild"></span><span class="icon-wild"></span> icons are handy over the slightly more situational <span class="icon-intellect"></span><span class="icon-intellect"></span> icons.

Playing on Hard two-player through Children of Blood, I thought the deck worked great. I used the 5 bonus XP from Hard difficulty for [upgraded Silver Tongue](/card/60380) and a copy of [upgraded The Grapevine](/card/60374). While the latter didn't shine too much in the campaign, drawing a card for something you were going to do anyway is a nice effect. I found [Polished Cane](/card/60363) in particular to be excellent - very easily triggering your extra action by testing at an 8 with [Olivier](/card/12046). As mentioned in my review of Polished Cane, it also allows you to pin Elusive enemies to a location if you get the over-success on your first action. [Prestidigitation](/card/12052) was extremely useful in shuffling hand assets around on the fly. Overall this build felt like the quintessential Rogue, which is exactly what I was going for!

<h2 style="color:rgb(72, 177, 79);"><strong>Final thoughts</strong></h2>

This deck allows you to have your cake and eat it. Enemy management (both fighting and evading) leverage André's high <span class="icon-agility"></span> and his easy money feeds into clue-finding. People often enjoy [Trish](/card/12007) because her Seeker splash gives clues, however André's additional action does this and more. [The Grapevine](/card/60374) is an added bonus, giving you card draw with absurd mobility, and convenient means to engage Aloof enemies like [Whippoorwills](/card/12166), [Zealots](/card/12188) and most Civilians. Hope you enjoy!
