<h2 style="color:rgb(92, 180, 253);"><strong>Overview</strong></h2>

- <strong><ins>Combos</ins>:</strong> With either [Rope Trap](/card/60578) or [Lie in Wait](/card/60566) at a neighbouring location to a boss, Daniela can drag non-Massive enemies to these locations - triggering her <span class="icon-reaction"></span> ability. Paired to a [Service Revolver](/card/60158), this gives a further <span class="icon-reaction"></span> attack (with another Revolver <span class="icon-reaction"></span> if attacked in the enemy phase, for 6 attacks total with Lie in Wait). [Upgraded Bodyguard](/card/12027) soaks these attacks of opportunity (AoOs), giving more testless damage and pairing especially well to Rope Trap. You can also take an AoO from playing a new Bodyguard over an existing Bodyguard (e.g. initiate play action for a new Bodyguard, AoO triggers first to kill existing Bodyguard freeing ally slot, your <span class="icon-reaction"></span> triggers, and then you can kill your second Bodyguard in the enemy phase for more auto-damage). For Massive enemies, as long these are Hunters you can still move away as your last action and have them chase you into a trap.

- <strong><ins>Complimentary Cards</ins>:</strong> You need a weapon, and so [Meat Cleaver](/card/12077), [Machete](/card/12020) and [Daniela's Wrench](/card/12002) give plenty of options (so many that you don't need [Right Tool for the Job](/card/12023)). Meat Cleaver and Daniela's Wrench in particular give you more avenues to damage your [Bodyguard](/card/12027). [Counterattack](/card/12026) is here for those instances where you don't want to take AoO damage or you want to cancel an attack aimed at a different investigator. [Lesson Learned](/card/12022) gives a clue for taking damage, which you'll be doing a lot!

- <strong><ins>Staples</ins>:</strong> [Bounty](/card/60161), [Emergency Cache](/card/12089) and [Logan Hastings](/card/12018) are all resource economy, with Logan providing a +1 <span class="icon-combat"></span> boost as well. [Stalk Prey](/card/60567) and [Overpower](/card/12092) provide card draw, with the former also providing a clue. For card draw otherwise, consider drawing cards during downtime (it's not like Daniela will be investigating high shroud locations!). [Scrape By](/card/12082) is in the deck because it's undeniably awesome for passing all sorts of tests (including basic investigates), while [upgraded Vicious Blow](/card/60176) pumps max damage per turn.

<h2 style="color:rgb(92, 180, 253);"><strong>Playtesting Notes</strong></h2>

I played this deck through two standalones: the Curse of the Rougarou, and Traces to Nowhere.

<ins><strong>The Curse of the Rougarou:</strong></ins> It's been about 4 years since I played Curse so I thought I'd dust it off, paying 2 player and on standard - though I think hard would have been fine.

Daniela's support cards worked well, in particular she used [Stalk Prey](/card/60567) twice for two clues - and [Scrape By](/card/12082) allowed her to pass the <span class="icon-intellect"></span> test needed for the [Bear Trap](/card/81020).

We ended up killing the [The Rougarou](/card/81028). I got two damage on it early with [Meat Cleaver](/card/12077). I then did it one damage with Cleaver (to avoid triggering it running away), and then dragged it to a location. This triggered Daniela's <span class="icon-reaction"></span>, so I attacked with Meat Cleaver again and committed [upgraded Vicious Blow](/card/60169) for 3 damage (I had a lot of horror so didn't couldn't get the Meat Cleaver +1) and then I used the [Service Revolver](/card/60158) <span class="icon-reaction"></span> attack and another Vicious Blow for 4 damage.

While you could say it's lucky that I got both [Vicious Blow](/card/60169) - if it hadn't had been Vicious Blow I would have likely drawn [Bodyguard](/card/12027), [Rope Trap](/card/60578) or [Lie in Wait](/card/60566) instead. For me this deck feels _lethal_ - there's so many different avenues to damage that you can always find a way to kill something!

<ins><strong>Traces to Nowhere</strong></ins>: We played two player on standard. It can be a tough scenario so two player on standard is still a difficult experience. I'll aim to keep spoilers minimal! Play time was 2 and a half hours (there's randomness in the scenario, and we got some of the worst possible randomness). However it does mean the deck was more thoroughly playtested!

Traces to Nowhere is a bit of a poor scenario for trappers, so traps didn't see much use. There's no Elusive enemies, and you'll usually want to kill the Hunters. There is a location you return to a lot, so putting a trap on a connecting location might have worked well - but it's only clearer in hindsight (and even then it might be a waste of time). There is a recurring enemy, but it's hard to know when and where they will show up, so again it's hard to put traps down in anticipation.

All that said, Daniela is still great at killing things. At one point an Aloof 4 health enemy showed up at a connecting location. Daniela moved, engaged, attacked with [Meat Cleaver](/card/12077) for 2 damage, and then attacked in the enemy phase to kill them. There's few investigators that can put such a hard stop to nuisance enemies than Daniela. Later a 5 health enemy showed up (not Aloof), and she did the same thing again (move, attack, attack, finish off in enemy phase).

[Meat Cleaver](/card/12077) was a mixed blessing. On the one hand it was good to dispatch 2 health enemies to heal horror by taking two fight actions (there's a lot of 2 health enemies in Traces). On the other hand as we were playing for 2.5 hours, eventually the horror does mount up and stop you using the two damage effect. I suspect this is RNG (and could have been eased with Daniela getting a second weapon out, which she didn't manage) but even so it's noticeable. The boss in Traces can also be extremely healthy, which doesn't help matters. Still, likely without it Daniela may well have been defeated due to horror, so I can't complain too much!

[Bodyguard](/card/12027) did make a showing, and was useful. However in Traces you don't want allies to die in the first half of the scenario, so this also made him a bit of a bad pairing to the scenario itself. Something that did happen is Daniela became absurdly rich (with [Logan](/card/12018) coming out turn 1 and [Bounty](/card/60161) making an appearance), and you can spend resources in Traces for <span class="icon-free"></span> travel on some locations so that came in handy. [Upgraded Vicious Blow](/card/60169) did allow for nice action compression a couple of times, turning 2 action kills on 3 health enemies into 1 action kills.

I think the conclusion here is, even in a scenario that plays against this deck's strengths (hard to anticipate enemy movement/spawning, a need to keep disposable allies alive) Daniela was still a murder machine! This was paired to the more cluever orientated Izzie deck of this series (proxying [Scrape By](/card/12082)) and, as there's a lot of enemies in Traces, Daniela was definitely needed to enable Izzie to do her thing - which is what a good Guardian should do. I think this deck faired much better in Curse of the Rougarou, where doing a big burst of damage killed the boss fairly early (and where we got [Service Revolver](/card/60158) out to support that). Traces to Nowhere was much more winning by attrition!

<h2 style="color:rgb(92, 180, 253);"><strong>Final thoughts</strong></h2>

The sexy premise here is even with the starting deck you can achieve 6 two damage attacks in a round with level 0 cards: [Lie in Wait](/card/60566), [Service Revolver](/card/60158) and [Meat Cleaver](/card/12077) or [Daniela's Wrench](/card/12002). You do this through two fight actions, dragging the enemy to the trapped location (triggering the trap and both the Revolver's and Daniela's <span class="icon-reaction"></span> abilities), and then triggering the Revolver once more in the enemy phase when you're attacked. If you do have the Wrench out, you can use its <span class="icon-fast"></span> ability to squeeze in a 7th attack with Revolver (probably not that advisable though!). Ordinary you only get 5 attacks (3 in your turn, and then Revolver and Daniela <span class="icon-reaction"></span> attacks in the enemy phase), so this offers a clear and definable advantage. Add on [Bodyguard](/card/12027) and [Vicious Blow](/card/60169), and this gets even better.

Overall this deck plays a little different to other Daniela decks, but in a very cool way. I also think this is one of my most eldritch photoshops to date (I don't understand why its neck is so long. Sometimes I swear I turn away from the screen, and when I look back its neck is a little longer...). Hope you enjoy!
