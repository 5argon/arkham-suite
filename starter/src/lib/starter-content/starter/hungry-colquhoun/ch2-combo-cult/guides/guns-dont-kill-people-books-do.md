<h2 style="color:rgb(239, 163, 69);"><strong>Overview</strong></h2>

- <strong><ins>Combos</ins>:</strong> The core combo here is [Scroll of the Pharaohs (Words of Ra)](/card/60280) with [Autopsy Report](/card/60276). This allows you to lean into <span class="icon-intellect"></span> (running [Dorothy Simmons](/card/12030)) and test at an effective 8 for intellect fighting, while receiving a big boost on your Autopsy Report free investigate. Hence (everyone likes a good hence...), this opens up the situations where you can trigger Joe's <span class="icon-reaction"></span> ability - as if there's no enemies around you will investigate as normal, but if there is you can still often kill the enemy to investigate anyway (and you can even have another investigator kill an enemy to trigger an investigate outside of your turn). [Caustic Reaction](/card/60273) provides a further avenue for <span class="icon-intellect"></span> fighting. If you really <ins>hate</ins> Autopsy Report, I've also included a deck variant below without it.

- <strong><ins>Complimentary Cards</ins>:</strong> [Scroll of the Pharaohs (Words of Ra)](/card/60280) necessitates horror healing and soak. [Detective's Intuition](/card/12005), [Psychoanalysis](/card/60264) and [Stakeout](/card/60166) provide the former, while [Resilience](/card/12021) provides the latter - for an extra 11 effective additional soak/healing (and useful icons otherwise). This works well considering your Scroll isn't the only way you deal damage (and you can spend two secrets per activation for 3 damage, in which you may only take two horror per tome). Resilience is chosen specifically because it's also health soak, reducing the need to sacrifice [Dorothy](/card/12030) to damage. [Literary Analysis](/card/60269) helps keep Scroll charged, while [Vicious Blow](/card/12025) pairs to [Insidious Truths](/card/60266) to give high damage and test value <span class="icon-combat"></span> options to conserve your Scroll's secrets.

- <strong><ins>Staples</ins>:</strong> [Deduction](/card/12039) and [Working a Hunch](/card/12038) keep the clues flowing nicely. While Joe is no [Carolyn](/card/60251) at clue-finding, his card advantage means you usually have the boosts you want when you need them. As this deck kills enemies, [Bounty](/card/60161) gives resources for the privilege - providing above average resource economy alongside [Dorothy Simmons](/card/12030), Joe's <span class="icon-elder_sign"></span> ability, [Detective's Intuition](/card/12005) and [Emergency Cache](/card/12089). I tend to find Joe has plenty of resources, even taking [Dead Ends](/card/12006) into account. [Machete](/card/12020) is a backup weapon for when your Scroll refuses to come out of your deck, as well as a <span class="icon-combat"></span> icon and a means to ditch empty [Scroll of the Pharaohs](/card/60280) (as it's unique and so can't replace itself).

<h2 style="color:rgb(239, 163, 69);"><strong>Playtesting Notes</strong></h2>

Unlike the other decks in this series which were playtested on Traces to Nowhere, I playtested this instead in Children of Blood (Hard, two player) due to it using a 'Researched' asset - though the rules police won't lock you up if you do play this on a standalone. I did also playtest the deck variant below through BoA on Hard two player, I've made some notes in that section...

I played CoB with the 5 bonus XP, buying two copies of [upgraded Caustic Reaction](/card/60273), with 1 XP spare. For the first scenario of CoB (playing with Miguel from my previous series), we did manage to research [Scroll of the Pharaohs](/card/60259) but it came at the cost of losing the scenario through damage. Still you get 3 bonus XP for losing, finishing with 8 XP (including the 1 XP I had left over) allowing me to upgrade both tomes.

CoB Scenario 2 was dramatically different, as with both copies of [Scroll of the Pharaohs (Words of Ra)](/card/60280) then Joe's enemy management role was in full swing. Joe found clues well using [Deduction](/card/12039), and fried the big boss with both his Scroll and [Caustic Reaction](/card/60273). [Resilience](/card/12021) was handy in keeping Joe alive too.

Joe was great in CoB Scenario 3, getting two clues off a 4 shroud location with [Deduction](/card/60267) plus [Stakeout](/card/60166), snagging an extra clue elsewhere with [Working a Hunch](/card/12038), and then destroying the final boss with [Insidious Truths](/card/60266) plus [Vicious Blow](/card/12025) followed by [Scroll of the Pharaohs](/card/60280) for 7 damage (the boss having taken 3 damage the previous round). The very good thing about Joe is his card draw is so strong that his fight assets and events tend to readily present themselves - he's the [Mark Harrigan](/card/03001) of high Intellect!

<h2 style="color:rgb(239, 163, 69);"><strong>Deck Variant</strong></h2>

For those of you who don't like [Autopsy Report](/card/60276) (y'know - all you theme-hating philistines!) then have I got a variant for you. The deck uses [upgraded Magnifying Glass](/card/60270) rather than Report, allowing you to crack high shroud locations a little easier on basic investigates (but losing out on Report's card draw from Joe's ability). Upgraded Mag Glass also makes hand slot juggling a little easier too (e.g. need a slot for [Machete](/card/12020)? Then pull Mag Glass back. Likewise, Machetes and empty [Scroll of the Pharaohs](/card/60280) can be bumped out with Mag Glass as needed - especially as Scroll is unique and so can't bump itself out).

As mentioned above I did playtest this variant through Hard two player BoA - which went well. I got [Scroll of the Pharaohs](/card/60259) researched and subsequently upgraded by scenario 2 (getting the max 8 XP out of the first scenario). I made short work out of [Servant of Flame](/card/12114) in the first scenario with [Caustic Reaction](/card/60261), [Insidious Truths](/card/60266) (with [Vicious Blow](/card/12025) committed) plus the damage you can deal with clues in that scenario (with two more damage coming from Marie - using the [Marie Poppin' Cultists](https://arkhamdb.com/decklist/view/64502/marie-poppin-cultists-ch2-starters-revisited-1.0) deck).

With [Scroll of the Pharaohs](/card/60280) upgraded it was plain sailing in scenario 2. I killed [Margaret Liu](/card/12144) with it in the final round of the scenario - drew her, engaged her, and then dealt 3 damage in a single attack - as well as getting big hits on the Elusive [Servant of Flame](/card/12138) for a kill assist.

In the final BoA scenario I had a bad mulligan (no assets!) and as there's not many clues around I couldn't get card draw that way either (I of course went to unrevealed sewer locations with no clues). However I still did manage to do a 4 damage attack on [Elokoss](/card/12179) with [Insidious Truths](/card/60266) and [Vicious Blow](/card/12025), so the deck still managed to get a crucial big hit in that helped secure the win (and I got [Machete](/card/12020) out before that which was useful for dispatching trash mob cultists).

Is the better version of the deck? <sub>...maybe.</sub> It can deal more damage by the 19XP mark due to upgrading [Vicious Blow](/card/60176) - though at least in BoA I didn't get the XP to upgrade these (I could either kill [Margaret](/card/12144) or get rid of [Dead Ends](/card/12006) in scenario 2, and chose the former) and then I didn't see either of my upgraded [Caustic Reactions](/card/60273) or [Magnifying Glasses](/card/60270) in the final scenario. I will say as you're upgrading into the damaging stuff earlier then, if you do get saddled unluckily with a Dead Ends you can't shift, you're likely to have upgraded into more damage-dealing cards sooner than the main deck. However, I'm a sucker for theme - so I like the first deck more!

<h2 style="color:rgb(239, 163, 69);"><strong>Final thoughts</strong></h2>

This essentially turns Joe full Guardian, and I love it! With this setup Joe can deal 11 damage in a turn while testing at an 8 or more (two [Insidious Truths](/card/60266) with [Vicious Blow](/card/60169), one [Scroll of the Pharaohs](/card/60280)), making him a very capable boss-killer. What sets this deck apart is its <span class="icon-intellect"></span> focus, so when you're not killing things then investigation is often trivial (especially as this deck is brimming with cards to commit). This gives you a more singular focus where you make fewer compromises (e.g. compared to say a 50-50 <span class="icon-intellect"></span> and <span class="icon-combat"></span> deck which usually needs [Charisma](/card/12095) and [Logan](/card/12018), and leaves much less deck space for pushing hard on Intellect icons while often requiring more setup).

I really liked this deck, it's probably my favorite Joe deck out of the several I've designed, and hopefully a good capper to this combo driven series. Regarding the title of the deck I know there is the [M1911](/card/12019) in the campaign starter, but I thought the current title was an improvement over, "Guns kill people for a little while, but then they're swapped out for books, which kill people better!" Enjoy...
