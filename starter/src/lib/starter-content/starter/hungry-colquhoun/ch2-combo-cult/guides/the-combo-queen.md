> In this mini-series I'm delving deeper in the Ch2 card pool for interesting combos. Why have one combo, when you can have four?! Here Izzie uses [Old Compass](/card/12083) and [Rabbit's Foot](/card/60560) as fail tech, getting clues off 4 shroud locations with ["Look what I found!"](/card/12078). She gets testless clues with [Will of the Cosmos](/card/12066), and places the doom on self-discarding [Hunter's Instinct](/card/12074) or [Same Old Thing](/card/60561). She heals horror and damage with [Consume Life](/card/60462) kills paired to [Cosmic Guidance](/card/60468) and [Fearless](/card/12069). Lastly, through double-dipping [Do-or-Die](/card/60568) with her ability then Izzie trivially recurs the ever-powerful [Scrape By](/card/12082). How I got this all into one deck I'll never know!

<h2 style="color:rgb(238, 74, 83);"><strong>Overview</strong></h2>

- <strong><ins>(Clue) Combos</ins>:</strong> Firstly there's <ins>_Fail Tech_</ins>, as [upgraded Old Compass](/card/12083) allows you to get 2 clues off 4 shroud locations with ["Look what I found!"](/card/12078) and its -2 shroud <span class="icon-reaction"></span> redo. [Rabbit's Foot](/card/60560) draws a card for the privilege, and if you're certain you're going to fail a test (e.g. testing on 5 shroud before the redo) then [On the Brink](/card/12084) is both a cantrip or a <span class="icon-wild"></span><span class="icon-wild"></span> boost when needed. Additionally there's <ins>_Testless Clues_</ins> with the superb [Will of the Cosmos](/card/12066), which puts doom on self-discarding assets like [Same Old Thing](/card/60561) or [Hunter's Instinct](/card/12074), with the latter synergistically recurring Cosmos. Finally there's <ins>_Passing Impossible Investigates_</ins> with [Scrape By](/card/12082) and [Do-or-Die](/card/60568). With Izzie's ability you can double dip on Do-or-Die, giving an extremely trivial mechanism to recur Scrape By (and your self-discarding <span class="icon-survivor"></span> assets). Late in a scenario, if you have 10 or so cards, you'll see Do-or-Die over and over again and so recur Scrape By all the time (you can use On the Brink from your hand/discard pile as a second commit to better pass the Do-or-Die test). Of course you can use Scrape By to pass _any_ test, but getting a clue more or less guaranteed is a good use-case!

- <strong><ins>Complimentary Cards</ins>:</strong> Izzie thrives with some healing, both to fuel her ability and offset [Breaking Point](/card/12015). For damage there's [Consume Life](/card/60462) with [Cosmic Guidance](/card/60468), allowing for a two-damage heal in one action if you have both and kill an enemy - but also they standalone as there's plenty of enemies to kill or <span class="icon-willpower"></span> tests on scenario cards. While Consume Life is expensive, [Same Old Thing](/card/60561) funds its full cost - and it's nice to have a fight event for boss battles (with [Hunter's Instinct](/card/12074) allowing recursion). [Canteen](/card/60573) (with [Relic Hunter](/card/12096) for the slot) and one copy of [Fearless](/card/12069) are your vital horror healing - Canteen is completely standalone and Fearless works similarly to Cosmic Guidance. Canteen replaces [Lucky Charm](/card/12061) in the level 0 deck, which does the same job but a little worse - so this means you're not waiting on an XP purchase to function (though you do need to juggle the accessory slot clash in scenario 1).

- <strong><ins>Staples</ins>:</strong> [Timely Intervention](/card/12081) is great by itself (especially for evades), but also allows you to sometimes fiddle [Old Compass](/card/12083) tests and increase the effective range of ["Look what I found!"](/card/12078). A single copy of [Aleksey Saburov](/card/12072) is included as a panic button - dialled down from two copies in the level 0 deck where he's more essential. Given he's unique, second copies are a bit of a dead card, and you also only play him if you need him as 3 resources is a little steep - his swap to [Fearless](/card/12069) is the final planned upgrade as Fearless is also a little situational. [E-Cache](/card/12089) is E-cache, with [Hunter's Instinct](/card/12074) you can re-use it if needed (but generally the deck is pretty cheap, as [Same Old Thing](/card/60561) covers event costs).

<h2 style="color:rgb(238, 74, 83);"><strong>Playtesting Notes</strong></h2>

I played this deck through two standalones: the Curse of the Rougarou, and Traces to Nowhere.

<ins><strong>The Curse of the Rougarou:</strong></ins> It's been about 4 years since I played Curse so I thought I'd dust it off, paying 2 player and on standard - though I think hard would have been fine.

This deck did what it was supposed to - getting clues automatically or through easy tests. Early on [Old Compass](/card/12083) was played, and and the -2 redo on it came in very handy at having a second bite of tests and facilitating passes. Izzie took on the titular [Curse](/card/81029), and seeing as she had [Canteen](/card/60573) this allowed for easy healing off of horror (with [Fearless](/card/12069) also making an appearance to heal more). A nice feature of Izzie is the support she can offer other investigators through her skills, and at the end of the scenario she committed [On the Brink](/card/12084) to Daniela's fight actions vs. the [Rougarou](/card/81028) helping [Vicious Blow](/card/60169) over-succeed and deal max damage. Izzie did also find [Consume Life](/card/60462) and had cards to boost it - but as Daniela could hit a lot harder we let her get to it instead!

Overall it was a solid performance and what you expect - getting clues by attrition and playing a decent support role. It's less flashy than murdering a werewolf like Daniela did, but it's no less useful!

<ins><strong>Traces to Nowhere</strong></ins>: We played two player on standard. It can be a tough scenario so two player on standard is still a difficult experience. I'll aim to keep spoilers minimal! Play time was 2 and a half hours (there's randomness in the scenario, and we got some of the worst possible randomness). However it does mean the deck was more thoroughly playtested!

Izzie was awesome throughout, with almost all of her combos firing. I got [Rabbit's Foot](/card/60560) and [Canteen](/card/60573) in my opening hand, and drew [Old Compass](/card/60558) early through Rabbit's Foot - so I was off to a good start! There's plenty of low shroud locations in Traces, so Old Compass was always useful - and I did pair it to ["Look what I found!"](/card/12078) and the Compass's redo to crack a 4 shroud location (with this fail tech maintaining card advantage with Rabbit's Foot).

Early on I took 3 damage (you can spend clues as a <span class="icon-free"></span> action to draw a potentially useful card - in this case it was an encounter card enemy who attacked me for 2 damage, with Izzie already having one damage from elsewhere). [Daniela](/card/12001) disposed of the bad guy for me next turn. A few turns later I had [Consume Life](/card/60462) and [Cosmic Guidance](/card/60468), and so killed a 2 health enemy engaged with Daniela to heal two damage. I then committed Cosmic Guidance out of my discard pile on a <span class="icon-willpower"></span> treachery later to full heal. There is a willpower test on a location card, and on several other scenario cards - so as stated there's plenty of opportunity to heal even without Consume Life tests. Dropping to 3 health early made be decrease survivability rating to 3 ★ as this deck is susceptible to RNG - if I hadn't found a way to heal you can see with a bit more bad RNG you can be defeated within the first few rounds.

[Will of the Cosmos](/card/12066) came in handy, I used it to get two clues and put a doom on [Same Old Thing](/card/60561). This was the same turn where I played [Consume Life](/card/60462) (I played Will, moved to a connecting location, killed the bad guy), which used up the remaining supplies on Same Old Thing and triggered discard to remove the doom. Talk about efficiency!

But by far the best combo here was [Do-or-Die](/card/60568) with [Scrape By](/card/12082). Traces to Nowhere does have some high difficulty Parleys which test a variety of stats, so Scrape By allowed me to pass an <span class="icon-intellect"></span> Parley very early. I used it to kill a two health enemy at one point using two basic fight actions (the first I passed with On the Brink). When Daniela needed breathing room late in the scenario, I engaged the big boss off of her and used it to pass the evade. As the scenario went on for a _long_ time, eventually I had only a few cards in my deck and when I reshuffled Do-or-Die I would see it all the time - allowing me to constantly recur Scrape By (especially when you can further commit skills to Do-or-Die tests such as [On the Brink](/card/12084) or [Timely Intervention](/card/12081) to help pass). I think I must have played Scrape By about 8 times over the course of the scenario, when you consider you're often using it on crucial actions it's absurdly powerful. All in all, Izzie was the MVP this time by a mile!

<h2 style="color:rgb(238, 74, 83);"><strong>Final thoughts</strong></h2>

This deck almost broke me! I always wanted fail tech with [Will of the Cosmos](/card/12066) for testless clues, but that takes up around 12 cards (the aforementioned, then [upgraded Old Compass](/card/12083), [Rabbit's Foot](/card/60560), [Hunter's Instinct](/card/12074), [Same Old Thing](/card/60561) and ["Look what I found!"](/card/12078)). On top of this, [Scrape By](/card/12082) with [Do-or-Die](/card/60568) is another auto-include combo. After all that, it was hard to put a _deck_ around the deck!

Before this, I planned both an [upgraded Meat Cleaver](/card/12085) deck and an [Arcane Initiate](/card/60455) plus [Shadowmeld](/card/60461) (because it's cheap!) - still using [Consume Life](/card/60462), [Cosmic Guidance](/card/60468) and [Fearless](/card/12069) on the second deck for <span class="icon-willpower"></span> testing. For the former, I could see it wouldn't work well without finding the Cleaver - and the [level 0 Meat Cleaver](/card/12077) is pretty sucky (plus the 3XP upgrade is a big jump). For the latter, there's vanishingly little room to include additional spell targets for Initiate, and [Second Sight](/card/60460) is both expensive and anti-synergistic with [Old Compass](/card/12083).

This deck borrows bits from both, starting with [Aleksey Saburov](/card/12072) and [Lucky Charm](/card/12061) - but phasing these out, and then leaving [Consume Life](/card/60462) in to combo with [Cosmic Guidance](/card/60468) and [Fearless](/card/12069) (though crucially, none of these actually _need_ to combo to function). If there's one negative to this deck it's card draw - as it's only [On the Brink](/card/12084) and [Rabbit's Foot](/card/60560) - but I think that's a general Izzie issue: to include skill cantrips ([Guts](/card/12090), etc.) then it's harder to include <span class="icon-wild"></span> skill cards. Upgraded On the Brink helps however, and if you do get Rabbit's Foot into play early than you're golden.

I guess I'm now "happy" with this deck, and honestly the Traces to Nowhere playthrough it was remarkably good. For those of you horrified by the photoshop, I'm British so I'm allowed (I think). It's a rather creepy image of the Queen in the first place (not AI - someone actually pained that). Hope you enjoy!
