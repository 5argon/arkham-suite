<h2 style="color:rgb(238, 74, 83);"><strong>Overview</strong></h2>

- <strong><ins>Witchy healing</ins>:</strong> This deck heals both damage with [Cosmic Guidance](/card/60468) and horror with [Fearless](/card/12069) on <span class="icon-willpower"></span> tests, and Izzie can commit these from her discard pile with her <span class="icon-reaction"></span> ability. [Cosmic Flame](/card/12059) and [Second Sight](/card/60460) allow for persistent Willpower tests, boosted by [Jim](/card/12060) and [Bloodstone](/card/60457). [Retribution](/card/60473) is your payoff for keeping healthy, allowing Izzie to avenge her sister by dealing large amounts of testless damage against bosses.

- <strong><ins>Smooth consistency</ins>:</strong> [Arcane Initiate](/card/60455) tracks down your spell assets and events, while [Favor of Baalshandor](/card/60463) acts as a psuedo-[Emergency Cache](/card/12089). The damage from Favor isn't ideal, but it's not direct and so it can go on an Initiate, [Jim](/card/12060), [Bloodstone](/card/60457) or yourself if you have [Cosmic Guidance](/card/60468) handy. [Isabelle's Twin .45s](/card/12014) and an [Old Compass](/card/60558) provide back-up assets.

- <strong><ins>Skillz gallore</ins>:</strong> In addition to [Cosmic Guidance](/card/60468) and [Fearless](/card/12069), this deck has a load of other useful skills. [Eldritch Whispers](/card/60471) can provide asset recharging with a bit of luck, with Izzie's ability meaning you can play Whispers semi-frequently and so improve your chances. [Guts](/card/12090) gives card draw, especially with your <span class="icon-reaction"></span> and [Jim](/card/12060). [Timely Intervention](/card/60582) can boost failures into successes retroactively, offering a godly +3 to any test when it's in hand.

- <strong><ins>Encounter management</ins>:</strong> The tried and true [Ward of Protection](/card/12065) is in this deck, and [Timely Intervention](/card/60582) generally covers your ass too. Upgraded [Levelheaded](/card/60579) gives you the means to tackle agility treacheries, as the deck is otherwise sparse on <span class="icon-agility"></span> icons to commit.

- <strong><ins>What to mulligan</ins>:</strong> You want spells, [Arcane Initiate](/card/60455) (if you don't have any spells), resource cards and [Bloodstone](/card/60457) - probably in that exact order. [Jim](/card/12060) and [Timely Intervention](/card/60582) are very handy as well. If you're missing these cards, you'll have potentially drawn a backup non-spell asset to help in the meantime. While you can't run Jim and an Initiate in tandem in the 19 XP deck, you'll usually kill your Initiates before too long so slot clash isn't a problem.

<h2 style="color:rgb(238, 74, 83);"><strong>Explain Yourself!</strong></h2>

This setup resembles my original Chapter 2 core box deck, and for good reason. Actionless healing for Izzie is so strong that for me it can't be ignored, making [Cosmic Guidance](/card/60468) a must - and realistically it works best with Mystic spell asset engine. [Bloodstone](/card/60457) is also a huge help, patching up your low health generally while boosting <span class="icon-willpower"></span> to boot. [Retribution](/card/60473) provides a fun payoff for your healing tech, setting Izzie apart from true Mystics investigators who can't recur healing quite so readily. [Arcane Initiate](/card/60455) also helps to find Retribution given it's a spell, so a single copy is recommended early (which you'll still see often enough courtesy of Initiate) and then the final copy later.

In terms of omissions, [Scrape By](/card/12082) is on Miguel's deck - thereby making these fully compatible. While Izzie might fail tests, [Timely Intervention](/card/60582) and [Levelheaded](/card/60579) make this less likely, and [Ward of Protection](/card/12065) can banish treachery tests you don't want to take. [Loner](/card/60557) is also better used in the Miguel deck to drag enemies into traps at empty locations (making it a bonafide combo in that deck, rather than just a nice-to-have like it would be here). I was tempted to leave [Soul Link](/card/12067) in the deck (especially as it's a spell and so can be drawn with [Arcane Initiate](/card/60455)) but I think the horror received between it, your ability and [Ward of Protection](/card/12065) then becomes a little much.

<h2 style="color:rgb(238, 74, 83);"><strong>Compatability Matrix</strong></h2>

Everyone likes a good matrix! Below are all the decks that are compatible at two player using one Chapter 2 Core and one 2026 Starter Deck set:

<img src="https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HFjVlxmWUAAlUZ0.png"/>

For higher player counts I've not 'compatabilized' the basic skills ([Overpower](/card/12092), [Manual Dexterity](/card/12091), [Guts](/card/12090), [Perception](/card/12093) and [Unexpected Courage](/card/12094)). There are only 4 copies of each in the Chapter 2 core, enough for any two decks. If playing 3+ players and there's a clash, you can either:

- Swap with an appropriate level 0 class skill which that deck can use (e.g. [On the Brink](/card/60569) for [Unexpected Courage](/card/12094)).

- Swap with a class event or asset with matching or similar symbols (e.g. [Working a Hunch](/card/12038) for [Perception](/card/12093)).

- Swap with a different underused basic skill you still believe would be useful for the deck (e.g. [Guts](/card/12090) for [Unexpected Courage](/card/12094)).

- Proxy however you see fit (especially if you own Chapter 1 cards, just use the old school versions and remember the slight difference around who draws the card).

I empower you to do sensible things!

<h2 style="color:rgb(238, 74, 83);"><strong>Final thoughts</strong></h2>

Comparing to my first Chapter 2 core only series, some of these decks provide a new playstyle for the core investigators while others will hone what was already there - in this case, it's very much the latter! Honestly, [Cosmic Guidance](/card/60468) and bigger, bolder [Timely Intervention](/card/60582) were far too good to pass up, and leaning Mystic means more consistency (especially with [Arcane Initiate](/card/60455)) and discounts (from [Favor of Baalshandor](/card/60463)). [Retribution](/card/60473) is the nice - yet dangerous - icing on the cake, very fitting for an investigator who wants to make evildoers "pay in blood". #Justice4Jenny
