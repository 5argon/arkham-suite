<h2 style="color:rgb(186, 129, 242);"><strong>Overview</strong></h2>

- <strong><ins>Putting the band back together</ins>:</strong> With [Jim](/card/60479) on sax and a [Bloodstone](/card/60457) amulet, Marie clue-finds at 6 <span class="icon-willpower"></span> with [Second Sight](/card/60460). Meanwhile, [Hand-Crank Flashlight](/card/12088) means you to investigate at a <span class="icon-intellect"></span> 5 too (useful to avoid losing charges Sight with a <span class="icon-cultist"></span> pull). [Will of the Cosmos](/card/12066) gives clues for "free", putting the doom on the easily discardable Flashlight or an [Offering Bowl](/card/60456). [Augur of Elokoss](/card/12070) can test at a 10 for high shroud locations, with [Premonition](/card/12064) to activate its bonus. [Grand-Mère's Charm](/card/60452) can recharge Second Sight (e.g. after Bloodstone has been discarded).

- <strong><ins>Deck-churning behaviour</ins>:</strong> By taking damage, Marie draws two cards and discards one <ins>every phase</ins>. [Offering Bowl](/card/60456) and [Blood Curse](/card/60467) provide damage readily, in addition to enemy attacks, encounter effects and whenever you use [Grand-Mère's Charm](/card/60452). Upgraded [Jim](/card/60479) also provides a resource and an extra card for damage you receive once per round. All told, you should usually have a sizeable hand to tackle [Called to Guinée](/card/60453).

- <strong><ins>Cash4Blood</ins>:</strong> Each [Offering Bowl](/card/60456) nets five resources for 3 damage, which is readily healed with [Cosmic Guidance](/card/60468), [Infuse Life](/card/60464), your <span class="icon-elder_sign"></span> ability or [Blood Ward](/card/60472) (or soaked with [Jim](/card/60479) or [Bloodstone](/card/60457)). As highlighted Jim also gives you resources once per turn when you take damage or horror, and [Emergency Cache](/card/01088) provides more on top. This resource generation means high cost events like Infuse Life are feasible. I guess it's Arkham's version of Cash4Gold?

- <strong><ins>Enemy management</ins>:</strong> Enemy management is [Shadowmeld](/card/60461) and basic evades, as well as cancelling attacks with [Blood Ward](/card/60472). You can use Shadowmeld for action compression by spending a charge to move to an adjacent location, but you don't have to and it doesn't spend charges otherwise. This is a great way to refund an enemy management action.

- <strong><ins>Horror soak and encounter management</ins>:</strong> I've mentioned that [Jim](/card/60479) is good damage soak, turns out he's good horror soak too. With Marie's above average <span class="icon-willpower"></span> stat, you shouldn't take much horror to begin with. There's a crazy amount of <span class="icon-wild"></span> icons for treachery tests, but the neat part is sometimes you'll want to fail these and take damage to trigger Marie's ability.

<h2 style="color:rgb(186, 129, 242);"><strong>Explain Yourself!</strong></h2>

Just like [Jacqueline Fine's](/card/60401) above average Intellect, a great feature of Marie is her <span class="icon-intellect"></span> stat of 4. It means that with a [Hand-Crank Flashlight](/card/12088) you can readily find clues even without [Second Sight](/card/60460), especially with all the <span class="icon-wild"></span> icons in the evergreen Mystic card pool. For me this casts Marie as a clue-finder, so I lent into that - focusing on [Shadowmeld](/card/60461) (to get to new locations and find clues), plus any additional clue support I could get. I also couldn't resist [Jim Culver](/card/60479), mainly because he incentivises Marie's ability (but also he can play sax while Marie does vocals).

Even considering the Mystic cards I ringfenced for Dexter, I don't think there's any changes I would make to this deck. For me there isn't enough targets of [Favor of Baalshandor](/card/60463) for it to be worthwhile, and with the evade focus then [Spiritual Charm](/card/60466) isn't sensible. [Arcane Initiate](/card/60455), while normally a life-saver for Mystics, is much less needed here due to Marie's ability. [Ritual Dagger](/card/60475) feels pricey in terms of XP just to shuffle events back into your deck, especially when you can use assets to duplicate a lot of what events can do.

<h2 style="color:rgb(186, 129, 242);"><strong>Compatability Matrix</strong></h2>

Everyone likes a good matrix! Below are all the decks that are compatible at two player using one Chapter 2 Core and one 2026 Starter Deck set:

<img src="/image/starter/hungry-colquhoun/ch2-starter-guide/blood-money/HFjVlxmWUAAlUZ0.webp"/>

For higher player counts I've not 'compatabilized' the basic skills ([Overpower](/card/12092), [Manual Dexterity](/card/12091), [Guts](/card/12090), [Perception](/card/12093) and [Unexpected Courage](/card/12094)). There are only 4 copies of each in the Chapter 2 core, enough for any two decks. If playing 3+ players and there's a clash, you can either:

- Swap with an appropriate level 0 class skill which that deck can use (e.g. [On the Brink](/card/60569) for [Unexpected Courage](/card/12094)).

- Swap with a class event or asset with matching or similar symbols (e.g. [Working a Hunch](/card/12038) for [Perception](/card/12093)).

- Swap with a different underused basic skill you still believe would be useful for the deck (e.g. [Guts](/card/12090) for [Unexpected Courage](/card/12094)).

- Proxy however you see fit (especially if you own Chapter 1 cards, just use the old school versions and remember the slight difference around who draws the card).

<h2 style="color:rgb(186, 129, 242);"><strong>Final thoughts</strong></h2>

Marie is really good! She plays similarly to Trish, with slightly worse movement (but still good movement with [Shadowmeld](/card/60461)) but on the whole better clue assets and events. On top of this she has absurd card draw, so her consistency is extremely high. I'd say [Augur of Elokoss](/card/12070) is the slight sleeper hit, while 3 XP for a +1 clue event is pricey by Chapter 1 terms - I kind of like the rebalance of XP costs for Chapter 2. It makes your charges on [Second Sight](/card/60460) easier to conserve, and the skill boost it provides is massive, so its presence gels the deck together...
