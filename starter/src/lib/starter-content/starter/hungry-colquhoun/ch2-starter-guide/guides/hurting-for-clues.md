<h2 style="color:rgb(92, 180, 253);"><strong>Overview</strong></h2>

- <strong><ins>The Sherman tank</ins>:</strong> This deck is designed to incentivize dealing with enemies. [Stalk Prey](/card/60567) is used to spawn an enemy in and get a clue and a card for doing so (definitely a target for your opening hand), then if you draw a card with an enemy engaged (provoking an AoO) you get more clues with tanky [Detective Sherman](/card/60177) and [Lesson Learned](/card/12022). You can then punish this AoO with your <span class="icon-reaction"></span> ability using [Sledgehammer](/card/12028), even using the <span class="icon-action"></span><span class="icon-action"></span> ability if you so choose - which comes in at a 9 test with Sherman. [Hunter's Instinct](/card/12074) gives further benefit from bad guys, recurring events such as Lesson Learned and Stalk Prey, or resource economy like [Emergency Cache](/card/12089).

- <strong><ins>That survival instinct</ins></strong><strong>:</strong> Daniela makes a lot of use out of her survival pool, using [Same Old Thing](/card/60561) to discount events, [Extra Rations](/card/60570) for healing, and then [Do-or-Die](/card/60568) for added recursion of <span class="icon-survivor"></span> assets or events. All of this gives a nice contrast to true blue Tommy!

- <strong><ins>Super soaker</ins>:</strong> [Detective Sherman](/card/60177) (and before Sherman, [Rookie Cop](/card/60157)) provide horror soak, with one copy of [Canteen](/card/60573) meaning you're not reliant on Sherman alone to keep sane. [Extra Rations](/card/60570) keep Sherman healthy and makes [In Harm's Way](/card/12003) almost harmless. Sherman or Rookie Cop can soak for other investigators, letting you tank AoOs aimed at them to punish with a <span class="icon-reaction"></span> attack. In addition to enemy attacks, if you soak damage from treacheries with Sherman this also can provide a clue.

- <strong><ins>Consistency</ins>:</strong> There's a 93% chance of drawing either a weapon or [Right Tool for the Job](/card/12023) in a hard mulligan. This is reasonable consistency, especially with further card draw from [Overpower](/card/12092) and [Stalk Prey](/card/60567), and from drawing a card with an enemy engaged to punish with your <span class="icon-reaction"></span> ability. For clue finding consistency, ["Make 'em sing"](/card/60160) helps support.

- <strong><ins>Encounter deck</ins>:</strong> The saving grace of this deck is [Scrape By](/card/12082) which allows Daniela to pass any test should she need to. Being able to recur this with [Do-or-Die](/card/60568) adds robustness. While other encounter options are a little thin on the ground, having one which is very strong convinced me to provide a 4 ★ encounter defense rating.

<h2 style="color:rgb(92, 180, 253);"><strong>Explain Yourself!</strong></h2>

The new starter decks added some really interesting event support for Daniela (including card draw and clues from [Stalk Prey](/card/60567)), with more clue support from allies as well. I wanted to capitalize on these events and also use recursion tech such as [Hunter's Instinct](/card/12074). Clue tech really rounds Daniela off nicely, as she hits hard already for me there's a sense of diminishing returns in more damage capability.

In terms of what's missing, there's [Bounty](/card/60161) - but with [Same Old Thing](/card/60561) and [Emergency Cache](/card/12089) (which you can recur) you're wealthy enough (no [Logan](/card/12018) on a similar train of thought). If you're not also running Tommy, you could run a second copy of [Right Tool for the Job](/card/12023), but equally it's not going to transform the deck. [Scene of the Crime](/card/12024) is alright, but I like all the other clue options - e.g. ["Make 'em sing"](/card/60160) - even more. While you could say 8 suitable events is low for [Hunter's Instinct](/card/12074), in practice I think this works - especially as you can loop a single event over and over.

<h2 style="color:rgb(92, 180, 253);"><strong>Compatability Matrix</strong></h2>

Everyone likes a good matrix! Below are all the decks that are compatible at two player using one Chapter 2 Core and one 2026 Starter Deck set:

<img src="/image/starter/hungry-colquhoun/ch2-starter-guide/hurting-for-clues/HFjVlxmWUAAlUZ0.webp"/>

For higher player counts I've not 'compatabilized' the basic skills ([Overpower](/card/12092), [Manual Dexterity](/card/12091), [Guts](/card/12090), [Perception](/card/12093) and [Unexpected Courage](/card/12094)). There are only 4 copies of each in the Chapter 2 core, enough for any two decks. If playing 3+ players and there's a clash, you can either:

- Swap with an appropriate level 0 class skill which that deck can use (e.g. [On the Brink](/card/60569) for [Unexpected Courage](/card/12094)).

- Swap with a class event or asset with matching or similar symbols (e.g. [Working a Hunch](/card/12038) for [Perception](/card/12093)).

- Swap with a different underused basic skill you still believe would be useful for the deck (e.g. [Guts](/card/12090) for [Unexpected Courage](/card/12094)).

- Proxy however you see fit (especially if you own Chapter 1 cards, just use the old school versions and remember the slight difference around who draws the card).

I empower you to do sensible things!

<h2 style="color:rgb(92, 180, 253);"><strong>Final thoughts</strong></h2>

We've already seen big damage Daniela, so this deck tries something new. I tested this deck on Hard difficulty, and with the [Sledgehammer](/card/12028) big boost and auto-clues it performed quite well.

As it stands, Guardian with Survivor splash is the only way to run both [Stalk Prey](/card/60567) and together with all the blue enemy clue tech ([Lesson Learned](/card/12022), ["Make 'em sing"](/card/60160), [Detective Sherman](/card/60177)). This gives Daniela wild-eyed, hammer-wielding hunter vibes, which is neat and a nice change of pace from Tommy.
