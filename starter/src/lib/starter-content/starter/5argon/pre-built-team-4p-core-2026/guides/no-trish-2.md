The internet is lacking Core 2026 starter decks that work up to 4 players with no adjustments right now, so I wanted to fill this gap.

At a 4 player count and limited to Core 2026 only, cards will be scarce and you are forced to "team build" rather than deckbuild. If someone modifies their cards, there's a high chance that it is also someone else's good card. You can't really name each individual deck as anything in this limitation, so let's think of them together as a team. This is a "No [<span class="fg-rogue">Trish</span>](/card/12007)" team, with all her <span class="icon-rogue"></span> Rogue cards going to [<span class="fg-mystic">Dexter Drake</span>](/card/12010) and all her <span class="icon-seeker"></span> Seeker cards going to [<span class="fg-seeker">Joe Diamond</span>](/card/12004). [<span class="fg-guardian">Daniela Reyes</span>](/card/12001) and [<span class="fg-survivor">Isabelle Barnes</span>](/card/12013) do have to share with each other, as well as with those other two members.

<span class="thumbnail small">[**Pre-Built Team for 4P Core 2026**](https://arkhamdb.com/decklists?author=5argon&name=Pre-Built+Team+for+4P+Core+2026) : This series is about a set of 4 Core 2026 investigator decks built together, taking every advantage of the cards donated from that known one missing Core investigator stated in the team name. They are automatically compatible with 2P or 3P games as well, as long as that one investigator stays missing. </span>

# Stop! Have you played the pre-con yet?

Read this if you are a newcomer. Out of the box, the game comes with 5 playable decks, where each deck has **1 copy of every non-neutral card** it can use. This is the most unopinionated, flexible way the game could make sure they work in 2, 3, or 4 player teams no matter who's picked, with no one taking each other's cards. (Even if this game went up to 5 players, that would still work.)

Obviously the performance is not ideal, and you will see cards that don't fit with what the investigator is good at, or even cards waiting for future non-Core products to fulfill their purpose, but you are forced to use them now! But performance isn't the purpose of these decks. **I highly recommend that you play through Brethren of Ash at least once using the pre-con** :

- Getting to know the investigators. Core Set investigators can use 2 classes, and they form a circular chain of classes. A very optimized deck might automatically lean into the primary class, and that removes the "wonders" that you could have stumbled onto. You won't know how far they can flex if you start with a deck that's too consistent.
- Table talk. Since there's only 1 copy of each class card in each deck, if your friend has overlapping class access with you, then when they see you play something, they learn as well and might get their own copy of that card soon. When a card doesn't work with your investigator, teamwork happens. You will come up with many more interesting, scrappy combos than an optimized team would.
- There's always a way out. If you play with the pre-con, you will surely be pushed toward certain less-than-ideal solutions throughout Brethren of Ash, which I think will get you hooked on the game.

That is, I wanted to make sure you know this pre-built team isn't intended to replace your first-time experience. Rather, this should be **one example** of an adjustment you can make. Double up copies of cards you agree with, keep one-ofs of cards you think are situational or want to share, and cut cards you don't like. That's deckbuilding / team building. Have fun!

# The No Trish Team

Since Core 2026 investigators form a ring of classes, when [<span class="fg-rogue">Trish Scarborough</span>](/card/12007) is gone, the nutrients go to [<span class="fg-seeker">Joe Diamond</span>](/card/12004) and [<span class="fg-mystic">Dexter Drake</span>](/card/12010).

The problem with the Core 2026 set is that only [<span class="fg-rogue">Trish Scarborough</span>](/card/12007) is good (and very good) at evasion. The game designers put a lot more emphasis on evasion than in the previous chapter of this game. So in this team, I need to get EVERYONE somehow able to evade, since unlike Fight, Evade needs engagement. If only a certain someone could evade, and they need to engage and then evade, that'd be a huge tempo loss. The loss of Trish also reduces investigation power.

<span class="icon-mystic"></span> [**<span class="fg-mystic">Dexter Drake</span>**](/card/12010)

<a href="/image/starter/5argon/pre-built-team-4p-core-2026/no-trish-2/4p-no-trish-dexter.webp" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><img src="/image/starter/5argon/pre-built-team-4p-core-2026/no-trish-2/4p-no-trish-dexter.webp" alt="Dexter" width="500" style="width:500px; max-width:100%; height:auto;"/></a>

For this deck we should start with Dexter, since this decision is quite bold. Because cards are limited and all Rogue cards are free to use, it is tempting to give him the less optimal <span class="icon-agility"></span> suite of [<span class="fg-rogue">Thieves' Kit</span>](/card/12049) + [<span class="fg-rogue">M1903 Hammerless</span>](/card/12045) + [<span class="fg-rogue">Olivier Bishop</span>](/card/12046) + [<span class="fg-rogue">Sticky Fingers</span>](/card/12048), and so [<span class="fg-survivor">Isabelle Barnes</span>](/card/12013) can have her full working suite of [<span class="fg-mystic">Cosmic Flame</span>](/card/12059) + [<span class="fg-mystic">Second Sight</span>](/card/12062). Sure, 3 <span class="icon-agility"></span> is not impressive, and his 5 <span class="icon-willpower"></span> is left unused. But we put up with it for the first scenario, because this deck's upgrade plan is to spend 10 XP (!) on 2x [<span class="fg-mystic">Cosmic Flame (5)</span>](/card/12071), and then your team will be cosmic flaming to victory with both Dexter and Isabelle. I decided to leave both [<span class="fg-rogue">Breaking and Entering</span>](/card/12050) in the collection, as you likely won't get +2 over-success even with [<span class="fg-rogue">Olivier Bishop</span>](/card/12046), and [<span class="fg-rogue">Silver Tongue</span>](/card/12047) isn't getting +2 for 1 resource on it. The best evasion he can use is [<span class="fg-rogue">Silver Tongue</span>](/card/12047) + [<span class="fg-rogue">Sticky Fingers</span>](/card/12048) (since you'd get refunded the 1 resource you just spent) with [<span class="fg-mystic">Soul Link</span>](/card/12067) to push that limit. [<span class="fg-mystic">Soul Link</span>](/card/12067) evasion horror can be put on [<span class="fg-mystic">Cloak of Resonance</span>](/card/12058) to make a bit of forward progress, as well as [<span class="fg-mystic">Ward of Protection</span>](/card/12065) horror. This deck keeps 2x [<span class="fg-rogue">Silver Tongue</span>](/card/12047) until the end. [<span class="fg-mystic">Premonition</span>](/card/12064) can help avoid over-spending on evasion.

<span class="icon-survivor"></span> [**<span class="fg-survivor">Isabelle Barnes</span>**](/card/12013)

<a href="/image/starter/5argon/pre-built-team-4p-core-2026/no-trish-2/4p-no-trish-isabelle.webp" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><img src="/image/starter/5argon/pre-built-team-4p-core-2026/no-trish-2/4p-no-trish-isabelle.webp" alt="Isabelle" width="500" style="width:500px; max-width:100%; height:auto;"/></a>

Isabelle gets a comfortable full set of 2x [<span class="fg-mystic">Cosmic Flame</span>](/card/12059) + 2x [<span class="fg-mystic">Second Sight</span>](/card/12062) + 2x [<span class="fg-mystic">Jim Culver</span>](/card/12060), along with the [<span class="fg-mystic">Lucky Charm</span>](/card/12061) + [<span class="fg-mystic">Cloak of Resonance</span>](/card/12058) damage-pinging technique. To emphasize evasion, this deck will not remove [<span class="fg-survivor">Slippery</span>](/card/12080), so when Dexter tries to go for a big evade with [<span class="fg-rogue">Silver Tongue</span>](/card/12047), that exhaust sticks for one more round. You can also commit it on your own if you have [<span class="fg-survivor">Timely Intervention</span>](/card/12081) ready in hand. [<span class="fg-survivor">"Shove off!"</span>](/card/12079) are removed after scenario 1, and [<span class="fg-survivor">Timely Intervention</span>](/card/12081) can help with that test too. [<span class="fg-mystic">Premonition</span>](/card/12064) can be a good way to ensure evasion however much it takes. [<span class="fg-survivor">Hunter's Instinct</span>](/card/12074) is used to replay [<span class="fg-mystic">Premonition</span>](/card/12064), [<span class="fg-neutral">Emergency Cache</span>](/card/12089), and [<span class="fg-survivor">"Look what I found!"</span>](/card/12078).

<span class="icon-guardian"></span> [**<span class="fg-guardian">Daniela Reyes</span>**](/card/12001)

<a href="/image/starter/5argon/pre-built-team-4p-core-2026/no-trish-2/4p-no-trish-daniela.webp" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><img src="/image/starter/5argon/pre-built-team-4p-core-2026/no-trish-2/4p-no-trish-daniela.webp" alt="Daniela" width="500" style="width:500px; max-width:100%; height:auto;"/></a>

Since Isabelle is on [<span class="fg-mystic">Jim Culver</span>](/card/12060) with her full suite of spells, Daniela uses [<span class="fg-guardian">Logan Hastings</span>](/card/12018) + [<span class="fg-survivor">Aleksey Saburov</span>](/card/12072) in this deck, which will be better after [<span class="fg-neutral">Charisma</span>](/card/12095). She shares 1x of [<span class="fg-guardian">Endurance</span>](/card/12017) with [<span class="fg-seeker">Joe Diamond</span>](/card/12004), so both can go broke and get a last-ditch evasion. She also shares 1x of [<span class="fg-guardian">Bodyguard</span>](/card/12016) with Joe, and both will get one upgraded version as well. The 1x [<span class="fg-survivor">Hunter's Instinct</span>](/card/12074) that Isabelle shared with her could be used to replay [<span class="fg-neutral">Emergency Cache</span>](/card/12089), [<span class="fg-guardian">Lesson Learned</span>](/card/12022), or [<span class="fg-guardian">Scene of the Crime</span>](/card/12024). 1x [<span class="fg-guardian">Right Tool for the Job</span>](/card/12023) is also shared with Joe because Joe got a lot of Seeker _**Tool**_ assets from the absent [<span class="fg-rogue">Trish Scarborough</span>](/card/12007), which I want this card to hit.

<span class="icon-seeker"></span> [**<span class="fg-seeker">Joe Diamond</span>**](/card/12004)

<a href="/image/starter/5argon/pre-built-team-4p-core-2026/no-trish-2/4p-no-trish-joe.webp" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><img src="/image/starter/5argon/pre-built-team-4p-core-2026/no-trish-2/4p-no-trish-joe.webp" alt="Joe" width="500" style="width:500px; max-width:100%; height:auto;"/></a>

Joe gets 2x [<span class="fg-guardian">Machete</span>](/card/12020), as 2x [<span class="fg-guardian">M1911</span>](/card/12019) are better on Daniela when she uses her <span class="icon-reaction"></span> to attack unengaged enemies that just hit others. 1x [<span class="fg-guardian">Endurance</span>](/card/12017) is for late-game evasion, when [<span class="fg-seeker">Dorothy Simmons</span>](/card/12030) gets more resources than he needs. Along with [<span class="fg-neutral">Hand-Crank Flashlight</span>](/card/12088), Joe gets all the Seeker hand assets in the game with the absence of Trish. Other than [<span class="fg-neutral">Emergency Cache</span>](/card/12089), there are no resource-boosting cards. To play [<span class="fg-seeker">Fingerprint Kit</span>](/card/12031) and still have some to spare, you will want to mulligan for and play [<span class="fg-seeker">Dorothy Simmons</span>](/card/12030) early, and hope your work brings you more resources.
