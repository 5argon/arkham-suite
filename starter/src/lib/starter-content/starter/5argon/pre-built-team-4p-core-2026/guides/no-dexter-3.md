The internet is lacking Core 2026 starter decks that work up to 4 players with no adjustments right now, so I wanted to fill this gap.

At a 4 player count and limited to Core 2026 only, cards will be scarce and you are forced to "team build" rather than deckbuild. If someone modifies their cards, there's a high chance that it is also someone else's good card. You can't really name each individual deck as anything in this limitation, so let's think of them together as a team. This is a "No [<span class="fg-mystic">Dexter</span>](/card/12010)" team, with all his <span class="icon-mystic"></span> Mystic cards going to [<span class="fg-survivor">Isabelle Barnes</span>](/card/12013) and all his <span class="icon-rogue"></span> Rogue cards going to [<span class="fg-rogue">Trish Scarborough</span>](/card/12007). [<span class="fg-guardian">Daniela Reyes</span>](/card/12001) and [<span class="fg-seeker">Joe Diamond</span>](/card/12004) do have to share with each other, as well as with those other two members.

<span class="thumbnail small">[**Pre-Built Team for 4P Core 2026**](https://arkhamdb.com/decklists?author=5argon&name=Pre-Built+Team+for+4P+Core+2026) : This series is about a set of 4 Core 2026 investigator decks built together, taking every advantage of the cards donated from that known one missing Core investigator stated in the team name. They are automatically compatible with 2P or 3P games as well, as long as that one investigator stays missing. </span>

# Stop! Have you played the pre-con yet?

Read this if you are a newcomer. Out of the box, the game comes with 5 playable decks, where each deck has **1 copy of every non-neutral card** it can use. This is the most unopinionated, flexible way the game could make sure they work in 2, 3, or 4 player teams no matter who's picked, with no one taking each other's cards. (Even if this game went up to 5 players, that would still work.)

Obviously the performance is not ideal, and you will see cards that don't fit with what the investigator is good at, or even cards waiting for future non-Core products to fulfill their purpose, but you are forced to use them now! But performance isn't the purpose of these decks. **I highly recommend that you play through Brethren of Ash at least once using the pre-con** :

- Getting to know the investigators. Core Set investigators can use 2 classes, and they form a circular chain of classes. A very optimized deck might automatically lean into the primary class, and that removes the "wonders" that you could have stumbled onto. You won't know how far they can flex if you start with a deck that's too consistent.
- Table talk. Since there's only 1 copy of each class card in each deck, if your friend has overlapping class access with you, then when they see you play something, they learn as well and might get their own copy of that card soon. When a card doesn't work with your investigator, teamwork happens. You will come up with many more interesting, scrappy combos than an optimized team would.
- There's always a way out. If you play with the pre-con, you will surely be pushed toward certain less-than-ideal solutions throughout Brethren of Ash, which I think will get you hooked on the game.

That is, I wanted to make sure you know this pre-built team isn't intended to replace your first-time experience. Rather, this should be **one example** of an adjustment you can make. Double up copies of cards you agree with, keep one-ofs of cards you think are situational or want to share, and cut cards you don't like. That's deckbuilding / team building. Have fun!

# The No Dexter Team

Since Core 2026 investigators form a ring of classes, when [<span class="fg-mystic">Dexter Drake</span>](/card/12010) is gone, the nutrients go to [<span class="fg-rogue">Trish Scarborough</span>](/card/12007) and [<span class="fg-survivor">Isabelle Barnes</span>](/card/12013). For this team I've decided that :

<span class="icon-rogue"></span> [**<span class="fg-rogue">Trish Scarborough</span>**](/card/12007)

<a href="/image/starter/5argon/pre-built-team-4p-core-2026/no-dexter-3/4p-no-dexter-trish.webp" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><img src="/image/starter/5argon/pre-built-team-4p-core-2026/no-dexter-3/4p-no-dexter-trish.webp" alt="Trish" width="500" style="width:500px; max-width:100%; height:auto;"/></a>

Is a [<span class="fg-rogue">Prestidigitation</span>](/card/12052) user with [<span class="fg-rogue">Olivier Bishop</span>](/card/12046) as her ally, of course, since she has the free evade. This allows discounting expensive hand assets like [<span class="fg-seeker">Local Map</span>](/card/12033) and [<span class="fg-seeker">Fingerprint Kit</span>](/card/12031), while returning much cheaper _**Item**_ assets like [<span class="fg-neutral">Hand-Crank Flashlight</span>](/card/12088) or [<span class="fg-rogue">Thieves' Kit</span>](/card/12049) that have been used up. I've added the fighting option [<span class="fg-rogue">M1903 Hammerless</span>](/card/12045) as another thing that can be used up. [<span class="fg-seeker">Magnifying Glass</span>](/card/12034) would have been good for this too, but I wanted [<span class="fg-seeker">Joe Diamond</span>](/card/12004) to have it to add to his [<span class="fg-seeker">Dorothy Simmons</span>](/card/12030) for unlimited decent investigation.

<span class="icon-survivor"></span> [**<span class="fg-survivor">Isabelle Barnes</span>**](/card/12013)

<a href="/image/starter/5argon/pre-built-team-4p-core-2026/no-dexter-3/4p-no-dexter-isabelle.webp" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><img src="/image/starter/5argon/pre-built-team-4p-core-2026/no-dexter-3/4p-no-dexter-isabelle.webp" alt="Isabelle" width="500" style="width:500px; max-width:100%; height:auto;"/></a>

Gets the full Mystic suite of 2x [<span class="fg-mystic">Cosmic Flame</span>](/card/12059) + 2x [<span class="fg-mystic">Second Sight</span>](/card/12062). She has the clue-cheating [<span class="fg-mystic">Will of the Cosmos</span>](/card/12066) technique (usually Dexter's thing) with [<span class="fg-mystic">Premonition</span>](/card/12064) and [<span class="fg-survivor">Jumpsuit</span>](/card/12075) to void away the doom, with 1x [<span class="fg-survivor">Hunter's Instinct</span>](/card/12074) to bring it back and cheat at more 4-shroud locations. It also brings back [<span class="fg-survivor">"Look what I found!"</span>](/card/12078), which, despite her pitiful 2 <span class="icon-intellect"></span>, becomes workable with [<span class="fg-neutral">Hand-Crank Flashlight</span>](/card/12088) and [<span class="fg-survivor">Timely Intervention</span>](/card/12081). Her ally of choice is [<span class="fg-mystic">Jim Culver</span>](/card/12060), pushing spell accuracy to 5 <span class="icon-willpower"></span>, with [<span class="fg-survivor">Timely Intervention</span>](/card/12081) to make the impossible happen, and now you draw a card with each ability use! She also has 1x [<span class="fg-mystic">Lucky Charm</span>](/card/12061), both for saving her own sanity for ability use and for helping with [<span class="fg-guardian">Daniela Reyes</span>](/card/12001)'s horror. Without [<span class="fg-survivor">Aleksey Saburov</span>](/card/12072), the [<span class="fg-mystic">Lucky Charm</span>](/card/12061) combo is not quite immortal, but I want him on Daniela more.

<span class="icon-seeker"></span> [**<span class="fg-seeker">Joe Diamond</span>**](/card/12004)

<a href="/image/starter/5argon/pre-built-team-4p-core-2026/no-dexter-3/4p-no-dexter-joe.webp" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><img src="/image/starter/5argon/pre-built-team-4p-core-2026/no-dexter-3/4p-no-dexter-joe.webp" alt="Joe" width="500" style="width:500px; max-width:100%; height:auto;"/></a>

It's better to look at Joe before Daniela to see how many of the Guardian cards he took. Due to Daniela's tendency to attack out of the blue when others are hurt, and since that attack is often without engagement, the [<span class="fg-guardian">Machete</span>](/card/12020) is given to Joe so Daniela can have the [<span class="fg-guardian">M1911</span>](/card/12019). Since I left all the Seeker toys for Trish to have fun with her [<span class="fg-rogue">Prestidigitation</span>](/card/12052), I intend Joe to hold [<span class="fg-seeker">Magnifying Glass</span>](/card/12034) permanently. Or double it up, and you can do a one-way transition to [<span class="fg-guardian">Machete</span>](/card/12020) later. [<span class="fg-guardian">Endurance</span>](/card/12017) has the potential to be super good in the endgame, when he has too many resources from [<span class="fg-seeker">Dorothy Simmons</span>](/card/12030), and suddenly Joe is evading at 6 <span class="icon-agility"></span> by paying 2. I want Daniela to have this power too with her [<span class="fg-guardian">Logan Hastings</span>](/card/12018) cash, so let's share 1x each. Joe will be a [<span class="fg-neutral">Charisma</span>](/card/12095) user after upgrading, so [<span class="fg-guardian">Bodyguard</span>](/card/12016) allows a little bit of splitting up. Joe is quite card-starved, so 2x [<span class="fg-seeker">Sharp Rhetoric</span>](/card/12035), which I don't really want, are there, but I still don't want to add the 2x [<span class="fg-seeker">Through the Cracks</span>](/card/12037) and 2x [<span class="fg-neutral">Fedora</span>](/card/12087) that are left in the collection no matter what. 2x [<span class="fg-neutral">Broken Bottle</span>](/card/12086) + 2x [<span class="fg-neutral">Hand-Crank Flashlight</span>](/card/12088) match both of his 4 stats, so it's quite sound.

<span class="icon-guardian"></span> [**<span class="fg-guardian">Daniela Reyes</span>**](/card/12001)

<a href="/image/starter/5argon/pre-built-team-4p-core-2026/no-dexter-3/4p-no-dexter-daniela.webp" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><img src="/image/starter/5argon/pre-built-team-4p-core-2026/no-dexter-3/4p-no-dexter-daniela.webp" alt="Daniela" width="500" style="width:500px; max-width:100%; height:auto;"/></a>

She gets both [<span class="fg-guardian">Logan Hastings</span>](/card/12018) and [<span class="fg-survivor">Aleksey Saburov</span>](/card/12072) and will be the other [<span class="fg-neutral">Charisma</span>](/card/12095) user of this team. [<span class="fg-survivor">Meat Cleaver</span>](/card/12077) is a powerful asset that reaches the +2 <span class="icon-combat"></span> range quickly, and for +1 damage, you can keep healing back if it's not a boss fight. For that, you need lots of soak so you aren't accidentally defeated by horror. The 1x [<span class="fg-survivor">Hunter's Instinct</span>](/card/12074) that's shared with [<span class="fg-survivor">Isabelle Barnes</span>](/card/12013) is a cool one-off that allows you to replay [<span class="fg-neutral">Emergency Cache</span>](/card/12089) and [<span class="fg-guardian">Lesson Learned</span>](/card/12022). More caches mean more ally refreshes and more soak for more counterattacks. [<span class="fg-survivor">Bandages</span>](/card/12073) can be used to cover [<span class="fg-survivor">Isabelle Barnes</span>](/card/12013)'s [<span class="fg-neutral">Breaking Point</span>](/card/12015), which works for each "then". (You can trigger <span class="icon-reaction"></span> on each "then", but the effects queue up to occur after all the "then"s, so Isabelle does need to survive the whole thing before being healed.)
