Puzzling card! [<span class="fg-survivor">"Shove off!"</span>](/card/12079) is an evasion with damage. Suppose it were damage (a **Fight**) with an automatic evasion instead. That'd be much better, since you wouldn't need to be engaged to exhaust the enemy. (Hello, [<span class="fg-rogue">Polished Cane</span>](/card/60363)?) If you think of it as a Survivor card that lets you "fight" with <span class="icon-agility"></span>, why not use [<span class="fg-survivor">Pocketknife</span>](/card/60559), which is an actual **Fight** that doesn't need engagement?

You also have to pass this evasion yourself, with no accuracy boost. Something like [<span class="fg-survivor">Slippery</span>](/card/12080) adds 1 <span class="icon-agility"></span>, and its long evade might be better than 1 damage overall, plus you can commit it to others' evasions.

The situations where this is useful are highly specific. Say a 3 HP enemy is engaged with you, and the team's fighter has a weapon that deals 2 damage per hit. You evade with this card, bringing it down to 2 HP and no longer engaged with you, so you don't risk friendly fire. Or you can talk with your Rogue to set up [<span class="fg-rogue">M1903 Hammerless</span>](/card/12045) / [<span class="fg-rogue">A Sudden Fall</span>](/card/60367) / [<span class="fg-rogue">A Sudden Fall (2)</span>](/card/60378) so they have an enemy that's already exhausted.

The "if you fail" effect seems like a very minor feature. If you fail, you've already spent the action and 1 resource. It doesn't even let you try again right away, since the return to hand is delayed until the end of your turn!

Is ***Tactic*** its niche, since that's usually a Guardian thing? No! [<span class="fg-survivor">Guerrilla Tactics</span>](/card/60564) is also an evergreen **Tactic**. It can both fight and evade, and has a +1 skill value bonus and the same play cost, with a nifty ranged feature!

But core set cards can be like this. We can speculate about its niche from everything we see. Currently, the most I can squeeze out of this card is with something like [<span class="fg-survivor">Daniel Jameson</span>](/card/60555), which lets it fail and then succeed on the retry, so you get its full effect and also get it back at the end of your turn. But that's quite the gymnastics to make it work. It also pains me, because Lin Hsiang is an amazing artist, but the card is not very playable.
