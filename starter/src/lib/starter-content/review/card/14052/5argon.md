Surely there's no card that starts at Dreaming 5, so this ***Sigil*** sets the goal of lengthening the dream of a more typical Dreaming 2~3 card, and it probably has to outpace the forced -1 dream at the beginning of each of your turns. (e.g. It needs 6 dreams coming into your turn to end up with 5 dream tokens without lengthening it within your turn.)

For the trouble, you are granted a free play of any Dreaming X card at the end of your turn, and since this is a triggered reaction, it'll also skip AoO. It's not going to skip the dreaming phase, but it saves your action. Note that there's a limit of 3 dreaming cards. Since one is presumably being kept at high dreams, you'll have 2 free slots left to schedule the good things with this.

# Dreaming X

"Dreaming X" is the name of a keyword, the same way "Uses (X)" and "Victory X" are mentioned in the rules. So there is no need to define what X is here. "A card with dreaming X" means any card with the dreaming keyword.

> **Reid B. (FFG):** Dreaming X is the keyword, not a defined value
>
> A “dreaming card” refers to a card that is currently in dreaming (facedown in your play area with dreams on it)
>
> A “card with dreaming X” is a card with the keyword on it
