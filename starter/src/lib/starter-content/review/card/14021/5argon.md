So they made a card that could **add** Dreaming X to any other card in the game. Chaos and fun ensue! And by the way, it doesn't require the investigator to be at the same location as you! The only limitations are that it has to be Lv. 0, an asset (no turning any event into a time bomb!), and have a printed resource cost.

With this card, it is safe to say that the wait on Dreaming is roughly equal to 1 resource per round. The Seeker using [<span class="fg-seeker">Oneiric Insight</span>](/card/14021) also directly donates an action to play that card.

By the way, the target investigator must play something among the eligible targets, and the event and skill cards they find within those 9 cards could surprisingly narrow down the choices. It could be a bit funny if they only find high-cost assets they were looking to play instantly with their resources, but now they are forced to wait.

# Dreaming X cards are not eligible

Dreaming X cards don't have a printed resource cost, so it isn't zero either. The crystal ball graphic in place of the cost seems to be equivalent to "-", or to how skill cards have no cost.

> What if you were to choose a Dreaming asset, which don't have a resource cost? Is it eligible and would it allow you to treat the card as if it was Dreaming 0?

> **Nick (FFG):** Not eligible

# Dreaming 0

An asset that costs 0 would have Dreaming 0. It goes in facedown and flips faceup right away, so it's the same as playing it normally.

> do we know what happens with Dreaming 0?

> **Nick (FFG):** Enters facedown and then immediately flips faceup
>
> So essentially functions like playing it normally

# Unique, limit

A dreaming card isn't in play. A unique card or a card with a limit could be in the dreaming state while you have another copy in play. But when it flips faceup and turns out to violate the rule, it can't enter play, and it's discarded. (In normal circumstances, you wouldn't be allowed to play it from your hand.)

> If "limit 1" card or unique card is coming into play due to 0 dream token and it is going to violate the limit or unique rule, you just discard it?

> **Nick (FFG):** Yeah limit 1's and uniques would discard if they cannot enter play

# When is the play?

I've got a ruling answer from FFG that "After you play X" abilities trigger when you play the dreaming card facedown, but an "after you play this card" ability that references the card itself triggers when it flips faceup.

So, for example, if [<span class="fg-seeker">Oneiric Insight</span>](/card/14021) forces [<span class="fg-mystic">Dexter Drake</span>](/card/12010) to play something, he can trigger his <span class="icon-reaction"></span> at the moment he plays that something facedown. But if [<span class="fg-seeker">Laboratory Assistant</span>](/card/12032) is the target of [<span class="fg-seeker">Oneiric Insight</span>](/card/14021), the draw 2 <span class="icon-reaction"></span> on the card itself triggers when it stops dreaming and flips faceup.

> **Nick (FFG):** "After you play X" abilities proc when you play the Dreaming card facedown.
>
> If a Dreaming card has a self-referential "after you play this card," it procs when it flips faceup. (I don't think any dreaming cards in the box say "after you play this card," but you can give Dreaming to other cards, so we had to account for it)
