[<span class="fg-rogue">Double Team</span>](/card/14034) is a kind of [<span class="fg-rogue">Breaking and Entering</span>](/card/12050) for **Fight**, in that it can auto-evade even ***Elite*** enemies. But:

- You deal 1 accurate damage instead of getting a clue.
- Instead of testing against the location's shroud, you test against the enemy's fight value as usual.
- The stat addition can work across 2 investigators! This is new!

Evading an enemy without having to engage it is awesome in general. I think this is great for Rogues, since it saves them an engage action. To build on that accuracy, the investigator fighting should be someone with good <span class="icon-combat"></span> but poor <span class="icon-agility"></span>, like [<span class="fg-guardian">Daniela Reyes</span>](/card/12001).

# Timing

Like [<span class="fg-rogue">Breaking and Entering</span>](/card/12050) and [<span class="fg-rogue">Polished Cane</span>](/card/60363), it adds an auto-evade consequence in ST.7. Therefore, the attacking investigator can't trigger the <span class="icon-reaction"></span> on [<span class="fg-rogue">Sticky Fingers</span>](/card/12048), because that happens in ST.6, while success or failure is still being determined. (Besides, that investigator succeeded at a fight, not an evade, to begin with.)

# Double teaming with yourself

"An investigator at your location" includes you, so you can play it on your own attack and add your <span class="icon-agility"></span> to your own <span class="icon-combat"></span>. You might have to use your imagination for what's happening.
