They are careful not to make ***Melee*** weapons with 2 damage too much better than ***Firearm*** weapons. [<span class="fg-guardian">Machete</span>](/card/12020) has its damage capped with some restrictions. [<span class="fg-survivor">Meat Cleaver</span>](/card/12077) can repeat 2 damage by taking horror. [<span class="fg-guardian">Sledgehammer (3)</span>](/card/12028) and [<span class="fg-guardian">Knight's Longsword</span>](/card/14003) have no <span class="icon-combat"></span> boost. This one has a +2 <span class="icon-combat"></span> boost, but it's in the <span class="icon-rogue"></span> Rogue class at Lv. 3, so off-class Rogue can't get to it (and so it's more likely that the base <span class="icon-combat"></span> won't be higher than 3, I guess?), and the damage is gated by exhaust just like [<span class="fg-rogue">M1903 Hammerless</span>](/card/12045).

I'd look at the +1 damage on this one as a refund on the evade action you want to do first in order to get things from evade payoffs such as [<span class="fg-rogue">Sticky Fingers</span>](/card/12048), [<span class="fg-rogue">Right Under Their Noses</span>](/card/60368), etc. And like [<span class="fg-rogue">M1903 Hammerless</span>](/card/12045), I think it is better on a team with multiple investigators who can evade from time to time, so you don't have to do it all.

Perhaps you can intentionally fight a ready enemy for 1 damage repeatedly in order to farm more resources. It is an additional consequence of succeeding at the attack. It happens in ST.7 along with the attack's damage, after ST.6 has determined that you succeeded. You can reorder it as you wish among all the consequences you are getting in ST.7. 

{{timing 4.007}}

{{rule 4.007 "Resolve the appropriate consequences"}}

For example, if you play [<span class="fg-rogue">Double Team</span>](/card/14034) on the [<span class="fg-rogue">Blade of the Sky-Sea</span>](/card/14040)'s attack while the enemy is not yet exhausted, it adds an evasion consequence in the same ST.7. You can order that consequence to happen first and the fight's damage consequence after it, so now you've satisfied the exhaust clause and get +1 damage.

As additional learning material, look at [<span class="fg-rogue">Sticky Fingers</span>](/card/12048), whose typical wording points to ST.6 and not ST.7, triggering while the test is still determining success or failure.

Maybe in the future you'll have something that needs resources on an enemy's defeat, and then it'd matter that you order the +1 resource consequence first. For example, if someone who can use both [<span class="fg-rogue">Blade of the Sky-Sea</span>](/card/14040) and [<span class="fg-survivor">Field Dressing</span>](/card/60571) is fighting with 1 resource, they'd be able to get +1 resource and use it to play [<span class="fg-survivor">Field Dressing</span>](/card/60571) on the enemy's defeat if the damage is ordered later.
