[<span class="fg-survivor">Hunter's Instinct</span>](/card/12074) is an exciting generic event recursion for Survivor. The hoops you have to jump through is to play it in advance for 2 resources, engaging an enemy, and having Lv. 0 target card at that moment. Playing it in advance when you don't have anything in the discard pile yet does feel weird, finding the right timing to play it can be surprisingly tricky.

Even though the condition requires engaging an enemy you can bring back things unrelated to handling that enemy like [<span class="fg-neutral">Emergency Cache</span>](/card/12089)  or [<span class="fg-survivor">"Look what I found!"</span>](/card/12078). The thing that handles the enemy might be simply an event with high commit icons, like all the Miguel box traps ([<span class="fg-survivor">Decoy Trap</span>](/card/60562), [<span class="fg-survivor">Glassing</span>](/card/60563), [<span class="fg-survivor">Lie in Wait</span>](/card/60566)).

The more exciting thing is when someone with access to this card using it to revive events of their other class access. It can feels like those events are not "supposed to be" replayed but you somehow could! Built in the core set, [<span class="fg-guardian">Daniela Reyes</span>](/card/12001) could replay Guardian events and [<span class="fg-survivor">Isabelle Barnes</span>](/card/12013) can replay Mystic events with it.  The list of cross-class recursion only keep growing so please check often after each release.

# [<span class="fg-guardian">Daniela Reyes</span>](/card/12001)

If you just got the engage from enemy coming back from exhaust in Upkeep Phase, getting [<span class="fg-guardian">Scene of the Crime</span>](/card/12024) seems perfect. You can also grab [<span class="fg-guardian">Bounty</span>](/card/60161) to use after defeating it, or [<span class="fg-guardian">Lesson Learned</span>](/card/12022) if you are looking to provoke AoO to use her <span class="icon-reaction"></span>.

# [<span class="fg-survivor">Isabelle Barnes</span>](/card/12013)

The in-class Mystic event recursion is [<span class="fg-mystic">Ritual Dagger</span>](/card/60475), but that goes back into the deck and might not be as hands-on as [<span class="fg-survivor">Hunter's Instinct</span>](/card/12074).

[<span class="fg-mystic">Will of the Cosmos</span>](/card/12066) and [<span class="fg-mystic">Ward of Protection</span>](/card/12065) are looking to be good target generally. [<span class="fg-mystic">Will of the Cosmos</span>](/card/12066) works with [<span class="fg-survivor">Hunter's Instinct</span>](/card/12074)'s self discarding to clear the doom too. Something cheap and Fast like [<span class="fg-mystic">Premonition</span>](/card/12064) is also interesting, which you can also use right away to deal with the engaged enemy.
