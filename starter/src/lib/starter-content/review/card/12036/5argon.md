I play Pot of Greed! It allows me to draw 2 more cards! An enemy enters your location if it spawns on you, or on a friend at the same location as you. You can point to [<span class="fg-neutral">Pursued</span>](/card/12102)'s reminder text, even though the Grimoire only explicitly talks about entering and exiting under the Move topic. Because you just got 1 resource back in the Upkeep Phase, you will surely have a resource to play this in the Mythos Phase.

{{rule 1.098 "enter the new location"}}

That said, it can get a bit tricky depending on player order, because you have to decide right away, when the enemy spawns, whether to Fast play it or not. To play this correctly, you need to announce the play before the next player draws their encounter card. It might sound inconsequential, but I've seen cases where [<span class="fg-seeker">Gather Intel</span>](/card/12036) draws some sort of weakness card that makes the upcoming encounter draw go south, or even outright defeats you!

If someone moves into your location with an enemy in their threat area, you can also play [<span class="fg-seeker">Gather Intel</span>](/card/12036). Rogues can do this easily with [<span class="fg-rogue">Olivier Bishop</span>](/card/12046), so try talking with them. [<span class="fg-survivor">Loner</span>](/card/60557) can drag enemies along too, but it cannot move into a location with someone there.

{{rule 1.144 "at the same location as the investigator"}}

An another one I could think of is [<span class="fg-mystic">Spiritual Charm</span>](/card/60466) that could move an enemy into you, so also requires coordination with your Mystic.

Lastly, a Hunter enemy moving into your location in the Enemy Phase is another moment you can play it. Hunter Aloof enemy exists, so it is not necessarily that you are going to get hit.

# [<span class="fg-seeker">Joe Diamond</span>](/card/12004)

Playing [<span class="fg-seeker">Gather Intel</span>](/card/12036) in the Mythos Phase and drawing into [<span class="fg-guardian">Scene of the Crime</span>](/card/12024) is as perfect as it gets, as a combo with an enemy spawning on you.

If you play [<span class="fg-seeker">Gather Intel</span>](/card/12036) after a Hunter enemy moves into your location, whether it is going to attack you or your friend, you have a chance of pulling cards that are usable right away with Joe. Those are all <span class="icon-guardian"></span> Guardian cards.

- [<span class="fg-guardian">Counterattack</span>](/card/12026) : You can cancel the attack and even deal 1 damage back to it. This is very cool to pull off, and it works even if it is attacking your friend.
- [<span class="fg-guardian">Lesson Learned</span>](/card/12022) : You can get a clue, but it must attack you.
- [<span class="fg-guardian">Restrained</span>](/card/60165) : You can lock it down, but it must attack you.

All of them require 1 more resource to play in addition to [<span class="fg-seeker">Gather Intel</span>](/card/12036). Note that [<span class="fg-seeker">Detective's Intuition</span>](/card/12005) isn't a good draw here, since the Enemy Phase is not "during your turn".
