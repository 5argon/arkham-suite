A very cheap 2-health soak asset, with an unusually on-demand recursion effect! (Look at [<span class="fg-survivor">Do-or-Die</span>](/card/60568), [<span class="fg-survivor">Hunter's Instinct</span>](/card/12074), [<span class="fg-survivor">Hunter's Instinct (2)</span>](/card/60574), [<span class="fg-survivor">On the Brink</span>](/card/60569), [<span class="fg-survivor">On the Brink (2)</span>](/card/12084), [<span class="fg-survivor">Respite</span>](/card/60577), or [<span class="fg-survivor">Isabelle Barnes</span>](/card/12013), and you'll see the hoops you have to jump through to replay something.) The fact that ***Weapon*** and ***Tool*** aren't level- or class-gated makes this an exciting ability for anyone with <span class="icon-survivor"></span> Survivor access, letting them be both bulky and resourceful.

***Weapon*** and ***Tool*** cards can reach the discard pile through treachery accidents, two-handed things bumping them out, discarding themselves when they run out of uses, or simply because you commit them on purpose. This does require a bit of thought about whether your things can actually reach the discard pile, or will just sit empty in your play area.

# [<span class="fg-guardian">Daniela Reyes</span>](/card/12001)

As a <span class="icon-guardian"></span> Guardian main, she'll find that ***Weapon*** opens up many big things to revive, and that means a complete ammo refill as well. Some of the BuildQL presets above can help you find expensive things to replay. It might help to buy just 1x of those things and replay them for more uses instead, with [<span class="fg-guardian">Right Tool for the Job</span>](/card/12023)'s help to find them, since the trait matches exactly.

High-XP weapons also often come with decent commit icons, though the devs didn't go as overboard with them as I thought: [<span class="fg-guardian">Sledgehammer (3)</span>](/card/12028), [<span class="fg-guardian">Winchester Model 12</span>](/card/12029) and [<span class="fg-guardian">Thompson Submachine Gun</span>](/card/60182) all simply have <span class="icon-combat"></span><span class="icon-combat"></span>. (And juicier things like [<span class="fg-survivor">Meat Cleaver (3)</span>](/card/12085), which could have been useful to commit for <span class="icon-willpower"></span><span class="icon-willpower"></span>, are out of Daniela's reach.)

Daniela also needs to bulk up her health to take more attacks, and to account for the virtual 3 damage from [<span class="fg-neutral">In Harm's Way</span>](/card/12003).

# [<span class="fg-survivor">Isabelle Barnes</span>](/card/12013)

Competition for this [<span class="fg-survivor">Jumpsuit</span>](/card/12075) is fierce within the Core Set team. Isabelle would love to bulk up her 5 health, especially with [<span class="fg-neutral">Breaking Point</span>](/card/12015) threatening her, and also to replay her signature [<span class="fg-survivor">Isabelle's Twin .45s</span>](/card/12014), which is easy to knock into the discard pile with any one-handed asset. This gun can already deal 12 damage in 3 consecutive actions, provided that you solve the <span class="icon-combat"></span> / <span class="icon-agility"></span> mini-game. How about more?

<span class="icon-mystic"></span> Mystic ***Tool*** and ***Weapon*** cards don't look that exciting, unlike the big guns of the <span class="icon-guardian"></span> Guardian class. But we'll see as the game progresses a bit more.
