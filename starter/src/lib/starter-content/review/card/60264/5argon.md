[<span class="fg-seeker">Psychoanalysis</span>](/card/60264) is great fun!

First, its icons are really generous (<span class="icon-intellect"></span><span class="icon-wild"></span>), which positions it as a good utility event you can easily sneak into a deck. You can commit it to get a clue when no one wants the support anymore. Its icons are even more generous than [<span class="fg-seeker">Working a Hunch</span>](/card/12038)'s.

Its actual effect is generous too: it offers a filtered draw to anyone! Note that <span class="icon-guardian"></span> Guardians really lack draw, and you spending an action and 2 resources on them might mean they find their big XP weapon sooner. Or you can choose to heal a hefty 2 horror AND STILL get a kind of filtered draw, by reordering the cards that will come in the upcoming Upkeep Phases.

# The reveal

Another fun thing about this card is that it explicitly asks the target to **reveal** 3 cards. Revealing lets the entire table know about them. It's very flavorful, since it implies the clinical talk happened publicly!

Cards like [<span class="fg-seeker">Establish Motive</span>](/card/60268), [<span class="fg-mystic">Arcane Initiate</span>](/card/60455), [<span class="fg-seeker">Mysterious Grimoire</span>](/card/12040), etc. are searches, and you look at those cards privately.

{{rule 1.160 "that player looks at all of the cards"}}

If you play this game properly, with hidden hands and no talking about the cards in your hand, then seeing the 1 of 3 cards that will be added to the target's hand, or all 3 cards that were reordered and will all come soon, can spark very fun table talk! Something like, "Since you're supposed to have [<span class="fg-guardian">Vicious Blow</span>](/card/12025) by now, I'll punch it down to 2 HP for you!"

# Forcing a weakness

Getting the signature weakness or basic weakness in an unopportune time is bad. If someone sees their weakness among 3 cards they can choose to draw it so they can deal with it when it's still convenient! This and the Lv. 3 version [<span class="fg-seeker">Psychoanalysis (3)</span>](/card/60278) are the only cards in the game that offer this feature **to others**.

- [<span class="fg-seeker">Establish Motive</span>](/card/60268) : Limited to ***Insight*** events, unless the weakness happen to be exactly that.
- [<span class="fg-seeker">Mysterious Grimoire</span>](/card/12040) : "You" only.
- [<span class="fg-seeker">Unbridled Knowledge</span>](/card/12043) : "You" only.

# [<span class="fg-seeker">Carolyn Fern</span>](/card/60251)

I've read a ruling from FFG where someone asked whether the target choosing to heal horror and reorder 3 would satisfy [<span class="fg-seeker">Carolyn Fern</span>](/card/60251)'s <span class="icon-reaction"></span>. The answer is **no**, because that investigator **heals themselves**. Carolyn didn't heal them. She needs to heal herself with [<span class="fg-seeker">Psychoanalysis</span>](/card/60264) to discover a clue.
