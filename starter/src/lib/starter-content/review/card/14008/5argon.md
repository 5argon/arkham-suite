Guardians accelerate dreams. Seeker move dreams. Rogue buy dreams. Mystic extend dreams. Survivor re-dream. But most of those are limited to their own cards. This one is special because [<span class="fg-guardian">Dream Mastery</span>](/card/14008) lets you support others also playing Power of the Dreamlands cards.

Since its <span class="icon-willpower"></span><span class="icon-combat"></span> commit icons are only so-so, I think to play this one the best you have to accelerate other classes' very valuable dreaming cards, or your own dreaming cards but stack up the acceleration. (e.g. from [<span class="fg-guardian">Knight's Longsword</span>](/card/14003))

The usual timing of dreaming card enter play is at the start of your turn. Being able to remove a dream token elsewhere might allow some cool techniques. For example, you have a card with 2 dream tokens, then you can let other player go first and take some test at your location so you can commit [<span class="fg-guardian">Dream Mastery</span>](/card/14008) to accelerate it to 1 dream. When you begin your turn it would now enter play. Some cards like [<span class="fg-guardian">Somnium Shotcaster</span>](/card/14013) with enter play effect might allow you to trigger it even in Mythos Phase tests if it enters play due to [<span class="fg-guardian">Dream Mastery</span>](/card/14008) there.
