As a re-dreaming reloader, compared with [<span class="fg-survivor">Explorer's Sextant</span>](/card/14057) and [<span class="fg-survivor">Lash of Ulthar</span>](/card/14067), this is a more on-demand one since it doesn't require you to fail. It has the same Dreaming 2 wait as [<span class="fg-survivor">Lash of Ulthar</span>](/card/14067).

The flip is a cost to play it, so things like the <span class="icon-reaction"></span> on [<span class="fg-survivor">Staff of Atal</span>](/card/14070) would trigger before the test begins. You'd then also have that 1 extra resource to use within the test, e.g. paying for [<span class="fg-survivor">"Look what I found!"</span>](/card/12078).

It does require you to somehow get this [<span class="fg-survivor">Dream Quest</span>](/card/14060) into the discard pile, and it has no commit icon. Just playing it normally gives you quite a high +2 <span class="icon-intellect"></span>, though, which is just about right against 3 shroud with [<span class="fg-survivor">"Look what I found!"</span>](/card/12078) in hand, I think.

# Reloading

[<span class="fg-survivor">Explorer's Sextant</span>](/card/14057), [<span class="fg-survivor">Lash of Ulthar</span>](/card/14067), and [<span class="fg-survivor">Dream Quest</span>](/card/14060) are all capable of re-dreaming an asset. It is clear that Survivors won't see this as a drawback. They can add [<span class="fg-survivor">Staff of Atal</span>](/card/14070) to do this sort of thing better.

Since it can flip any kind of asset, reloading things like [<span class="fg-survivor">Same Old Thing</span>](/card/60561) or [<span class="fg-survivor">Hunter's Instinct</span>](/card/12074) is widely talked about right now. You can take this fantastical refreshing power to other classes with Survivor access, like [<span class="fg-guardian">Daniela Reyes</span>](/card/12001), who is able to refresh [<span class="fg-guardian">M1911</span>](/card/12019), [<span class="fg-guardian">Somnium Shotcaster</span>](/card/14013), [<span class="fg-guardian">Resilience</span>](/card/12021), or [<span class="fg-guardian">Bodyguard (2)</span>](/card/12027). 

# Exceeding the dreaming slots

This card has the potential to make your board go all dreaming fast, as it can even make regular asset cards dream. So you may be exceeding the limit of 3 dreaming cards more often. It's resolved like a slot conflict.

> Just thought about it. The article says there's a limit of 3 dreaming cards... What happens if you got 3 dreaming cards already and fail a test? Maybe one of them is discarded? Just like other slots

> **Nick (FFG):** This is what happens if you go over your limit. Just like equipment slots.

FFG even answered that you can discard the new one that was just added to the dreaming slots, though the slot rule actually says "another". I'm not sure if this is an exception for the dreaming slots, or if the general slot rule will be adjusted.

{{rule 1.136 "choose and discard another asset or assets under their control to make room"}}

> if you have 3 dreaming cards in play and another card flips an asset face down to be dreaming, which of the 4 cards are eligible to be chosen to be discarded? only the original 3 or the new (4th) one too?

> **Nick (FFG):** All 4 are eligible

# Unique, limit

A dreaming card isn't in play. A unique card or a card with a limit could be in the dreaming state while you have another copy in play. But when it flips faceup and turns out to violate the rule, it can't enter play, and it's discarded. (In normal circumstances, you wouldn't be allowed to play it from your hand.)

> If "limit 1" card or unique card is coming into play due to 0 dream token and it is going to violate the limit or unique rule, you just discard it?

> **Nick (FFG):** Yeah limit 1's and uniques would discard if they cannot enter play
