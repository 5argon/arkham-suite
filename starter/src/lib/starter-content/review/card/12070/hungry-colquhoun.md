It appears 3 XP is the new benchmark for clue compression events outside the Seeker class, with other examples including [Stakeout](/card/60179) and [Right Under Their Noses](/card/60382). For me, I don't actually mind this XP pricing - I think in Chapter 1 clue compression was by and large too easy (e.g. compare to 0 XP [Read the Signs](/card/06117)), so putting a sizeable XP price tag on compression is a nice balancing effect.

Even so, there's plenty of other cards Mystics want, so is this worth it with respect to the wider Chapter 2 <span class="icon-mystic"></span> card pool? To that I say... maybe. For one, it gives you a very good chance to crack a high shroud location by adding <span class="icon-willpower"></span> to <span class="icon-intellect"></span> for the investigation - and once you pass that test then you get two clues off the location. 2 resources doesn't seem too steep for this effect.

The extra "if" effect I see as icing on the cake, and there are some nasty new Hex ([Langour](/card/12193)) and Terror ([Unspeakable Truths](/card/12125)) treacheries in the core it can target (while also able to target relevant treachery weaknesses such as [Pursued](/card/12102)). There are enablers of this in the Mystic kit (e.g. [Premonition](/card/12064), [Sacrificial Doll](/card/60458), [Mask of Silenus](/card/12068)), with Premonition being the lowest effort of the three. I don't think you necessarily need to trigger this effect for Augur to worthy however.

In terms of close competition, there's upgraded [Second Sight](/card/60481). I tend to find this a bit expensive for what it is, as it doesn't net you more clues than basic [Second Sight](/card/12062). The benefit of Augur is it will actually make your Second Sight charges go further, so I'd say it's complementary. For synergy, there is [Ritual Dagger](/card/60475) - but as it shuffles the event back into your deck you need someone who can rattle through their deck quickly to make this work (e.g. [Marie Lambeau](/card/60451)).

**Conclusions:** It's worth consideration on Mystic decks focused on clue-finding, and/or for an event focused deck with [Ritual Dagger](/card/60475). Otherwise, it's probably worth a miss on most decks (and it's not worth it at all on decks using the wider legacy card pool).
