[<span class="fg-rogue">Corsair's Cache</span>](/card/14033) searches for an ***Item*** card, not an ***Item*** asset. Some ***Item*** cards aren't assets, and it can hit those too, like the Guardian events [<span class="fg-guardian">Iron Sights</span>](/card/60163) and [<span class="fg-guardian">Extended Barrel</span>](/card/60172).

Signature assets are often ***Item*** too: [<span class="fg-guardian">Daniela's Wrench</span>](/card/12002), [<span class="fg-survivor">Isabelle's Twin .45s</span>](/card/12014), [<span class="fg-seeker">Experimental Psychology</span>](/card/60252), [<span class="fg-guardian">Becky</span>](/card/60152), [<span class="fg-survivor">Miguel's Knapsack</span>](/card/60552). This could be a decent way to tutor them out for others.

You can also discard ***Item*** weaknesses like [<span class="fg-neutral">The Gold Bug</span>](/card/12098) or [<span class="fg-neutral">The Necronomicon</span>](/card/12012), but I guess that wouldn't happen too often.

I'm not sure how effective it is to make someone with a lot of expensive ***Item*** assets the target of this card. Usually those have a high XP cost, like [<span class="fg-guardian">Thompson Submachine Gun</span>](/card/60182), and you do want it rather than selling it away. Expensive assets that aren't high XP are often allies like [<span class="fg-rogue">Olivier Bishop</span>](/card/12046) or [<span class="fg-mystic">Jim Culver</span>](/card/12060), and those aren't ***Item*** and can't be targeted. Only [<span class="fg-seeker">Fingerprint Kit</span>](/card/12031) and [<span class="fg-seeker">Dreamer's Chronicle</span>](/card/60255) come to mind as expensive ***Item*** assets at Lv. 0.

## [<span class="fg-mystic">Dexter Drake</span>](/card/12010)

The search has no "non-weakness" clause, and Dexter's [<span class="fg-neutral">The Necronomicon</span>](/card/12012) is an ***Item***. So if it's in the top 9 cards, you can find it and discard it, and it's gone from your deck before you ever draw it. It has no printed cost, so you gain no resources for it, but that's not why you'd do it.
