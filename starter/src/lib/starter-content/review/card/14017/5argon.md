A representative card of the Seeker dreaming style, where they can move dream tokens around between dreaming cards. However, this card itself requires Dreaming 3. Is it worth the wait for 3 chances to do that?

A "dreaming card" isn't the same as "a card with the dreaming keyword". Dreaming X is the keyword instructing you to play it as a dreaming card with X resources on it as dream tokens. The dreaming card is the facedown one waiting with its dreams. Once it flips faceup, it's a card in play that happens to have the keyword.

So when [<span class="fg-seeker">Lunar Charts</span>](/card/14017) moves 1 dream "from a dreaming card you control to another dreaming card you control", both cards must be facedown. And of course you can't move a dream to the Lunar Charts you're using, since it's faceup in play.

## [<span class="fg-seeker">Idol of Bokrug</span>](/card/14028)

[<span class="fg-seeker">Idol of Bokrug</span>](/card/14028) has the power to treat a faceup card as if it were still dreaming: dreams may be moved to and from it "as if it were a dreaming card under your control". So it's the one faceup card Lunar Charts can move a dream to or from.
