The only damage / horror cancelling card in the game.

{{rule 1.024}}

Unlike [<span class="fg-guardian">Counterattack</span>](/card/12026) / [<span class="fg-mystic">Blood Ward</span>](/card/60472), the attack still happens, but you remove the consequence. This might matter if [<span class="fg-guardian">Daniela Reyes</span>](/card/12001) is looking to use her <span class="icon-reaction"></span>, which needs the attack to happen.

It works with treachery or story card effects that deal damage and horror. If the treachery has Peril, you have to be the one resolving it to be able to play this card during the skill test.

{{rule 1.109}}

It's reactive, so in a way it could be looked at as a sharable [<span class="fg-mystic">Ward of Protection</span>](/card/12065), where you can let them test, and if they happen to pass and don't get punished with damage / horror, you just keep holding this one in your hand. (Same style as a Survivor holding [<span class="fg-survivor">Timely Intervention</span>](/card/12081) heading into any test.)

It might be useful for dealing with a non-Massive ***Elite*** with a heavy attack and Retaliate or something. In this case, you can take your turn before the fighter and try to set up as much of a play discount as possible, like playing a Dreaming 3 card or adding some more dreams to one, then end your turn at their location. Now they can safely take a Retaliate attack, or the attack in the Enemy Phase.

[<span class="fg-mystic">Veil of Thalarion</span>](/card/14048) cancels "all of that damage and/or horror", but how much "that" is depends on how the damage or horror was dealt. Damage or horror dealt "for each" of something can be one effect or many.

If the amount can be worked out and dealt all at once, like "take 1 horror for each point you fail by", it's a single effect, and Veil of Thalarion cancels all of it.

{{rule 1.070 "it should be resolved (and may be canceled, ignored, or prevented) as a single cumulative effect"}}

If each instance needs its own choice, like "for each point you fail by, you must either lose 1 action or take 1 horror", each one is a separate effect, and Veil of Thalarion cancels just one of them.

{{rule 1.070 "each instance should be resolved as a separate effect"}}

All that said... paying 5 is unreasonable. I say it needs to be no more than 2 to look good. Since dream tokens will keep decreasing every time you begin your turn, the longer you want to hold onto this card to play reactively, the longer you need to keep playing some sort of Dreaming X cards. There seem to be quite a lot of Dreaming 3 cards out there, and if you just played one, it would bring the cost of this down to 2 in the upcoming Mythos Phase. To do more, you'll have to dabble in dream extension, like from the [<span class="fg-mystic">Enchanting Fife</span>](/card/14043). Having more concurrently dreaming cards won't help, since it lets you select only 1 card for the discount.

[<span class="fg-mystic">Sigil of Thalarion</span>](/card/14052) suggests that the goal of having 5 or more dreams on a single card will be feasible with cards in this box. In that case, the cost will be reduced to 0. Sounds like that's the common goal, then.
