Yep, you read that right. This card is equivalent to testing at a skill value higher than any minus in the bag, so the only thing that can fail you is <span class="icon-auto_fail"></span>. Except you didn't invest any commits to reach that skill value, and since it's reactive, you get to keep [<span class="fg-survivor">Scrape By</span>](/card/12082) ready in hand.

Maybe you do need to look at the token to see whether you take horror, but even if the horror were forced 100% of the time, this card would still be super strong.

Recursion is easy when paired with [<span class="fg-survivor">Do-or-Die</span>](/card/60568), or a little more involved with [<span class="fg-survivor">Hunter's Instinct (2)</span>](/card/60574), which costs 2 XP on top (but you can use it right away on the enemy you're engaging). [<span class="fg-survivor">Respite</span>](/card/60577) can also bring all copies back to your deck.

The "you" only restriction and the reactive play bring [<span class="fg-survivor">Timely Intervention</span>](/card/12081) into comparison. The text of [<span class="fg-survivor">Timely Intervention</span>](/card/12081) reads much fairer than [<span class="fg-survivor">Scrape By</span>](/card/12082)'s, but the eventual effect might be closer than you think.

Both Scrape and Timely have silly power, and any future investigator who touches the Survivor class even a little will be able to borrow this stupid power and mix it with their other class access! We already get a taste of it with [<span class="fg-guardian">Daniela Reyes</span>](/card/12001)'s Guardian cards and [<span class="fg-survivor">Isabelle Barnes</span>](/card/12013)'s Mystic cards.

## Timely wins on...

- It is 1 XP cheaper and has no play cost, so it can be in your starting deck. No random horror penalty.
- It can push a success into a bigger success, vs. being playable only if you fail.
- It can push a failure into a smaller failure for fail-tech, vs. only changing it to succeed by 0.
- It can be committed to others' tests normally for <span class="icon-willpower"></span><span class="icon-willpower"></span> or <span class="icon-agility"></span><span class="icon-agility"></span>, vs. only <span class="icon-wild"></span>.

## Scrape By wins on...

- Turning a failure by more than 1 point into a success on <span class="icon-intellect"></span> and <span class="icon-combat"></span> tests.
- Turning a failure by more than 2 points into a success on <span class="icon-willpower"></span> and <span class="icon-agility"></span> tests.
- Sometimes you do want the horror for its benefits.
- Event recursion seems to be easier than skill recursion.
- You can intentionally go into a test with an obviously losing skill value and still win. Though in that case it stops being reactive insurance and plays like a proactive event. (Unless a +1 or <span class="icon-elder_sign"></span> lets you actually win legitimately.)

That said, having both would indeed make you a god of test insurance. I'm just trying to say it is not _that far_ from [<span class="fg-survivor">Timely Intervention</span>](/card/12081) in power level (if you play Standard). It's a second [<span class="fg-survivor">Timely Intervention</span>](/card/12081) with a different card type, widening your ways of recurring it.

It rather eats into the territory of [<span class="fg-survivor">Timely Intervention (3)</span>](/card/60582), which costs more XP and has <span class="icon-wild"></span><span class="icon-wild"></span><span class="icon-wild"></span>, almost equivalent to bringing you back to a success, unless you care about committing to others or about succeed-by effects. Being an upgrade of [<span class="fg-survivor">Timely Intervention</span>](/card/12081), it can't sit beside the Lv. 0 version, unlike [<span class="fg-survivor">Scrape By</span>](/card/12082).

As busted as it sounds, something interesting happens when you actually try to upgrade your deck. When you ask yourself whether you need 2 more "win a test you have no business winning" buttons ASAP for 2 XP, the answer is often no. (While you might say yes more often to upgrading [<span class="fg-rogue">Out the Door</span>](/card/60370) to [<span class="fg-rogue">Out the Door (1)</span>](/card/60373) ASAP.) You want cards that work with your good stats in the deck earlier, so that you are consistently at "only -3, -4 and <span class="icon-auto_fail"></span> fail me". Getting [<span class="fg-survivor">Scrape By</span>](/card/12082) early only removes the "-3, -4" from that for 2 tests, or 2 more tests with [<span class="fg-survivor">Do-or-Die</span>](/card/60568). And [<span class="fg-survivor">Do-or-Die</span>](/card/60568) may be busier with its other tasks than you think.

Finding deck space for this has proven harder than expected as well. When looking to replace the draw-1 neutral skills that were there at the start (e.g. [<span class="fg-guardian">Daniela Reyes</span>](/card/12001) with 2x [<span class="fg-neutral">Guts</span>](/card/12090) + 2x [<span class="fg-neutral">Overpower</span>](/card/12092)), it does make me wonder whether I'm just making the deck slightly worse. Sure, it becomes reactive, but [<span class="fg-guardian">Daniela Reyes</span>](/card/12001) committing [<span class="fg-neutral">Overpower</span>](/card/12092) is as good as "only <span class="icon-auto_fail"></span> fails me", and you get a draw, with no play cost and no horror rebound. So I ended up having to find room for it elsewhere, and that often means it doesn't make the upgrade plan after all!

You can also think of it as a [<span class="fg-mystic">Ward of Protection</span>](/card/12065)-like card for Survivors at 1 XP that sometimes doesn't deal horror, provided that the Revelation comes with a test.

I've heard comments that some might actually "upgrade" from [<span class="fg-survivor">Timely Intervention</span>](/card/12081) into [<span class="fg-survivor">Scrape By</span>](/card/12082), because they think they now have good enough assets to test high and only want to guard against a few very high minuses. Survivors are eating so well that they have to think about whether to remove an already good card!

It's being debated online whether it should be tabooed or forbidden soon. From what I've said here about it being closer to [<span class="fg-survivor">Timely Intervention</span>](/card/12081) than expected, I'm leaning toward "it's very good, but it's fine". They were talking about infinite recursion with legacy cards completely removing all tension from the game, but that's why Chapter 2 is a thing, right? Rather, [<span class="fg-survivor">Scrape By</span>](/card/12082) might make the designers ask whether they should print more cards that bring it back, beyond [<span class="fg-survivor">Hunter's Instinct (2)</span>](/card/60574) and [<span class="fg-survivor">Do-or-Die</span>](/card/60568). (Or just add a clause that removes it from the game.)

# [<span class="fg-survivor">"Look what I found!"</span>](/card/12078)

If you also have [<span class="fg-survivor">"Look what I found!"</span>](/card/12078) in hand and 2 resources, that widens to either 1 or 2 clues. If you fail within the range of [<span class="fg-survivor">"Look what I found!"</span>](/card/12078), you just don't play [<span class="fg-survivor">Scrape By</span>](/card/12082).

Both trigger at the same skill test timing step, ST.6 "Determine success/failure of skill test", but "when" comes before "after". If you choose to play [<span class="fg-survivor">Scrape By</span>](/card/12082) on the "when", its "instead" replaces the triggering condition, so you can no longer play [<span class="fg-survivor">"Look what I found!"</span>](/card/12078). # [<span class="fg-guardian">Daniela Reyes</span>](/card/12001)

Since she has 2 <span class="icon-intellect"></span> and 2 <span class="icon-agility"></span>, using it for 100% miracle evades looks like its best feature. Fighters are good at fighting, but when they can't finish in one round, they take hits. Now Daniela is almost guaranteed to be able to exhaust any kind of boss with 100 <span class="icon-agility"></span> or whatever, and spare her team for the next round.

Daniela can use it with two 2-damage weapons that have no accuracy boost: the <span class="icon-action"></span> on [<span class="fg-guardian">Sledgehammer (3)</span>](/card/12028) or the <span class="icon-reaction"></span> on [<span class="fg-guardian">Thompson Submachine Gun</span>](/card/60182). There it gives better coverage than [<span class="fg-survivor">Timely Intervention</span>](/card/12081). With [<span class="fg-guardian">Winchester Model 12</span>](/card/12029), it only fixes the fail-by friendly fire.

It covers <span class="icon-agility"></span> treachery tests, especially fail-by ones, which [<span class="fg-survivor">Timely Intervention</span>](/card/12081) might not completely nullify.

There are other expensive <span class="icon-guardian"></span> Guardian events that Daniela could totally cheat with [<span class="fg-survivor">Scrape By</span>](/card/12082). [<span class="fg-guardian">"Make 'em sing"</span>](/card/60160) has no non-_**Elite**_ restriction, presumably because a boss enemy was assumed to have 10+ health. Now, with [<span class="fg-survivor">Scrape By</span>](/card/12082), any eldritch abomination will sing: you can auto-evade it without even engaging, and get a clue too! (It's a [<span class="fg-rogue">Breaking and Entering</span>](/card/12050)-esque play, but no one who plays that card can take [<span class="fg-survivor">Scrape By</span>](/card/12082) at the moment.) You can also use [<span class="fg-guardian">Stakeout (3)</span>](/card/60179) to guarantee 2 clues and heal 2 horror at any high-shroud location.

Her 5 sanity makes the horror penalty look slightly scary. Though if you are not replaying it, I think you can ignore that condition.

# [<span class="fg-survivor">Isabelle Barnes</span>](/card/12013)

Her not-as-bad 3 <span class="icon-agility"></span> and 3 <span class="icon-combat"></span>, plus her skill recursion, make [<span class="fg-neutral">Manual Dexterity</span>](/card/12091) and [<span class="fg-neutral">Overpower</span>](/card/12092) harder to replace with [<span class="fg-survivor">Scrape By</span>](/card/12082) in her deck.

However, her skill recursion can also recur [<span class="fg-survivor">Do-or-Die</span>](/card/60568) to revive [<span class="fg-survivor">Scrape By</span>](/card/12082). So it is still a good purchase if you can find the space. With just evergreen products, you've got a Hard / Expert compatible Isabelle who can succeed at any test regularly.

Ensuring maximum damage on [<span class="fg-survivor">Isabelle's Twin .45s</span>](/card/12014) with [<span class="fg-survivor">Timely Intervention</span>](/card/12081) + [<span class="fg-survivor">Scrape By</span>](/card/12082) together can result in a turn of very high damage! You need the first <span class="icon-combat"></span> shot to succeed, which [<span class="fg-survivor">Timely Intervention</span>](/card/12081) isn't good at, and [<span class="fg-survivor">Scrape By</span>](/card/12082) covers it.
