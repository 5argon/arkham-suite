At first it might read like a card to get something out of an unfortunate event: at least you get a clue. However, in this game enemies also "attack" you when you provoke an AoO, when you fail your fight/evade and they Retaliate/Alert, or by other interesting means.

For example, say an enemy engaged you before you could play [<span class="fg-guardian">Machete</span>](/card/12020). Normally you might think about evading it, or punching it to death before you play anything, so as not to take an AoO. But if you have 1 extra resource and even get 1 clue that you otherwise couldn't get, the AoO no longer feels like a drawback and might even be a tempo boost!

If you Move to provoke an AoO, you get attacked at your original location and get a clue at that original location. It might be a good way to move out when there's one clue left to get the victory point.

At the end of any scenario, see how much health and sanity you have left. They could be thought of as unused resources that you could have spent to grab clues.

I like that this card is in the Core Set 2026, because it teaches you that "attack" goes far beyond that one timing in the Enemy Phase.

# [<span class="fg-guardian">Daniela Reyes</span>](/card/12001)

It's easy to see the synergy with [<span class="fg-guardian">Daniela's Wrench</span>](/card/12002), easier than provoking an AoO to get attacked. Sometimes you just don't have anything to do to provoke an AoO, and the wrench can get you the attack you want without spending an action.

In multiplayer, more encounter cards are drawn, so there are more chances of finding enemies. Not only do you get the engagement without spending an action, you can also hang out with your friends in one high shroud location and not have to care who got the enemy.

When Daniela uses <span class="icon-reaction"></span> to fight a Retaliate enemy (perhaps one attacking another investigator), if she fails the fight test she also gets attacked (in addition to the attack hitting your friend).

# [<span class="fg-guardian">Counterattack</span>](/card/12026)

If an attack is canceled, the enemy didn't attack you.
