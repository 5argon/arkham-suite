Very powerful card. You get even more clues than when you win, plus it makes any shroud look reachable, even for someone like [<span class="fg-guardian">Daniela Reyes</span>](/card/12001) with just 2 <span class="icon-intellect"></span>. The play cost of 2 is well worth it. It even has decent <span class="icon-intellect"></span><span class="icon-intellect"></span> icons for regular investigation!

Normally you need around 6 <span class="icon-intellect"></span> to have a go at 4 shroud, and having a go may still need commits to get there, or a valuable [<span class="fg-seeker">Deduction</span>](/card/12039), to get the same amount of clues. If you fail, you might lose a lot of those limited resources. Suddenly, having <span class="icon-intellect"></span> exactly equal to the shroud looks even more appetizing, because you are going to get even more clues than by winning. And since this is a reactive card, you can keep retrying in that general range without losing the [<span class="fg-survivor">"Look what I found!"</span>](/card/12078) in your hand.

It might look like a test insurance card, but in practice it's used more to intentionally snipe the fail range and cheat 2 clues, since you get more out of failing. [<span class="fg-survivor">Timely Intervention</span>](/card/12081) and [<span class="fg-survivor">Scrape By</span>](/card/12082) also come to mind. Both let you hold the "I win" in hand indefinitely until the right moment comes.

# The 2 shroud technique

No matter how high a minus modifier is, your skill value ends up functioning as 0 at minimum when determining failure. So investigating a 2 shroud location ensures that [<span class="fg-survivor">"Look what I found!"</span>](/card/12078) can be played.

{{rule 1.097 "Any time a new modifier is applied" "cannot be reduced so that it functions with a value below zero" "For example: Danny tests"}}

Even if you get an <span class="icon-auto_fail"></span>, you'll be able to play [<span class="fg-survivor">"Look what I found!"</span>](/card/12078) if the shroud is 2 or less. The effect of this token sets your skill value to 0.

{{rule 4.006 "their total skill value for that test is considered to be 0"}}

[<span class="fg-neutral">Hand-Crank Flashlight</span>](/card/12088) / [<span class="fg-survivor">Old Compass</span>](/card/60558) / [<span class="fg-survivor">Old Compass (2)</span>](/card/12083) have an ability to reduce shroud, so you can extend this technique to even 3 or 4 shroud.

# Fail packages

With [<span class="fg-survivor">Old Compass</span>](/card/60558) / [<span class="fg-survivor">Old Compass (2)</span>](/card/12083), not only is the shroud lowered by 1 / 2 for you, you can also fail and have a retry. If you play [<span class="fg-survivor">"Look what I found!"</span>](/card/12078) on the first test where you failed and win on the 2nd try, you'll end up getting 3 clues in only one action. Of course, you can also play [<span class="fg-survivor">"Look what I found!"</span>](/card/12078) on the 2nd try if you failed by too much on the first one. Add [<span class="fg-survivor">Rabbit's Foot</span>](/card/60560) and you'll want to fail then win at all costs.
