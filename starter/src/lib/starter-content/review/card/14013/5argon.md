Fast + Dreaming lets you save a play action and skip AoO when you start dreaming.

If you are planning to play [<span class="fg-guardian">Custom Grip</span>](/card/60162) with it, Fast could be quite handy, so you can do it all even in the mess of being engaged. Then, if you accelerate it with [<span class="fg-guardian">Knight's Longsword</span>](/card/14003) kills and [<span class="fg-guardian">Dream Mastery</span>](/card/14008), you could be summoning this gun again much faster than you think!

For someone with Survivor access like [<span class="fg-guardian">Daniela Reyes</span>](/card/12001), re-dreaming it with [<span class="fg-survivor">Explorer's Sextant</span>](/card/14057), [<span class="fg-survivor">Lash of Ulthar</span>](/card/14067), or [<span class="fg-survivor">Dream Quest</span>](/card/14060) would also reload it and let its <span class="icon-reaction"></span> trigger again on entering play.

The <span class="icon-reaction"></span> that deals 1 damage to an enemy at any location sounds technical, but it's a rarely seen power. (Perhaps analogous to [<span class="fg-mystic">Will of the Cosmos</span>](/card/12066), which can get a clue from far away.) You could intentionally move far away from a pesky Aloof cultist that generates doom and say "you are already dead" while this gun is still dreaming. Or as long as you have a way to accelerate 2 dreams you can already factor the damage ping into your equation in a controlled way. e.g. The [<span class="fg-guardian">Knight's Longsword</span>](/card/14003) + [<span class="fg-guardian">Dream Mastery</span>](/card/14008) kill combo, thanks to Fast on the gun and an engaged enemy to defeat, you can try to get the damage ping now.

## [<span class="fg-guardian">Tommy Muldoon</span>](/card/60151)

Tommy's <span class="icon-action"></span> ability, which plays a ***Firearm*** from your hand with +2 ammo, does work with Dreaming, just "delayed".

> How does Tommy's ability interact with the new Somnium Shotcaster? My first thought is that it just...does not, sadly

> **Nick (FFG):** It does you just gotta remember the +2 ammo

The 2 additional ammo come as it enters play, which for a dreaming card is when it flips faceup. **However**, there's still something really disappointing: Tommy's ability is an <span class="icon-action"></span>, so you basically remove the nifty Fast keyword from this card. Poor Tommy!
