So this is a [<span class="fg-seeker">Working a Hunch</span>](/card/12038) time bomb (with the same <span class="icon-intellect"></span><span class="icon-intellect"></span> icons even), plus **perpetual** shroud reduction to any revealed location! It is quite common for the boss fight location to require you to gather a lot of clues from it to fight the boss itself. I think most of the XP cost is due to this ability to tech a boss fight.

These are the other cards in Chapter 2 so far that lower a location's shroud:

- [<span class="fg-survivor">Old Compass</span>](/card/60558) and [<span class="fg-survivor">Old Compass (2)</span>](/card/12083): -1 shroud for one investigation, or -2 when the level 2 one tries again.
- [<span class="fg-neutral">Hand-Crank Flashlight</span>](/card/12088): discard it after you succeed for -1 shroud until the end of the round.
- [<span class="fg-mystic">Lunar Sight</span>](/card/14054): spend 1 charge for -2 shroud for that test.

[<span class="fg-seeker">Light of Ilarnek</span>](/card/14026) is the only perpetual one. A close second is [<span class="fg-neutral">Hand-Crank Flashlight</span>](/card/12088), which lasts until the end of the round. By stacking shroud reduction as a team, you might be able to reach test difficulty 0 more easily! Just -2 from [<span class="fg-seeker">Light of Ilarnek</span>](/card/14026) can turn 4 shroud into 0 for a [<span class="fg-mystic">Lunar Sight</span>](/card/14054) user.
