Dreaming X is an additional cost, as you might have expected.

{{rule 1.034 "Some cards add additional costs"}}

So [<span class="fg-rogue">Dalkura</span>](/card/14030) is the idea of a Dreamlands Rogue who can skip the dreaming part by paying resources.

> **Reid B. (FFG):** Dreaming X is an alternate cost to play the card, so any time you play a card with dreaming X it goes into dreaming unless you're explicitly told to ignore that cost

{{rule 1.079 "If a cost is ignored, it is no longer required to be paid"}}

The wording is wider than "ignore the Dreaming X keyword", so we might have to be on the lookout for Dreaming X cards with other expensive additional costs to play, which [<span class="fg-rogue">Dalkura</span>](/card/14030) would also ignore. They'd begin with something like "...as an additional cost to play". (Note that it needs the Dreaming X keyword, so it can't target something like [<span class="fg-seeker">Insidious Truths</span>](/card/60266), which has a valid additional cost but no dreaming.)

There are dreaming cards with Fast on them. If you use the <span class="icon-action"></span> on [<span class="fg-rogue">Dalkura</span>](/card/14030) to play one, you effectively remove the advantage of Fast.
