[<span class="fg-neutral">Aziza Steer</span>](/card/73059) adds a fixed bonus to common traits on every event in the game going forward, and it covers 3 traits at once!

Her <span class="icon-reaction"></span> only works during your turn, so events with more generous Fast timings might miss out on the benefit. That's a small restriction, though. "After you play" waits until the event has fully resolved. 

{{rule 1.008 "fully resolved"}}

I think the 3/3 soak looks a bit too generous. 2/2 or 1/2 would still look good to me for 3 XP with this effect. And it's neutral! This is a 3/3 neutral soak that could potentially fix anyone's health/sanity imbalance.

By the way, Reward cards are always in the Current Environment regardless of release date. So Aziza is here to stay forever. Wouldn't this give the devs a hard time designing traits on event cards? Aziza is neutral, so she affects event designs in every class. I mean, a new ***Trick*** card might cost a little more than expected, because there'll always be a "but" with Aziza. If a new ***Trick*** costs zero to play, it becomes an economy card with Aziza. It sounds both exciting and worrying, and I wonder whether the devs will manage to handle it.

{{rule 12.001 "Reward cards may be used regardless of release date"}}

# Events with 2 of the traits

I've provided a BuildQL preset above that finds events with more than one of the supported traits, so you can check back after each release to see whether this list grows.

The events in the André Patel pack that felt underwhelming look tons better with Aziza. I wonder if they were designed with "fear" of Aziza in the first place? Since they cost exactly 2 to play, ***Trick*** gives you back the play cost, in addition to the extra ***Tactic*** damage.

- [<span class="fg-rogue">A Sudden Fall</span>](/card/60367) / [<span class="fg-rogue">A Sudden Fall (2)</span>](/card/60378) : The Lv. 2 version now deals 4 damage! However, if you evade the enemy yourself and then spend another action on this card, an equivalent fighter attacking 2 times could easily deal 4 damage too. So it's better to put Aziza's damage on another enemy, to make the flexibility worth it.
- [<span class="fg-rogue">Clean Sweep</span>](/card/60364) / [<span class="fg-rogue">Clean Sweep (2)</span>](/card/60376) : If you're able to investigate, the enemy that takes Aziza's ***Tactic*** damage must be engaged with a friend, or Aloof.
- [<span class="fg-rogue">Quick Exit</span>](/card/60366) / [<span class="fg-rogue">Quick Exit (2)</span>](/card/60377) : Aziza's ***Tactic*** damage on this one feels like what should have been printed on the card in the first place. The ***Trick*** refund is nice, but [<span class="fg-survivor">Slippery</span>](/card/12080) can already do the same thing at zero cost!

There's also [<span class="fg-seeker">Through the Cracks</span>](/card/12037) / [<span class="fg-seeker">Through the Cracks (2)</span>](/card/12041) which refunds you with ***Trick*** and gets you a clue with ***Insight***. It turns the unappetizingly expensive Lv. 0 version into quite a practical card if you manage to pair it with Aziza.
