I got a ruling from FFG that all the "after you play..." <span class="icon-reaction"></span> abilities trigger when you play the card into the dreaming state, not when it flips faceup after dreaming (e.g. [<span class="fg-survivor">Miguel's Knapsack</span>](/card/60552), [<span class="fg-mystic">Dexter Drake</span>](/card/12010), [<span class="fg-neutral">Aziza Steer</span>](/card/73059)). Only a <span class="icon-reaction"></span> on the dreaming card that references itself would trigger at the moment it flips faceup.

> would any "after you play an event" reactions proc from playing a dreaming event? let's say if a Dreaming X event had the Tactic/Trick/Insight traits ...

> **Nick (FFG):** "After you play X" abilities proc when you play the Dreaming card facedown.
>
> If a Dreaming card has a self-referential "after you play this card," it procs when it flips faceup. (I don't think any dreaming cards in the box say "after you play this card," but you can give Dreaming to other cards, so we had to account for it)

Then, one could still question whether the traits exist in the facedown state for [<span class="fg-neutral">Aziza Steer</span>](/card/73059) to "see". FFG answered that there's a moment of playing, before the card goes facedown, when the traits are available.

> all right, so a certain ally detective can see the traits on a facedown dreaming event, yes?

> **Nick (FFG):** Technically she sees them before they go facedown, but yes

So in summary, [<span class="fg-neutral">Aziza Steer</span>](/card/73059)'s bonus is given when you start dreaming a Dreaming X event with the appropriate traits. But none of the dreaming events revealed so far has those traits. [<span class="fg-seeker">Light of Ilarnek</span>](/card/14026) is a ***Ritual***, [<span class="fg-rogue">Corsair's Cache</span>](/card/14033) is ***Supply*** and ***Illicit***, and [<span class="fg-survivor">Dreams of Peace</span>](/card/14061) is a ***Spirit***. [<span class="fg-neutral">Call of the Dreamlands</span>](/card/14071) is usable with [<span class="fg-neutral">Aziza Steer</span>](/card/73059), but it is not a Dreaming X card. [<span class="fg-seeker">Oneiric Insight</span>](/card/14021) can only dream-ify an asset.
