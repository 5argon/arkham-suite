This idol is supposedly the centerpiece that removes the clunkiness of having 2 or more concurrently dreaming cards in order to play the cards that move dream tokens, like [<span class="fg-seeker">Lunar Charts</span>](/card/14017). So with this idol as the target of the move, you get only the dream acceleration and not the delay. You can go the other way as well, moving dreams from this idol to dreaming cards to delay them.

To empty the idol, you'd need a card with uses (secrets) as the target of its <span class="icon-action"></span>. Otherwise, you are stuck forever with a full 5-dream idol!

The middle ability of [<span class="fg-seeker">Idol of Bokrug</span>](/card/14028) is **Forced**, and Forced is forced. After each of your turns begins, you must move 1 dream from a dreaming card you control to the Idol, as long as the Idol has fewer than 5 dreams and you control a dreaming card. This is dream acceleration, but for something like [<span class="fg-seeker">Light of Ilarnek</span>](/card/14026), you might have to calculate more carefully when it'd finish dreaming.

{{rule 1.069 "trigger each time its specified timing point is met"}}

# Only assets that use secrets can receive them

The Idol's last ability moves its dreams to an asset you control **with uses (secrets)**, turning them into secrets. You cannot put them on an asset that uses charges, ammo, or supplies, or on an asset with no uses type defined. The ability says so explicitly with "with uses (secrets)" here, but even if it didn't, the rule still forbids placing secrets on assets unrelated to secrets.

{{rule 1.153 "Only the type of uses defined by a card can be placed on that card"}}
