A thought-provoking card about probability.

Will you include 2x of what you want, as well as 1~2x of [<span class="fg-guardian">Right Tool for the Job</span>](/card/12023)? Or would you rather have real alternatives that are playable right away once drawn? As a basic example, say you want to find weapons playing [<span class="fg-guardian">Daniela Reyes</span>](/card/12001). Without [<span class="fg-seeker">Joe Diamond</span>](/card/12004) or [<span class="fg-guardian">Tommy Muldoon</span>](/card/60151) taking the cards, you can run 2x [<span class="fg-guardian">M1911</span>](/card/60155), 2x [<span class="fg-guardian">Service Revolver</span>](/card/60158), 2x [<span class="fg-guardian">Machete</span>](/card/12020) and so on. But if you don't like the limitations of [<span class="fg-guardian">Service Revolver</span>](/card/60158) and [<span class="fg-guardian">Machete</span>](/card/12020), will you go for 2x [<span class="fg-guardian">M1911</span>](/card/60155) + 2x [<span class="fg-guardian">Right Tool for the Job</span>](/card/12023), so it feels as if you have 4 of the reliable guns in the deck? Or will you slot in 2x [<span class="fg-neutral">Broken Bottle</span>](/card/12086) at the same play cost, and use it until [<span class="fg-guardian">M1911</span>](/card/60155) arrives and then switch to it?

There's also ammo to think about when it comes to doubling up a purchase. Getting 2x [<span class="fg-guardian">M1911 (2)</span>](/card/60171) sounds good, because playing the 2nd copy also gets you that much more ammo. A single unlimited-use weapon like [<span class="fg-guardian">Sledgehammer (3)</span>](/card/12028), which you are done looking for once found, might open up an optimization in XP spending: you purchase just one and leave the task of finding it to 2x [<span class="fg-guardian">Right Tool for the Job</span>](/card/12023), so you can spend 3 XP on something else.

Sometimes you just don't have the choice of doubling something up. Getting 2x [<span class="fg-guardian">Winchester Model 12</span>](/card/12029) or 2x [<span class="fg-guardian">Thompson Submachine Gun</span>](/card/60182) will drain half of your campaign XP. In this case, [<span class="fg-guardian">Right Tool for the Job</span>](/card/12023) waiting in the starting deck to find your 1x high XP card can be a good plan.

Planning for it to flex between both ***Weapon*** and ***Tool*** is another consideration. [<span class="fg-guardian">Daniela Reyes</span>](/card/12001) doesn't have as many options as [<span class="fg-seeker">Joe Diamond</span>](/card/12004) when it comes to that.

When you look at search options in other classes, like [<span class="fg-seeker">Mysterious Grimoire</span>](/card/12040) or [<span class="fg-mystic">Arcane Initiate</span>](/card/60455), a range of 9 cards is high. This being in the Core means that Guardians have the power to get singles of ***Tool*** and ***Weapon*** and flex between a variety of them, rather than the typical doubling up for consistency to do just one thing.

A small gotcha: it gives the vibe of getting you a game-progressing asset, because most of those are either ***Weapon*** or ***Tool***, but there are some that are simply ***Item***, like [<span class="fg-seeker">Local Map</span>](/card/12033) and [<span class="fg-seeker">Dreamer's Chronicle</span>](/card/60255), that could also progress the game.

# [<span class="fg-guardian">Daniela Reyes</span>](/card/12001)

Only [<span class="fg-survivor">Old Compass</span>](/card/60558), [<span class="fg-survivor">Old Compass (2)</span>](/card/12083), and [<span class="fg-neutral">Hand-Crank Flashlight</span>](/card/12088) would be ***Tool*** that's not also ***Weapon***. Still more interesting than [<span class="fg-guardian">Tommy Muldoon</span>](/card/60151) with just [<span class="fg-neutral">Hand-Crank Flashlight</span>](/card/12088).

Her 2 <span class="icon-intellect"></span> is not nothing, however. You can play around with this! Impress your friend with the fact that 2 is more than 1 rather than ignore it. The best thing for 2 <span class="icon-intellect"></span> would be her <span class="icon-survivor"></span> Survivor access that brings in [<span class="fg-survivor">"Look what I found!"</span>](/card/12078) and [<span class="fg-survivor">Timely Intervention</span>](/card/12081) failure adjustments. Combined with replays from [<span class="fg-survivor">Hunter's Instinct</span>](/card/12074) (or [<span class="fg-survivor">Hunter's Instinct (2)</span>](/card/60574) if you got juicier things to replay), and you might have a really cool build where [<span class="fg-guardian">Right Tool for the Job</span>](/card/12023) pulls an unlikely secret trick. Not to mention [<span class="fg-survivor">Scrape By</span>](/card/12082) to completely bypass the test if needed, and so you can surely blow up [<span class="fg-neutral">Hand-Crank Flashlight</span>](/card/12088) for your friend to take over the job.

Whenever I add [<span class="fg-guardian">Right Tool for the Job</span>](/card/12023) to Daniela for weapon consistency, I always think about as well hiding little [<span class="fg-neutral">Hand-Crank Flashlight</span>](/card/12088) and its related supporting Survivor cards, and it's also thematically sound that she would have other machine fixing tools other than her wrench.

# [<span class="fg-seeker">Joe Diamond</span>](/card/12004)

This card points to Joe's Guardian access and you'd imagine Seeker would have a lot more not-***Weapon*** ***Tool*** cards to impress. Turns out, you have only [<span class="fg-seeker">Magnifying Glass</span>](/card/12034), [<span class="fg-seeker">Magnifying Glass (1)</span>](/card/60270), [<span class="fg-seeker">Fingerprint Kit</span>](/card/12031), and [<span class="fg-seeker">Typewriter</span>](/card/60272).

Basic build of Joe with [<span class="fg-guardian">Right Tool for the Job</span>](/card/12023) (for finding weapons which might be fewer than real Guardians) so he could accidentally hit other interesting things as well, might be going for 2x [<span class="fg-seeker">Magnifying Glass</span>](/card/12034) + 2x [<span class="fg-neutral">Hand-Crank Flashlight</span>](/card/12088) for ***Tool*** density.

The [<span class="fg-seeker">Fingerprint Kit</span>](/card/12031) I found very interesting, however. This card is an expensive and slow "Seeker gun" that exhausts each time, that I found some in the community said it's good but not their style. With [<span class="fg-guardian">Right Tool for the Job</span>](/card/12023), you might be able to strike the balance by having just 1x [<span class="fg-seeker">Fingerprint Kit</span>](/card/12031) so it doesn't randomly come when you are broke, but come with you explicitly call for it with [<span class="fg-guardian">Right Tool for the Job</span>](/card/12023). You do need to pay 4 on top of 1 to play it, though.
