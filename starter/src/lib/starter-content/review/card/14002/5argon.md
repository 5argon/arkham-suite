[<span class="fg-guardian">Knight of Celephaïs</span>](/card/14002) is similar to [<span class="fg-guardian">Bodyguard (2)</span>](/card/12027), which is a leveled card played at 3 cost. (You can compare that with Dreaming 4 here however you want.)

I've always had trouble deciding whether to play assets like [<span class="fg-guardian">Resilience</span>](/card/12021) or [<span class="fg-guardian">Bodyguard (2)</span>](/card/12027) proactively if my resources would be down to zero or really low from that play. Sometimes it felt like I need to have more resources than their play cost to finally want to play them for the just-in-cases.

It seems like this Dreaming X feature, where you have to wait but it won't eat into your resources, would remedy some of the pain points I'm having. At least I only have to worry about the play action, and then I can forget about it for a while. I think there's something inherently fun in doing that, like the feeling when you plant a seed before the rain. It just feels effortless, and you are excited for the result.

Without [<span class="fg-neutral">Charisma</span>](/card/12095), you might be compelled to wait for it with an empty ally slot, which is an opportunity cost when you'd usually go with a perpetual ally like [<span class="fg-guardian">Logan Hastings</span>](/card/12018). Given that the <span class="icon-reaction"></span> that accelerates the dream can be used with the other copy of itself, I think [<span class="fg-neutral">Charisma</span>](/card/12095) will be an important piece, so you don't have to worry about when the conveyor belt of allies will randomly kill off an existing ally. [<span class="fg-neutral">Charisma</span>](/card/12095) would also allow stacking 2x [<span class="fg-guardian">Knight of Celephaïs</span>](/card/14002).

Unlike [<span class="fg-guardian">Knight's Longsword</span>](/card/14003), this guy's commit icons suck. (Not even a <span class="icon-willpower"></span> for treachery defense!) So getting it late will be less useful, as you are running out of time to dream.

This might be an unpopular opinion, but I kinda like this guy. (By the eye test, at least. I might change my mind once I actually get to play it.) Yes, that Dreaming 4 sticks out. But when I see it, my brain goes "I can play the equivalent of a 4-cost asset for free!" and wants to have a go at that. Part of that is because I've played with [<span class="fg-guardian">Bodyguard (2)</span>](/card/12027), and I know how convenient it is to be able to absorb all kinds of treachery failures or enemy attacks with 3/2, and now I could have that for free? Sign me up! The defeat <span class="icon-reaction"></span> to deal 2 damage was quite hard to line up, so I have no problem with it being a dream acceleration on [<span class="fg-guardian">Knight of Celephaïs</span>](/card/14002) instead.
