Such a simple and powerful reward card, but the true skill ceiling is in the subtly different timing and condition of its <span class="icon-reaction"></span> trigger. It's best compared with [<span class="fg-rogue">Sticky Fingers</span>](/card/12048), which has a similar condition. You successfully evade an enemy in ST.6. You evade the enemy as a consequence of the test in ST.7. Immediately after the evade resolves in ST.7, you can trigger the <span class="icon-reaction"></span> on [<span class="fg-rogue">Covert Ops</span>](/card/12008).

{{rule 1.059 "during Step 7 of the skill test"}}

Let's compare it with the majority of other success / failure cards in this game (even ones conditioned on evading) : [<span class="fg-rogue">Lucky Cigarette Case</span>](/card/12044), [<span class="fg-rogue">Sticky Fingers</span>](/card/12048), [<span class="fg-survivor">Rabbit's Foot</span>](/card/60560), [<span class="fg-survivor">"Look what I found!"</span>](/card/12078), [<span class="fg-seeker">Dorothy Simmons</span>](/card/12030), [<span class="fg-rogue">Right Under Their Noses</span>](/card/60368), etc. These all trigger within ST.6, where you determine success / failure, in its "after" layer. (The "after" of any step is in fact within that step, but grouped to come last before moving on.)

{{rule 1.150 "The word \"after\""}}

This matters for cards that add (automatic) evasion as a consequence, like [<span class="fg-rogue">Breaking and Entering</span>](/card/12050) and [<span class="fg-rogue">Polished Cane</span>](/card/60363). (Other examples that add consequences are the majority of skill cards in the game: [<span class="fg-guardian">Vicious Blow</span>](/card/12025), [<span class="fg-seeker">Deduction</span>](/card/12039), [<span class="fg-survivor">Slippery</span>](/card/12080), [<span class="fg-survivor">On the Brink</span>](/card/60569), [<span class="fg-seeker">Literary Analysis</span>](/card/60269), etc. Some non-Rogue assets that add consequences : [<span class="fg-survivor">Pocketknife</span>](/card/60559), [<span class="fg-survivor">Old Compass</span>](/card/60558).) Those additional automatic evade consequences don't match the ST.6 condition of [<span class="fg-rogue">Sticky Fingers</span>](/card/12048), but they do match [<span class="fg-rogue">Covert Ops</span>](/card/12008). [<span class="fg-rogue">Breaking and Entering</span>](/card/12050) in particular adds 4 + 4 = 8 <span class="icon-intellect"></span> and looks set to be one of Trish's staples for a long time to come, thanks to this synergy with [<span class="fg-rogue">Covert Ops</span>](/card/12008).

# Exercise!

This is a complex example that helps you run through the steps correctly. Suppose that Trish has 1 resource, with [<span class="fg-rogue">Sticky Fingers</span>](/card/12048), [<span class="fg-rogue">Covert Ops</span>](/card/12008), and [<span class="fg-rogue">Lucky Cigarette Case</span>](/card/12044) in play, all ready (her full "board"). She evades a wimpy 1 <span class="icon-agility"></span> enemy with [<span class="fg-neutral">Manual Dexterity</span>](/card/12091) committed, which adds a consequence of drawing 1 card in ST.7. [<span class="fg-mystic">Dexter Drake</span>](/card/12010) on the same location commits [<span class="fg-rogue">Out of Sight</span>](/card/12053), which adds another consequence in ST.7 if she succeeds by 2. She succeeds by a lot.

ST.6 / "after" : Trigger the <span class="icon-reaction"></span> on [<span class="fg-rogue">Sticky Fingers</span>](/card/12048) to get a resource. Then, trigger the <span class="icon-reaction"></span> on [<span class="fg-rogue">Lucky Cigarette Case</span>](/card/12044) to draw a card. She drew [<span class="fg-rogue">Right Under Their Noses (3)</span>](/card/60382). She can play this card right away if she has 2 resources, because it's the same ST.6 "after". Fortunately, [<span class="fg-rogue">Sticky Fingers</span>](/card/12048) just brought her up to the 2 resources she needed to play it.
 - The evade still has to resolve in ST.7 before Trish can trigger [<span class="fg-rogue">Covert Ops</span>](/card/12008) to move. By then, the ST.6 play window for [<span class="fg-rogue">Right Under Their Noses (3)</span>](/card/60382) has passed, so the move can't change which clues she can reach with the event.
 - Despite the similar "succeed by" of [<span class="fg-rogue">Out of Sight</span>](/card/12053) and [<span class="fg-rogue">Lucky Cigarette Case</span>](/card/12044), the skill card adds its effect as a conditional consequence in ST.7, not in ST.6 where you <span class="icon-reaction"></span> [<span class="fg-rogue">Lucky Cigarette Case</span>](/card/12044).

**ST.7.** Trish can resolve these three consequences in any order.

- Draw a card from [<span class="fg-neutral">Manual Dexterity</span>](/card/12091).
- Evade the original enemy, exhausting it and breaking its engagement.
  - Immediately after that evade, Trish can trigger the <span class="icon-reaction"></span> on [<span class="fg-rogue">Covert Ops</span>](/card/12008) to draw or move before she resolves another consequence.
- Disengage and move with [<span class="fg-rogue">Out of Sight</span>](/card/12053).

{{rule 4.007 "order of their choice"}}

If you draw with [<span class="fg-neutral">Manual Dexterity</span>](/card/12091) first and then resolve the evade, you can use what you saw to decide whether to draw or move with [<span class="fg-rogue">Covert Ops</span>](/card/12008). If you resolve the evade first, you decide whether to trigger its reaction before seeing what [<span class="fg-neutral">Manual Dexterity</span>](/card/12091) draws.

Suppose you draw with [<span class="fg-neutral">Manual Dexterity</span>](/card/12091) first and get another copy of [<span class="fg-rogue">Right Under Their Noses (3)</span>](/card/60382). Its ST.6 play window has already passed.

- Trish resolves the evade, then triggers [<span class="fg-rogue">Covert Ops</span>](/card/12008) to move. She can move again with [<span class="fg-rogue">Out of Sight</span>](/card/12053), even with no enemy left to disengage from.
- Trish resolves the evade, then triggers [<span class="fg-rogue">Covert Ops</span>](/card/12008) to draw. If she draws [<span class="fg-neutral">Black Chamber Operative</span>](/card/12009), it spawns engaged with her. She can then disengage from it and move with [<span class="fg-rogue">Out of Sight</span>](/card/12053).

Suppose [<span class="fg-neutral">Manual Dexterity</span>](/card/12091) draws [<span class="fg-neutral">Black Chamber Operative</span>](/card/12009) before Trish resolves the evade. The Operative spawns engaged with her.

- Trish resolves the evade against the original enemy, then triggers [<span class="fg-rogue">Covert Ops</span>](/card/12008) to move to the team's fighter. The engaged Operative moves with her. Then she can disengage and move with [<span class="fg-rogue">Out of Sight</span>](/card/12053), leaving the Operative at the fighter's location.
- Trish resolves the evade against the original enemy, then triggers [<span class="fg-rogue">Covert Ops</span>](/card/12008) to draw. If she draws [<span class="fg-seeker">Caustic Reaction (2)</span>](/card/60273), she can say no to the "may" on [<span class="fg-rogue">Out of Sight</span>](/card/12053) and stand her ground. She'll need 2 more resources to play the event.
