By basic action economy, a play cost of 1 plus the play action is equivalent to 2 resources. So you kind of have to make sure you'll evade more than 3 times to make up for it. That's usually the case if it's drawn early, so it's a good card to keep in your mulligan, along with other working cards like [<span class="fg-rogue">Thieves' Kit</span>](/card/12049).

It also works with evasion assets like [<span class="fg-mystic">Shadowmeld</span>](/card/60461), or evasion events like [<span class="fg-rogue">Quick Exit</span>](/card/60366), [<span class="fg-seeker">Unflappable</span>](/card/60262) or [<span class="fg-survivor">Guerrilla Tactics</span>](/card/60564).

It requires proactive setup, because if you are already engaged with an enemy, you can't play it without taking an AoO.

"Limit 1 per investigator" is about the card in play, not in your deck. They just don't want you to <span class="icon-reaction"></span> for 2 resources with 2 copies in play.

# Sequencing in ST.6

Players can order anything within the "after" layer of ST.6 as they wish. So you can always get a missing resource from the <span class="icon-reaction"></span> on [<span class="fg-rogue">Sticky Fingers</span>](/card/12048) first, then play Fast events like [<span class="fg-rogue">Right Under Their Noses</span>](/card/60368) / [<span class="fg-rogue">Right Under Their Noses (3)</span>](/card/60382), which also work off a successful evasion with the exact same timing wording.

If you succeed by 2 and also have [<span class="fg-rogue">Lucky Cigarette Case</span>](/card/12044), you might find better things to spend your extra resource on that you didn't see at first. It mostly doesn't matter when you <span class="icon-reaction"></span> to collect 1 resource, unless you have [<span class="fg-neutral">Paranoia</span>](/card/12101), in which case you might want to use draw effects before taking the resource.

# Automatic evades in ST.7

Some cards, like [<span class="fg-rogue">Polished Cane</span>](/card/60363) or [<span class="fg-rogue">Breaking and Entering</span>](/card/12050), add an **evasion consequence** in ST.7, in the same wording style as skill cards like [<span class="fg-guardian">Vicious Blow</span>](/card/12025), [<span class="fg-seeker">Deduction</span>](/card/12039) and [<span class="fg-survivor">Slippery</span>](/card/12080). Since [<span class="fg-rogue">Sticky Fingers</span>](/card/12048)'s <span class="icon-reaction"></span> triggers in ST.6 when you successfully evade, you can't use it with the bonus consequence of [<span class="fg-rogue">Polished Cane</span>](/card/60363) and [<span class="fg-rogue">Breaking and Entering</span>](/card/12050). ([<span class="fg-rogue">Covert Ops</span>](/card/12008) can, though, so compare the two carefully.) With [<span class="fg-rogue">Polished Cane</span>](/card/60363), you successfully **Fight** in ST.6. With [<span class="fg-rogue">Breaking and Entering</span>](/card/12050), you successfully **Investigate** in ST.6.
