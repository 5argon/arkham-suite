Interesting ally. You would always have to compare any new Mystic ally with [<span class="fg-mystic">Jim Culver</span>](/card/12060) and [<span class="fg-mystic">Arcane Initiate</span>](/card/60455) in the evergreen pool. After waiting out Dreaming 3, as long as you've got ***Spell*** tests and 2 resources to feed him, he'll keep giving his care package to you.

Replenish cannot add charges over its uses. [<span class="fg-mystic">Grand-Mère's Charm</span>](/card/60452) also comes with the same mechanic, but it isn't limited to your asset. Since you also get the heal, resource, and charge replenish when he comes into play from Dreaming 3 the first time, you'd want to spend some charges without him before then. Otherwise, the replenish will do nothing.

{{rule 1.121 "A card with uses can have its uses replenished"}}

The charge, of course, can be used to perpetually send him into his gate box again and again. If the spell is high XP, like [<span class="fg-mystic">Cosmic Flame (5)</span>](/card/12071), this charge refill will be all the more powerful. The damage heal can be used with [<span class="fg-mystic">Blood Curse</span>](/card/60467) and other damage-cost stuff in the Marie Lambeau box. And the +2 skill value on the test might help him compete with the passive +1 <span class="icon-willpower"></span> on [<span class="fg-mystic">Jim Culver</span>](/card/12060).

As for the Dreaming 3, I see that it might create an interesting rhythm with [<span class="fg-mystic">Arcane Initiate</span>](/card/60455), where you can find your spells while waiting, and then [<span class="fg-mystic">Luke Robinson</span>](/card/14045) coming in would bump [<span class="fg-mystic">Arcane Initiate</span>](/card/60455) out along with the doom.

"Skill test on a ***Spell*** card" is worth monitoring for weird strats in the future, I think!

- If there is any ***Spell*** that could initiate a test outside of your turn, it could accelerate dreaming, since one dream token is removed at the start of your turn. (Compared to sending Luke to sleep during your turn as normal, which means you won't have him on your next turn.)
- It doesn't say it has to be your ***Spell*** card. Say you have another Mystic on the team regularly testing their ***Spell***, a flex [<span class="fg-survivor">Isabelle Barnes</span>](/card/12013) using [<span class="fg-mystic">Consume Life</span>](/card/60462), or any Seeker using [<span class="fg-seeker">Commune with the Cosmos</span>](/card/60282). You can send him to dreaming too. Though the +2 would be of no use to you, since it's not your test. But it can be used to accelerate dreaming by having those tests occur before you begin your turn, I guess? You also don't have to invest your own charges to test on that ***Spell*** asset. (You still need to place 2 resources, however.)

# Unique violation entering play from dreaming

It is possible to rotate 2x [<span class="fg-mystic">Luke Robinson</span>](/card/14045) with careful timing so the two never meet and violate the unique card rule. If one copy is coming out of dreaming while another is already in play, we do have a ruling that the one coming in later will simply get discarded.

{{rule 1.151 "There can be no more than one copy of each unique card (by title) in play at any given time"}}
