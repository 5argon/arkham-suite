Event recursion from the discard pile back to hand overlaps with [<span class="fg-survivor">Hunter's Instinct</span>](/card/12074) / [<span class="fg-survivor">Hunter's Instinct (2)</span>](/card/60574), whose events don't need to be <span class="icon-survivor"></span> Survivor, but they are level-gated and need you to engage an enemy. Meanwhile, [<span class="fg-survivor">Do-or-Die</span>](/card/60568)'s condition is succeeding at a test that isn't <span class="icon-combat"></span>. For Survivor-access investigators other than [<span class="fg-survivor">Miguel de la Cruz</span>](/card/60551), I think a lot of the interesting recursion is of non-<span class="icon-survivor"></span> Survivor cards, such as [<span class="fg-guardian">Daniela Reyes</span>](/card/12001) replaying [<span class="fg-guardian">Scene of the Crime</span>](/card/12024), or [<span class="fg-survivor">Isabelle Barnes</span>](/card/12013) replaying [<span class="fg-mystic">Will of the Cosmos</span>](/card/12066) / [<span class="fg-mystic">Ward of Protection</span>](/card/12065). So this class restriction might be more limiting than you think.

Asset recursion from the discard pile back to hand overlaps with [<span class="fg-survivor">Jumpsuit</span>](/card/12075), which can return assets from classes other than <span class="icon-survivor"></span> Survivor, but only ***Tool*** or ***Weapon***, and has a more on-demand trigger.

Skill recursion is gated behind the 2 XP of [<span class="fg-survivor">Respite</span>](/card/60577), and it goes back to your deck, not your hand. [<span class="fg-survivor">Isabelle Barnes</span>](/card/12013) has the exclusive power to commit from the discard pile before the card goes back to the deck. Though [<span class="fg-survivor">Do-or-Die</span>](/card/60568) can recur [<span class="fg-survivor">Respite</span>](/card/60577) itself, which has pretty good <span class="icon-wild"></span><span class="icon-wild"></span> icons, so it is as if you had more copies of [<span class="fg-neutral">Unexpected Courage</span>](/card/12094).

There's no recursion of skill cards directly back to hand, unless you count asset or event cards with good skill icons. The BuildQL presets above may help you find such interesting targets. I've run 2x [<span class="fg-survivor">Meat Cleaver (3)</span>](/card/12085) for consistency before, despite it being a weapon with unlimited uses. Since its icons can commit for <span class="icon-willpower"></span><span class="icon-willpower"></span>, you can turn any [<span class="fg-survivor">Do-or-Die</span>](/card/60568) success into an extra defense against treacheries. It's the lack of level gating that allows high-XP assets with generous icons to be brought back for commits.

# [<span class="fg-survivor">Isabelle Barnes</span>](/card/12013)

As with [<span class="fg-survivor">Jumpsuit</span>](/card/12075), this is essentially a 6-ammo refill for [<span class="fg-survivor">Isabelle's Twin .45s</span>](/card/12014), provided that you have something else to knock the two-handed gun into the discard pile. (For example, if you have nothing but Arcane slot assets, you can't make [<span class="fg-survivor">Isabelle's Twin .45s</span>](/card/12014) leave play!)

The <span class="icon-willpower"></span> icon works rather well if she wants to play <span class="icon-willpower"></span> cards like [<span class="fg-mystic">Cosmic Flame</span>](/card/12059), [<span class="fg-mystic">Second Sight</span>](/card/12062), or even [<span class="fg-mystic">Consume Life</span>](/card/60462).

# [<span class="fg-survivor">Miguel de la Cruz</span>](/card/60551)

Since he can play events with his extra action, whenever you hold [<span class="fg-survivor">Do-or-Die</span>](/card/60568) you get another chance not to waste that action, by reviving a non-Fast event before your turn ends. Reviving zero-cost events like [<span class="fg-neutral">Emergency Cache</span>](/card/12089) would have been really good, but [<span class="fg-survivor">Do-or-Die</span>](/card/60568) rules that out with its <span class="icon-survivor"></span> Survivor restriction. The devs are smart, huh?
