A fun teamplay asset. Reactive healing cards are rare and fun to play. When you have it in hand and things are still quiet, deciding whether you need to set it up quickly brings both anxiety and excitement, because if the damage comes before you can play it, you won't be able to heal it.

It's not just enemies. Treacheries deal damage too. It's not uncommon to hear a [<span class="fg-survivor">Bandages</span>](/card/12073) user say "come end your turn here", so the uncertainty of the upcoming Mythos Phase can be softened.

It has no exhaust clause, so another slightly unconventional use might involve taking AoOs repeatedly during your turn, like when you need to Move and drag along an enemy that deals only damage.

Because the target has to take the damage first before you heal it back with a <span class="icon-reaction"></span>, the target can't be hanging on at critical health. If the target is an ally, it must have 2 or more health remaining so it isn't defeated first.

These are interesting ***Ally*** cards to play it with:

- [<span class="fg-guardian">Bodyguard</span>](/card/12016) / [<span class="fg-guardian">Rookie Cop</span>](/card/60157) / [<span class="fg-guardian">Bodyguard (2)</span>](/card/12027) / [<span class="fg-guardian">Detective Sherman</span>](/card/60177): The investigator using Bodyguard, the investigator taking the hit that Bodyguard absorbs, and the investigator with [<span class="fg-survivor">Bandages</span>](/card/12073) need not be the same investigator. This is my favorite simple combo. If [<span class="fg-guardian">Bodyguard (2)</span>](/card/12027) is tanking 2 damage out of its 3 health, having 2x [<span class="fg-survivor">Bandages</span>](/card/12073) to <span class="icon-reaction"></span> on each one will heal all the damage taken. (But that is rare.) [<span class="fg-guardian">Detective Sherman</span>](/card/60177)'s <span class="icon-reaction"></span> says damage "is placed" on him, which matches how [<span class="fg-survivor">Bandages</span>](/card/12073) works, so you can discover a clue before healing.
- [<span class="fg-survivor">Aleksey Saburov</span>](/card/12072): You might need to take 1 damage and 1 horror on him, and then you can only choose to heal either the damage or the horror at the start of your next turn. By using [<span class="fg-survivor">Bandages</span>](/card/12073) to heal the damage, you can choose to heal the horror and completely heal him up.
- [<span class="fg-mystic">Jim Culver</span>](/card/12060) / [<span class="fg-mystic">Jim Culver (4)</span>](/card/60479): Indirect, because the damage doesn't need to be placed on the Ally card. However, you can still heal the damage placed on the investigator card.

With only the Core Set 2026 box, **there is no other investigator damage healing**, compared with horror healing from [<span class="fg-survivor">Meat Cleaver</span>](/card/12077) / [<span class="fg-survivor">Meat Cleaver (3)</span>](/card/12085) / [<span class="fg-mystic">Fearless (2)</span>](/card/12069) or moving it with [<span class="fg-mystic">Lucky Charm</span>](/card/12061). ([<span class="fg-survivor">Isabelle Barnes</span>](/card/12013) might want to move damage to [<span class="fg-survivor">Aleksey Saburov</span>](/card/12072) for his built-in heal, but [<span class="fg-mystic">Dexter Drake</span>](/card/12010) will be reluctant to move damage.) You could bulk up with allies, [<span class="fg-guardian">Resilience</span>](/card/12021), or [<span class="fg-survivor">Jumpsuit</span>](/card/12075), but those are still limited to [<span class="fg-guardian">Daniela Reyes</span>](/card/12001) and [<span class="fg-survivor">Isabelle Barnes</span>](/card/12013). They don't extend to [<span class="fg-seeker">Joe Diamond</span>](/card/12004), [<span class="fg-rogue">Trish Scarborough</span>](/card/12007), and [<span class="fg-mystic">Dexter Drake</span>](/card/12010). So the teamplay aspect can be really important for keeping the whole team alive.

The five Investigator Decks bring many damage healing options in the [<span class="fg-mystic">Marie Lambeau</span>](/card/60451) box and the [<span class="fg-survivor">Miguel de la Cruz</span>](/card/60551) box, along with new bulk like [<span class="fg-mystic">Bloodstone</span>](/card/60457), and maybe you can work with soaks rather than heals.

## [<span class="fg-guardian">Daniela Reyes</span>](/card/12001)

She is the one who can pull off the combo of the various tanking Guardian allies + [<span class="fg-survivor">Bandages</span>](/card/12073) on her own. Still, it might be smart to save her setup cost and offload [<span class="fg-survivor">Bandages</span>](/card/12073) to either [<span class="fg-survivor">Isabelle Barnes</span>](/card/12013) or [<span class="fg-survivor">Miguel de la Cruz</span>](/card/60551) if you also have them on your team. The Guardian allies are quite expensive. Also, [<span class="fg-survivor">Field Dressing</span>](/card/60571) can heal Allies for the same cost and is also reactive, but not as automatic.

If you play around her <span class="icon-reaction"></span> Fight, you might be healing yourself often anyway. Don't be fooled by her 9 health. Don't forget to deduct 3 for [<span class="fg-neutral">In Harm's Way</span>](/card/12003). Doesn't that number exactly match the uses on [<span class="fg-survivor">Bandages</span>](/card/12073)?

## [<span class="fg-survivor">Isabelle Barnes</span>](/card/12013)

Her 5 health and her signature weakness [<span class="fg-neutral">Breaking Point</span>](/card/12015) point to [<span class="fg-survivor">Bandages</span>](/card/12073). With the [<span class="fg-mystic">Marie Lambeau</span>](/card/60451) box, she has more options for health problems, like the loopable [<span class="fg-mystic">Cosmic Guidance</span>](/card/60468) or a proactive one like [<span class="fg-mystic">Consume Life</span>](/card/60462).

[<span class="fg-survivor">Bandages</span>](/card/12073) can heal every instance of damage from Isabelle's [<span class="fg-neutral">Breaking Point</span>](/card/12015), but only after all of it has been taken.

From the rule about ["then"](https://arkham.build/rules#grimoire-entry-1.143), this is the relevant part:

> The post-then aspect of an effect has timing priority over all other indirect consequences of the resolution of the pre-then aspect. (For example, if an effect reads: "Draw an encounter card. Then, take 1 horror," and a player controls an ability that reads "After you draw an encounter card," the post-then "take 1 horror" aspect occurs before the "After you draw an encounter card" ability may initiate.)

There are two "then"s on that card. The post-then part after "Take 1 direct damage" also covers the next "then" in the chain. Therefore, while you can trigger reactions after each instance of direct damage as expected, the heals only come through all together at the end.

It's a candidate for the doom placement of [<span class="fg-mystic">Will of the Cosmos</span>](/card/12066), as it discards itself when it runs out of uses, though you need to foresee the damage.
