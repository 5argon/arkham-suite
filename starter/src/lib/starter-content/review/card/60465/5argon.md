The feature of [<span class="fg-mystic">Mirror Form</span>](/card/60465) suggests putting down a valuable, expensive, spammable asset like [<span class="fg-mystic">Cosmic Flame (5)</span>](/card/12071), [<span class="fg-mystic">Second Sight (5)</span>](/card/60481), or [<span class="fg-mystic">Dread Curse of Azathoth</span>](/card/60474) and using it as much as you can before you have to return the asset to your deck. Another feature is that the Fast timing lets you still use it if you can't manage to set up before an enemy comes, though you will lose it to your deck afterwards.

[<span class="fg-mystic">Arcane Initiate</span>](/card/60455) would be good for getting the shuffled ***Spell*** back as soon as possible.

Not sure if something interesting could be done with ***Charm*** cards or not. Most would exhaust after one use ([<span class="fg-mystic">Lucky Charm</span>](/card/12061), [<span class="fg-rogue">Lucky Cigarette Case</span>](/card/12044), [<span class="fg-survivor">Rabbit's Foot</span>](/card/60560), [<span class="fg-rogue">Extravagant Ring</span>](/card/60360), [<span class="fg-mystic">Sacrificial Doll</span>](/card/60458)), and it had better be really impactful to be worth the 1 play cost. Playing [<span class="fg-mystic">Bloodstone</span>](/card/60457) for a turn-long +1 <span class="icon-willpower"></span> in case you can't set it up fast enough might be decent, and you can also take 1 damage with it without discarding it. Though most of the time, you'd want to wait and set those assets up for real.

This card is the only card in evergreen that uses "put into play" so how about reviewing this exact rule?

# [<span class="fg-mystic">Dexter Drake</span>](/card/12010)

"Put into play" is not a play, so you cannot trigger his <span class="icon-reaction"></span> in response. Too bad! [<span class="fg-rogue">Sticky Fingers (2)</span>](/card/12054) remains the only option to somehow use Fast timing to trigger his <span class="icon-reaction"></span>.

{{rule 1.118 "A card that has been put into play"}}

It still has good synergy for his <span class="icon-reaction"></span> by using the Mirror Form-ed assets as a bounce back target, so you avoid shuffling back to the deck at the end of round.
