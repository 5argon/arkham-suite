A card in its dreaming state is not considered to be in play. And so it'd fail the "if it is still in play" part of [<span class="fg-mystic">Mirror Form</span>](/card/60465). Therefore, if something like [<span class="fg-mystic">Reality Fracture</span>](/card/14055) or [<span class="fg-mystic">Lunar Sight</span>](/card/14054) was cheated into play with [<span class="fg-mystic">Mirror Form</span>](/card/60465) skipping the Dreaming keyword, then flipped back to dreaming due to its own effect or another card's effect, you don't have to return it when [<span class="fg-mystic">Mirror Form</span>](/card/60465) asks for it. (And it comes back with refilled charges too!)
