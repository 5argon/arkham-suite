This is not a simple economy card. If you compare it with [<span class="fg-neutral">Emergency Cache</span>](/card/12089), you will find that you need a 3~4 HP enemy, which is surprisingly uncommon within its search range. Then after you get your resources, how will you deal with it? It shouldn't be thought of as [<span class="fg-neutral">Emergency Cache</span>](/card/12089) with **Parley** so you could ignore AoO or something.

I think you play this card because you want an enemy, and you want huge benefits from that one enemy.

An enemy with high enough HP, without Hunter, without bad things like placing doom, and within the searched cards is a lot to ask for. You had better assume by default that you will have to get rid of that enemy unless you are really lucky. After obtaining all the benefits you want from spawning it, that is.

I'm not talking about [<span class="fg-rogue">Trish Scarborough</span>](/card/12007) freely evading the enemy she just summoned to get [<span class="fg-rogue">Sticky Fingers</span>](/card/12048) + [<span class="fg-rogue">Lucky Cigarette Case</span>](/card/12044) triggers on her own. That still falls kind of behind compared with other options. You have to multiply the benefit across your team.

In a hypothetical situation where you are not getting any enemies despite having all the ingredients for every step, playing [<span class="fg-rogue">Paint the Town Red</span>](/card/12051) would be excellent.

Since it is a team card, you will rarely see it in online decklists, because those are built in a vacuum, ready to slot in, and not for a specific team. I do think this card is **great** if you build your Rogue together with your friends, after looking at their decklists and seeing some of the example cards above. The table talk will be fun, and when you group up to execute your plan, it'll be a good time.

And by the way, "draw it" means that you still have to follow its Spawn instruction. It might not land where you are standing, ruining your plan!

The resources matching the enemy's HP are actually a small bonus refund to help you deal with the enemy, not the real benefit of this card. It might also be waiting for **Parley** synergy in the future.
