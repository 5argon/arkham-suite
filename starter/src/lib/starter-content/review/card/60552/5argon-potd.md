# When is the play for Dreaming X events?

I've got a ruling answer from FFG that "After you play X" abilities trigger when you play the dreaming card facedown, but an "after you play this card" ability that references the card itself triggers when it flips faceup.

> **Nick (FFG):** "After you play X" abilities proc when you play the Dreaming card facedown.
>
> If a Dreaming card has a self-referential "after you play this card," it procs when it flips faceup. (I don't think any dreaming cards in the box say "after you play this card," but you can give Dreaming to other cards, so we had to account for it)

In the case of [<span class="fg-survivor">Miguel's Knapsack</span>](/card/60552)'s <span class="icon-reaction"></span> for Dreaming X events (and also [<span class="fg-mystic">Dexter Drake</span>](/card/12010)'s <span class="icon-reaction"></span> for Dreaming X assets), you trigger the <span class="icon-reaction"></span> at the moment you play the card from your hand into the dreaming state (not when it flips faceup later). So it does work, but nothing interesting happens. You'd better pick the draw 1 card option.

> How does the knapsack interact with dream events? We assume you tap it on dreaming the event, but then, when is the adjacent location chosen

> **Nick (FFG):** The knapsack causes the dreaming card to be played as if you were at another location, so you place it facedown as if you were somewhere else. The knapsack's ability does not also proc, nor does it extend, to when the dreaming event flips faceup.
>
> So knapsack "works" with dreaming events, but likely not in the way you would like them to.
