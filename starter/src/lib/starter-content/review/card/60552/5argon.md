"As if". This phrase has brought countless games to a halt! You must throw away your logic when evaluating the result of doing it. Well, if you are not sure about a ruling, at least you can take the draw! Now, if you are ready to play with an "as if", this is the rule:

{{rule 1.012}}

Look closely at the 2nd and 3rd bullet points. They list 4 things that could be affected by the "as if":

1. Play restrictions
2. Costs
3. Triggering conditions
4. Targeting

The 1st bullet point is straightforward: you gotta start resolving it first before you get the "as if". The 4th bullet point means that the 4 things mentioned are limited to the card with the "as if". The 5th bullet point makes sure you are not temporarily grabbing ready enemies into your threat area while you are "as if" somewhere else.

An obviously valid play would be laying down attachments at nearby locations without going there ([<span class="fg-survivor">Lie in Wait</span>](/card/60566), [<span class="fg-survivor">Glassing</span>](/card/60563), [<span class="fg-survivor">Decoy Trap</span>](/card/60562), [<span class="fg-survivor">Makeshift Bomb</span>](/card/60581), [<span class="fg-survivor">Rope Trap</span>](/card/60578), [<span class="fg-survivor">Hidden Shelter</span>](/card/60565)). Why do they work? Because it's targeting, and the "as if" can modify that. Now let's head to other examples:

- Friends at which location can commit to my test? Your actual location, not the "as if" location. See, those 4 things I mentioned don't have any power to move you.
- [<span class="fg-survivor">"Shove off!"</span>](/card/12079): Can this card drop an enemy at a nearby location with the Knapsack? No. You can only change the targeting, but since Evade is not as permissive as Fight, playing it at a nearby location wouldn't give you any new targets other than the enemies you are already engaged with. If your current location says it costs more to play an event, though, you would now be able to avoid that.
- [<span class="fg-survivor">"Look what I found!"</span>](/card/12078): You can suddenly discover 2 clues at a nearby location after failing an investigation at your current location. This is the "as if" changing targeting.
- [<span class="fg-survivor">Field Dressing</span>](/card/60571): You can play it when other investigators defeat a ***Creature*** or ***Monster*** at a nearby location. This is the "as if" working with play restrictions. You also distribute the 3 damage of healing at that "as if" location, including yourself. This is the "as if" changing targeting.
- [<span class="fg-survivor">Respite</span>](/card/60577): Allows you to play it even while you are engaged with an enemy, by choosing a nearby location with no enemies. Though you'll take an AoO, so that's probably not worth doing. If you can't play it because an Aloof enemy is watching you, then it might be a good idea.

[<span class="fg-survivor">Guerrilla Tactics</span>](/card/60564) / [<span class="fg-survivor">Guerrilla Tactics (2)</span>](/card/60576): Ugh... brace yourself. First, let's see the regular things these do:

- **Fight** an enemy you are engaged with right now.
- **Evade** an enemy you are engaged with right now.
- Whether or not you are currently engaged with something, you target an enemy at a connecting location to **Fight** instead. Note that "as if you were engaged with that enemy" is irrelevant if that enemy doesn't have Aloof. If it has Aloof, then this "as if" is doing the work. That's it for the targeting change, though. If this enemy is currently engaged with your friend and you fail the test, your friend still gets hurt as normal.
- Whether or not you are currently engaged with something, you use the "as if" to **Evade** an enemy at a connecting location. Note that "as if you were engaged with that enemy" is always relevant, as you can't evade an enemy that's not engaged with you.
- You cannot use it to **Fight** or **Evade** an Aloof enemy at your current location that you are not engaged with, since the engagement granted by the "as if" requires targeting something at a connecting location.

So the easiest way to think about it is to apply the "as if" of [<span class="fg-survivor">Miguel's Knapsack</span>](/card/60552) first, before you do the above. If you understand this correctly, you should now see that when the Knapsack is used, you can Fight or Evade an Aloof enemy 2 locations away, but you are no longer able to Fight or Evade an Aloof enemy 1 location away (which you could if you didn't use the Knapsack).
