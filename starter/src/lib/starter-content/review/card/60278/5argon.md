- Spreads the effect to everyone at your location, including yourself.

Even at the start of the scenario, if you mulligan into this, I think everyone will be really happy (in exchange for 2 resources that could have gone to your own setup), even though there's no horror to heal yet. # One at a time

Of course, since it is a reveal, everyone knows the one card that entered each investigator's hand. But I can't find any rule that says whether "one at a time" means in player order, in any order, or as the lead investigator says. It's not a simultaneous resolution, so it doesn't require player order.

{{rule 1.082}}

{{rule 1.117 "these choices are made in player order."}}

When I play this card, I simply go by the lead investigator's call, and my lead investigator will often say player order.

{{rule 1.090 "lead investigator is the final arbiter"}}

This matters because, for example, if you do it all at the same time and everyone reveals their 3 cards together, they can discuss a lot of material before deciding their individual draws to optimize the strategy. The correct way is that each person revealing later has more and more information from the investigators before them. Deciding the reveal order might be quite a strategic element.
