This guy is busted! It is widely speculated that no other Lv. 0 Rogue Ally will be able to compete with him. And even if one has an XP cost, you might not want to move on from [<span class="fg-rogue">Olivier Bishop</span>](/card/12046)'s superpower: a <span class="icon-free"></span> move whose only condition is that it's your turn!

If you look at other movement cards, [<span class="fg-survivor">Loner</span>](/card/60557) cares about an empty location. [<span class="fg-seeker">Through the Cracks</span>](/card/12037) / [<span class="fg-seeker">Through the Cracks (2)</span>](/card/12041) / [<span class="fg-rogue">Out of Sight</span>](/card/12053) / [<span class="fg-rogue">Out of Sight (3)</span>](/card/12057) care about the location already being revealed, so you can only escape backwards. [<span class="fg-rogue">The Grapevine</span>](/card/60359) / [<span class="fg-rogue">The Grapevine (2)</span>](/card/60374) also requires an enemy to be at a revealed location.

[<span class="fg-rogue">Clean Sweep</span>](/card/60364) / [<span class="fg-rogue">Clean Sweep (2)</span>](/card/60376) / [<span class="fg-mystic">Shadowmeld</span>](/card/60461) / [<span class="fg-mystic">Shadowmeld (5)</span>](/card/60482), [<span class="fg-survivor">Hunting Dog</span>](/card/60556) and [<span class="fg-seeker">Local Map</span>](/card/12033) can go forward, but nothing is as on-demand <span class="icon-free"></span> as [<span class="fg-rogue">Olivier Bishop</span>](/card/12046).

One free movement per turn lets a Rogue do their work all the time without moving themselves. Or they can succeed at their evasion on the 3rd action and still move once to get away. With a ***Massive*** enemy, they can just move away like nothing happened.

Even without that static +1 <span class="icon-agility"></span> boost, he'd still be really good! So good that you can assume the Rogue's class identity will be having no problem warping around the field at will.

# Dragging enemies

An easy-to-see benefit is that you can ignore AoO even if you have an enemy with you. So you can safely relocate enemies that are in your Threat Area.

{{rule 1.017 "An investigator provokes an attack of opportunity if they spend 1 or more actions"}}

{{rule 1.144 "The cards in an investigator's threat area are at the same location as the investigator"}}

Basic plays include serving your fighter an enemy, receiving commits, or playing cards that care about an enemy's presence, like [<span class="fg-guardian">Scene of the Crime</span>](/card/12024) or [<span class="fg-seeker">Gather Intel</span>](/card/12036).

# Moving within a test in your turn

There are two <span class="icon-free"></span> player windows available within the test, before and after the commit step (ST.2). If this test is in the Investigation Phase and you are currently taking your turn, you can move here too!

This opens up a can of worms... a can of treats. I'll provide some evergreen combos you can practice this skill ceiling with. Keep a lookout for any future player cards that make use of these windows!

## The test continues after movement

You only need to satisfy the restriction at the time you initiate the test. Be it a card to **Fight** an enemy at your location, **Evade** an engaged enemy, **Investigate** your location, or the basic actions of those types that initiate the test.

{{rule 5.001 "Check play restrictions"}}

By the time you arrive at the <span class="icon-free"></span> player windows before and after ST.2, you are free to move, and you'd still be doing the same test on the same target you initiated it with.

This comes from the previous chapter's FAQ :

> Once you initiate a skill test or ability, you’ll resolve that test or ability as completely as possible, regardless of your location

## Moving before commits

Using the window **before** ST.2, you can get commits from friends who aren't at the location where you started the test.

{{timing before 4.002 4.002}}

{{rule 4.002 "Each other investigator at the same location"}}

For example, you attempt to grab the last 2 remaining clues at your dead-end location. You don't have anything that could get 2 clues in one action. Your friend is waiting nearby with a [<span class="fg-seeker">Deduction</span>](/card/12039) commit in hand. You can move out of this dead end and get the commit, both at once. Passing this test is big action compression for both you and your friend. The clues are discovered at your original location, as if you vacuumed them in from range.

You can also start a **Fight** action against an enemy that's engaged with your friend, then move away to take commits from another friend. When this test resolves, it'll look as if you performed a ranged attack. However, if the enemy has Retaliate and you fail, you'll also get a ranged Retaliate attack from that enemy, on top of hitting your friend, since that is also baked in as a consequence of the test you must resolve in ST.7.

Maybe the location simply has some sort of hazard you want gone, for example, an Aloof enemy that reduces your stats as long as it's there. If the location's own text says something bad happens, read closely to see whether being away lets you avoid it. For example, this one is pretty clear: you do discover a clue there, but you are no longer at that location.

> **Forced -** After the last clue is discovered from this location: Each investigator at this location takes 1 direct horror.

## Moving after commits

{{timing 4.002 after 4.002 4.006}}

If you decide to move after the commit step, you can receive the commits at your original location, then change where the test will be resolved. You can use this on an evasion to drop an enemy off at a different location from the one where you started and received the commits. Or you can change the target location of anything that triggers in later steps, like [<span class="fg-rogue">Right Under Their Noses</span>](/card/60368) / [<span class="fg-rogue">Right Under Their Noses (3)</span>](/card/60382). If a theoretical investigator that could use both [<span class="fg-rogue">Olivier Bishop</span>](/card/12046) and [<span class="fg-survivor">"Look what I found!"</span>](/card/12078) exist, they would be able to control where they'll get 2 clues!
