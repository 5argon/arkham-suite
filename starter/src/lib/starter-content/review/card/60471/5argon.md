You can just commit [<span class="fg-mystic">Eldritch Whispers</span>](/card/60471) and hope for the best, but there are a few ways to manipulate tokens in evergreen :

- [<span class="fg-mystic">Sacrificial Doll</span>](/card/60458) : This is the most direct one. You will land on some sort of symbol (including <span class="icon-elder_sign"></span>).
- [<span class="fg-mystic">Premonition</span>](/card/12064) : Maybe you can use it for its normal purpose, and only commit [<span class="fg-mystic">Eldritch Whispers</span>](/card/60471) if you see a symbol.
- [<span class="fg-mystic">Mask of Silenus</span>](/card/12068) : A more random way to do it, with one more chance to see a symbol. However, if you do see a symbol, you don't have to resolve it, and so you also avoid the 1 horror from the mask's effect.
- ([<span class="fg-rogue">Contingency</span>](/card/60383) : But it is unlikely that any Rogue would spend resources just for you to see a symbol...)

{{rule 1.079 "an \"ignored\" chaos token was still drawn"}}

The effect is powerful, and going forward it will be a card to look at any time new token manipulation cards come out.

[<span class="fg-mystic">Augur of Elokoss</span>](/card/12070) is a card that wants to see the symbol too, but unfortunately this **Investigate** can't take <span class="icon-willpower"></span> commits.

Unlike many other skill cards, which add a new **consequence** (ST.7) on passing / failing the test (e.g. [<span class="fg-survivor">Slippery</span>](/card/12080), [<span class="fg-seeker">Establish Motive</span>](/card/60268), [<span class="fg-guardian">Vicious Blow</span>](/card/12025), [<span class="fg-survivor">On the Brink</span>](/card/60569), [<span class="fg-survivor">Do-or-Die</span>](/card/60568), and so on), this one's "if" is in the earlier ST.3, where the token is revealed, before the token's effect in ST.4. You get the charges / secrets in ST.3. Whether or not this turns out to matter, I believe it is another thing to excitedly watch out for in the future.

{{timing 4.003}}

To give a concrete example, though one that's hard to come by now: if you just used your final charge of [<span class="fg-mystic">Mask of Silenus</span>](/card/12068) to reveal a symbol, you can add 2 charges back to the mask in ST.3. Say that symbol token's effect reveals another token in ST.4. Now you have charges to use the mask again.
