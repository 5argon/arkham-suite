The time your ***Ally*** gets defeated is likely the Enemy Phase or the Mythos Phase. With the equivalent of Dreaming 1, it is going to be back on your next turn, in the upcoming Investigation Phase.

We have the in-class [<span class="fg-guardian">Detective Sherman</span>](/card/60177), who matches everything it offers. The +1 / +1 would let you discover clues with his <span class="icon-reaction"></span> more times. And then he revives himself for more! 

A Guardian main (access up to Lv. 3) with secondary access can look at allies in their secondary class. Actually, since it allows attaching to any investigator's ***Ally*** card at your location, there are a lot of teamplay combo possibilities to be realized. For every ally released in the environment with POTD, ask yourself: what if it could revive and enter play again?

It fixes the "VIP problem" where one would never want the ally that gives them good stuff to be defeated, even when taking an attack that would fit nicely within its remaining soak, unless the investigator is down to critical health / sanity. Say you are taking a heavy attack that fits perfectly on [<span class="fg-rogue">Olivier Bishop</span>](/card/12046)'s soak. You might see 2/1 on him but actually his effective soak is 1/0 because he's a super VIP and you never wanted him to die. With [<span class="fg-guardian">Oathsworn</span>](/card/14011), the soak got upgraded to 3/2. And this 3/2 is fully usable unlike before because he revives himself for 2/1 again (which you then think of it as 1/0 like before). Now compare the before-after: from 1/0 to 4/2 "willing" soaks. Adding this much bulk to anyone's ally for 0 play cost sounds amazing to me.

Even without any extra entry effect, I think it's a lot of soak you can give to anyone. An [<span class="fg-guardian">Oathsworn</span>](/card/14011)-ed [<span class="fg-mystic">Jim Culver (4)</span>](/card/60479) or [<span class="fg-neutral">Aziza Steer</span>](/card/73059) starts to look like another investigator already, as they would be 4/4 soak that could come back as 3/3. (In Jim's case, he even gives you tons of goodies while soaking for you!)

For some reason, [<span class="fg-guardian">Protective Vest</span>](/card/60159) / [<span class="fg-guardian">Protective Vest (4)</span>](/card/60181) can search for this.
