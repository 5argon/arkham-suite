The Marie box makes Mystic lean mainly on damage healing. So this [<span class="fg-mystic">Enchanting Fife</span>](/card/14043) might unexpectedly be a flex option for the class. They only have [<span class="fg-mystic">Fearless (2)</span>](/card/12069) for horror healing, and it isn't as on-demand.

Due to the Carolyn box, much of the horror healing is in Seeker (and not every card can target others): [<span class="fg-seeker">Psychology Student</span>](/card/60258), [<span class="fg-seeker">Occult Records</span>](/card/60256), [<span class="fg-seeker">Psychoanalysis</span>](/card/60264), [<span class="fg-seeker">Psychoanalysis (3)</span>](/card/60278), [<span class="fg-seeker">De-Escalate</span>](/card/60265), [<span class="fg-seeker">Unflappable</span>](/card/60262), [<span class="fg-seeker">Unflappable (1)</span>](/card/60271).

Survivors and Guardians mostly heal their own horror ([<span class="fg-guardian">Stakeout</span>](/card/60166), [<span class="fg-guardian">Stakeout (3)</span>](/card/60179), [<span class="fg-survivor">Meat Cleaver</span>](/card/12077), [<span class="fg-survivor">Meat Cleaver (3)</span>](/card/12085), [<span class="fg-survivor">Canteen</span>](/card/60573), [<span class="fg-survivor">Dreams of Peace</span>](/card/14061)), with [<span class="fg-survivor">Hidden Shelter</span>](/card/60565) as a rare piece of team horror healing.

Most of those heal an investigator or an ***Ally*** asset. The Fife heals "a card controlled by an investigator at your location", so it can heal any card with horror on it. We have things like [<span class="fg-mystic">Lucky Charm</span>](/card/12061) and [<span class="fg-mystic">Cloak of Resonance</span>](/card/12058) that could take horror and be healed with the Fife.

Giving a card draw might be very good for <span class="icon-guardian"></span> Guardian, which lacks card draw at the moment.

Delaying an ongoing dream might be some sort of tech rather than a downside for the cost. We will see as more cards are revealed.
