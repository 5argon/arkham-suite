**Display in Profile** controls whether this archive's **existence** is shown on your profile — some
widgets (the calendar and the "campaigns played" list) name individual campaigns and when you played
them. It also decides whether this campaign appears in a compiled profile you deploy or export.

This affects the campaign's *existence* only. Your **aggregate statistics** (cards played,
investigators, clears, achievements, …) always count this campaign regardless of the setting.

- **Default** — follows your account-level **Default Archive Display in Profile** setting (in
  *Customize Profile*). Changing that setting instantly affects every archive left on *Default*.
- **Visible** — its existence is always shown, regardless of your default.
- **Hidden** — its existence is never shown, regardless of your default.
