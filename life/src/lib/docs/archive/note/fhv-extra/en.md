In this campaign you choose which scenarios to fit into three short days, and which day and phase to
play them on. None of that timing survives in the campaign log, so widgets can't reconstruct your
schedule from logs alone.

Setting the “played on” day/phase for each scenario below fills the Day/Night Schedule matrix and
enriches the per-character views. The two locked night scenarios — The Twisted Hollow (Day 1 Night)
and The Longest Night (Day 2 Night) — are read from the log automatically, so you only need to place
the flexible ones.
