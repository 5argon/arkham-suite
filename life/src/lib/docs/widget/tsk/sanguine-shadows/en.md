Sanguine Shadows' **Succeed / Failed** is read from the scenario's **Resolution** you record in the
Extra tab — record it and the run counts; leave it blank and the run is simply left out (never
guessed).

## How each resolution counts

- **Win:** R1 (collect 3 targets, keep The Weeping Lady) and R2 (ally with La Chica Roja and beat
  the Sanguine Watcher).
- **Loss:** R3 (La Chica Roja keeps The Weeping Lady) and no resolution.
