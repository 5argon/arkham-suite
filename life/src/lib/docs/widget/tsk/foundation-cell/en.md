At the campaign's end the cell is judged on two tallies. Each recorded note on the **Foundation
Trust** list adds 1 to Trust; each on the **Cell Deception** list adds 1 to Deception. Together they
decide the epilogue — Trust at least equal to Deception earns a **permanent position**, otherwise the
cell is **dismantled**.

This is a different axis from **Tablet vs Elder Thing** (the chaos bag): a single choice can push one
up and the other down — e.g. aiding the Claret Knight is a chaos-bag *Trust* act but a *Cell
Deception* tally entry.

## Counted automatically — from the log

The tallies are derived from the individual notes (counting each present entry), not from a hand-written
final number.

- **Per-entry breakdown (below)** — counts **every game**, even a losing or abandoned run, since the
  notes are recorded during the campaign.
- **The high-score numbers** — only count runs that actually **reached the epilogue** (recorded a
  permanent position or dismantled), so they line up with those two totals: a tally is only meaningful
  once the cell was judged.
  - **Highest Foundation Trust / Cell Deception** — the most entries one side reached in a judged run.
  - **Most Trust over Deception / over Trust** — the biggest lead a side took (a long dash if a side
    never came out ahead).
- **Permanent position / Dismantled** — the actual epilogue outcome.

## What counts on each side

- **Foundation Trust:** the cell is assisting Agent Sirry · the cell told the truth to Taylor · Agent
  Quinn has your back · the cell is delivering intel *(crossed off)*.
- **Cell Deception:** the cell hid the truth from Taylor · the cell is off-mission · the cell aided the
  knight · Ece trusts the cell · the cell made a deal with Thorne · Aliki is on your side · Desi is in
  your debt · Tuwile Masai is on your side.
