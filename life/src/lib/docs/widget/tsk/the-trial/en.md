At the Congress of the Keys, every Red Coterie member casts a vote on the cell. This widget shows,
across your campaigns, how often you moved each member **off their default vote** — and what caused
the change. It's read entirely from the campaign log.

## How to read a row

Each row is one member and one possible vote change, e.g. *Thorne: Abstain → Nay*. The **default**
row is the vote a member casts when nothing swayed them; the other rows are the ways the campaign can
change it, with the **When** column naming the campaign-log entry that triggers it. The **Times**
column counts how many of your recorded campaigns ended on that vote.

Every play resolves to exactly **one** vote per member (the first trigger that matches, otherwise the
default), so a member's rows sum to your number of finished campaigns.

## Only finished campaigns count

The vote only happens at the Congress, so a campaign counts here only once it **reaches the finale**
(a judgment was recorded). Campaigns abandoned earlier are left out.

## A few special cases

- **Desi** needs one fact the log doesn't keep — whether the copy you kept was the real Desi or an
  impostor. You stored that copy face-down during Dancing Mad and flip it at the Trial, so record it
  in the **Extra** tab on **Congress of the Keys** and his Nay vs eerily-silent vote resolves
  correctly; without it that play is left out of his count.
- **Razin Farhi** has no default vote — he only votes if the lovers are reunited — so he has no
  default row.
- **The Red-Gloved Man** is always eerily silent.
