On Thin Ice's **Succeed / Failed** is read from the scenario's **Resolution** you record in the
Extra tab — record it and the run counts; leave it blank and the run is simply left out (never
guessed).

## How each resolution counts

- **Win:** R1 (made a deal with Thorne), R2 (defeated Thorne, kept The Sable Glass), and R3 (Thorne
  disappeared) — all reach act 4, so the scenario was completed.
- **Loss:** R4 ("Annihilation"), and no resolution (the Void Chimera escaped).
