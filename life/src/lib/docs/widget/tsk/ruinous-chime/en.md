The Ruinous Chime is Dr. Dewi Irawan's Scarlet Key — won on a quest that's a race against time. It
isn't a combat scenario; you win it when she **joins the cell**, which records *"Dr. Irawan joined
the cell."* Every number here is read straight from the campaign log.

## The quest, beat by beat

- **Met Dr. Irawan** — you found her in Perth (Paranatural Selection); the quest began.
- **Chased to New Guinea** — she left Perth for New Guinea (Manokwari) — the chase is on.
- **Obtained** — you reached Manokwari (Metamorphosis) **in time**, so she joined and gave you The
  Ruinous Chime (hovers to a per-difficulty breakdown).
- **Vanished** — you arrived too late: she vanished from existence and the Key is lost.

Because each beat records its own note, the widget can show the whole funnel — how often you met her,
chased her, and whether you reached her in time or lost her.
