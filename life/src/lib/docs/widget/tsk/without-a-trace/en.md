Without a Trace is a secret scenario played **before** the Congress of the Keys (not a replacement
finale), so a run usually goes on to the Congress afterwards.

## How the notes are counted

- **Knows the true nature / Aliki on your side / Haven't seen the last of Aliki** — these are
  *recorded* only by Without a Trace; the Congress merely *checks* them. So they're counted straight
  from the log, whether or not the run went on to reach the Congress.
- **The cell was hollowed** — this loss note is recorded at **both** Without a Trace and the Congress.
  It's credited to Without a Trace only when **no Congress vote outcome** is present — a hollowing here
  ends the run before the Congress, whereas a Congress hollowing rides alongside a vote outcome.

Each number hovers to its per-difficulty breakdown.

## Path to Without a Trace

The scenario is the payoff of a hidden quest, so its progress is folded in here (all log-derived):
take Aliki's whistle (Whistle on the Wind), go **off-mission** at Dead and Gone — which needs Agent
Quinn's trust (Ringing Hollow, met before 20 time) and the whistle — and Romulus and Remus then opens
the Bermuda Triangle. The setbacks that foreclose the path are shown too: **Quinn vanished** (met too
late) and **refused the whistle**.
