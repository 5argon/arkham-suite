The Scarlet Keys has four optional mini-quest stops. This widget tallies them across your campaigns,
showing **only facts the campaign log can prove** — a recorded note, whether that note was crossed
off, or a count. A quest's block appears only once you have data for it.

## Special Delivery (Lagos ↔ Tokyo)

A courier run for a small chaos-bag reward.

- **Picked up intel** — you took the intel (records *"the cell is delivering Foundation intel."*).
- **Delivered (+1 token)** — you completed the run, which **crosses that note off** and bumps a
  chaos token (per-difficulty hover).

## Strange Architecture (Bombay ↔ Stockholm)

A two-city déjà-vu for XP.

- **Noticed a building** — the first building (records *"the cell appreciated the architecture."*).
- **Déjà vu (+2 XP)** — the second building completes it, **crossing that note off** (per-difficulty
  hover).

## Quid Pro Quo (Foundation offices)

Free stops in San Francisco / Moscow that hand out intel (which also feeds Dead Heat and Dancing Mad).

- **Amaranth intel** — *"the cell knows Amaranth's real name."*
- **Desi intel** — *"the cell knows Desi's past."*

## Ruses and Reclamation (stolen-Key search)

After enough time passes, a Coterie member steals a random Key and you search four cities to get it
back. The theft and the recovery are kept only as hidden "Remember that…" notes, so they **can't** be
read from the log — the one persistent trace is the search itself:

- **Wrong leads chased** — total false leads followed across your campaigns.
- **Most in a run** — the worst single search.
