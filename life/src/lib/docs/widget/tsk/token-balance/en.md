The chaos bag starts every campaign with **1 Tablet** and **1 Elder Thing**. An **act of trust** removes
an Elder Thing and adds a Tablet; an **act of secrecy** does the reverse (each capped at 4 — an add to a
full bag becomes bonus XP instead). Either token can later be removed by an act of the opposite kind.

This is a different axis from **Foundation Trust & Cell Deception** (the epilogue tally): a choice can
swing the bag one way and the tally the other (e.g. aiding the Claret Knight is an act of trust here but a
*Cell Deception* tally entry).

## How each swap is detected

Most acts write (or cross off) a Campaign Log note, so we read them straight from the log. Three leave
no log trace and are read from the **Extra** tab instead.

### Trust (−Elder Thing, +Tablet)

- **Riddles and Rain — took Flint’s offer** — Extra tab (no log trace).
- **The Foundation — told Taylor the truth** — recorded *“the cell told the truth to Taylor.”*
- **Sanguine Shadows — told Bolívar everything** — Extra tab (recorded only as a hidden note, never in the Campaign Log).
- **Dealings in the Dark — worked with Ece** — recorded *“the cell is working with Ece.”*
- **Dead and Gone — told Quinn the truth** — *“Agent Quinn does not trust the cell”* is **crossed off** (the log remembers cross-offs).
- **On Thin Ice — made a deal with Thorne** — recorded *“the cell made a deal with Thorne”* (or the Raw Deal Extra).
- **Dogs of War — aided the Claret Knight** — recorded *“the cell aided the knight.”*
- **Whistle on the Wind — took Aliki’s whistle** — recorded *“the cell possesses a mysterious whistle.”*

### Deception (−Tablet, +Elder Thing)

- **Riddles and Rain — went on your own terms** — Extra tab (no log trace).
- **The Foundation — hid the truth from Taylor** — recorded *“the cell hid the truth from Taylor.”*
- **Sanguine Shadows — told Bolívar nothing** — Extra tab (hidden note only).
- **Dealings in the Dark — deceived Ece** — recorded *“the cell is deceiving Ece.”*
- **Dead and Gone — kept the Foundation in the dark** — recorded *“the Foundation remains in the dark.”*
- **On Thin Ice — refused Thorne** — Extra tab. There is no note for refusing, and the final Sable Glass bearer can’t stand in for it (Thorne can steal it back, some endings leave you without it, and R4 even erases the deal note).
- **Dogs of War — assisted Agent Sirry** — recorded *“the cell is assisting Agent Sirry.”*
- **Whistle on the Wind — refused Aliki** — recorded *“the cell refused Aliki’s offer.”*

> Fill the three Extra-tab choices (Riddles and Rain, Sanguine Shadows’ Bolívar, On Thin Ice) to make
> every swap in a run countable.

## Bonus XP from a full bag

Adding a token to a bag that already holds four of that side grants **+1 XP** instead. By replaying a
run's swaps in route order (bag starts at 1 Tablet / 1 Elder Thing), we know exactly when a side hit
four and overflowed — so we can total the bonus XP each side earned across all runs, and the most a
single run squeezed out. This only appears once you've actually overflowed at least once.
