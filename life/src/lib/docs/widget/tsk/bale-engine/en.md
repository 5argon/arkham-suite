The Bale Engine is Tuwile Masai's Scarlet Key. It isn't won at a combat scenario — you earn it when
Tuwile **joins the cell**, which records *"Tuwile Masai is on your side."* So every number here is
read straight from the campaign log.

## The two ways to win it

- **Infernal Machinery (Nairobi)** — if you've gathered enough Coterie allies (3 or more of: Chica
  on your side, aided the Knight, Aliki on your side, Desi in your debt, a deal with Thorne, Ece
  trusts you), Tuwile trusts you and joins on the spot.
- **The Great Work (Bermuda)** — if he *didn't* trust you in Nairobi he flees to Bermuda, recording
  *"Tuwile Masai fled to Bermuda."* There you get a second chance: with few enough estrangements
  (fewer than 2 of the "haven't seen the last of…" notes / Ece distrusts you) he joins after all.

## The numbers

- **Obtained** — campaigns where you ended up with The Bale Engine (hovers to a per-difficulty
  breakdown). It's the sum of the next two.
- **Joined at Nairobi** — he trusted you outright and joined at Infernal Machinery (he never fled).
- **Won back at Bermuda** — he fled to Bermuda first, but you won him back at The Great Work.
- **Fled to Bermuda** — every campaign he fled (whether or not you later won him back).
- **Lost for good** — he fled and was never won back, so the Key stayed out of the cell's hands.

The two win paths record the same "on your side" note, and only the Nairobi-distrust branch records
"fled to Bermuda" — so the presence of the *fled* note is what tells a direct Nairobi win apart from
a Bermuda comeback.
