Dead Heat's **Succeed / Failed** is read from the scenario's **Resolution** you record in the Extra
tab — record it and the run counts; leave it blank and the run is simply left out (never guessed).

## How each resolution counts

- **Win:** R3 (defeat Amaranth knowing her real name) and R4 (defeat her, keep The Last Blossom).
- **Loss:** R1 (the lovers reunite), R2 (Amaranth escapes), and no resolution.
- **Neither:** R5 — arrived too late, the scenario was skipped, so it counts as neither.
