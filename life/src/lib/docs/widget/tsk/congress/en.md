The Congress of the Keys is the finale. This widget has two parts beyond the usual scenario passport.

## Meeting Routes

The Coterie's trial resolves into one of **six judgments** (the "meeting routes" — deemed a liability /
an asset, overthrow, join, spared, destroyed from within), which collapse onto the finale's **three
versions**. There are more routes than versions, so hitting the unvisited ones is a planning goal.
Each route is lit when you reached it in a recorded play (with a per-difficulty hover); the version
column shows which finale it leads to.

## The final battle

Every Red Coterie member votes on the cell at the Congress, and the vote decides which side of the
final battle they take:

- **Fought alongside you** — members who voted **Nay** join as your **Conspirators**. (Tuwile Masai
  can vote Nay but has no Conspirator card, so he never appears here.)
- **Stood against you** — members who voted **Yea** are set aside as **enemies**, along with the
  **Red-Gloved Man** (always an enemy). Only members with an enemy card appear — Tuwile Masai,
  Abarran, and Razin Farhi can vote Yea but have none.

Each roster lights up by how many of your campaigns that member took that side (with a per-difficulty
hover). It's read from the campaign log: the vote is reconstructed from the same records that drive
**The Trial** widget, and only campaigns that reached the Congress count. The one fact the log can't
keep is whether **Desi** was the real Desi or an impostor — you flip the copy stored back in Dancing
Mad here at the Trial, so record it in the **Congress of the Keys Extra** and his vote resolves;
without it, only Desi is uncertain. Every other member is purely log-derived.
