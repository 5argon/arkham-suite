The Black Cat moves between the two campaigns: it ends up at the side of the Dream-Quest party or of
the Web of Dreams party. In interconnected play, record the other campaign's Black Cat notes in the
**Linked Campaign Log** so both halves of its story are counted.

## Where it ended up

Each play counts once, in the first of these that applies:

- **On your own** — a Web of Dreams half campaign, played without the cat.
- **Abandoned you** — *"you asked for it."*
- **Knows the truth** — *"the black cat knows the truth"* in The Dream-Quest.
- **At your side** — with the Dream-Quest party, otherwise with the Web of Dreams party.
- **Fate unresolved** — an interconnected play where none of the above is recorded yet.

**Had a hunch** and **Searched for the truth** are counted on top, whatever the ending.

## The cat's messages

Every message you gave the cat across the interludes, from either campaign's log.
