In an interconnected campaign the epilogue you read depends on **both** finales: the row is The
Dream-Quest's outcome at Where the Gods Dwell, the column is The Web of Dreams' outcome at Weaver of
the Cosmos. Each play lights the cell it reached. The finale widgets on each campaign page show that
side's own ending on its own.

## Verdicts

- **Win the campaign** — the green cells.
- **Averted one form of destruction, but not the other** — the amber cells.
- **No survivors** — Epilogue 1, the only total loss.

## Also counted

- **Awaiting the other campaign's outcome** — the play has only one side's final outcome. Record the
  other campaign's outcome in the **Linked Campaign Log** to place it on the grid.
- **Killed by cats** — the play broke the law of Ulthar. The cats' vengeance is a loss whichever cell
  the play reached.
