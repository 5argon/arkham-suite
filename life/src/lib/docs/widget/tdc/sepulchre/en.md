Sepulchre of the Sleeper is reached only by heading west, and it can be skipped. It is the gate to
the campaign's better ending: with **all 26 glyphs** decrypted, **all 6 artifacts** recovered and
**the inner sanctum unsealed** (The Grand Vault), the investigators can confront the nightmare at
R'lyeh and halt Cthulhu's awakening, instead of waiting for Cthulhu to reach Arkham.

**The Gate** counts the plays that met each requirement; **The Reckoning** counts whether the
awakening was halted or the nightmare left unconfronted.
