**Steps of the Bridge** is a running count through The Web of Dreams. Weaver of the Cosmos places
doom by the band the count ends in — the more Steps, the harder the finale:

- **0–2 Steps:** no doom.
- **3–5 Steps:** 1 doom.
- **6–8 Steps:** 2 doom.
- **9–11 Steps:** 3 doom.
- **12 or more:** 4 doom.

The track counts the plays that ended in each band.
